What players say

A free-to-play team shooter framed as a game show, where you fight over cash objectives in arenas you can blow apart. Destruction, gadgets and open loadouts let creative plays win fights.

Players love

  • free loadout choice across classes

  • creative, weird plays actually work

Watch out for

  • divisive balance patches and class nerfs

  • steep learning curve for new players
  • server lag, bugs and cheaters

Best for: shooter fans tired of standard deathmatch who enjoy chaotic, creative team play

Not for: solo players who want calm, fair matchmaking or stable balance
