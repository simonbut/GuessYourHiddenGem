What players say

A survival crafting game where you start on a tiny raft, hook floating debris, fend off a persistent shark and grow your raft into a floating home while visiting islands and following a story. Mostly relaxed, and best with friends.

Players love

  • great fun with friends
  • chill, low-stress pace
  • satisfying raft building and customization
  • story and islands give clear goals

How it feels

Watch out for

  • repetitive once the early game ends
  • clunky controls and janky platforming sections
  • annoying shark and bird attacks
  • multiplayer connection and audio issues for some

Best for: friends looking for a laid-back survival builder to chat over

Not for: players who want deep challenge or tight action
