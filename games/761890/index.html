What players say

A free-to-play sandbox MMO with no classes: your gear decides your role, nearly every item is player-crafted, and dangerous zones mean full-loot PvP where you can lose everything.

Players love

  • living player-driven economy
  • classless, gear-based builds
  • high-stakes full-loot PvP
  • lots of solo and group activities
  • cross-play with mobile

What stands out

  • Lots of content / long

How it feels

  • cutthroat
  • tense
  • grindy
  • social

Watch out for

  • solo players constantly ganked
  • premium status feels close to required
  • toxic community and distrust of developer
  • connection drops and crashes in some regions

Best for: players who enjoy risk, trading and grinding in a cutthroat PvP sandbox

Not for: solo players who want a relaxed, safe grind
