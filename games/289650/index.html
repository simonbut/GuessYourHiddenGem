What players say

An Assassin's Creed set in a dense, stunning recreation of Revolutionary Paris. Fans call its parkour the best in the series, and it has a co-op mode. It is far more stable than at its notorious launch, though the launcher and the controls still frustrate some players.

Players love

  • best parkour in the series
  • gorgeous, detailed Paris
  • graphics that still hold up
  • deep gear customization

How it feels

  • atmospheric
  • immersive
  • historical

Watch out for

  • mandatory Ubisoft launcher login hassles
  • parkour controls snap the wrong way
  • lingering bugs and performance issues
  • weak story

Best for: players who love free-running through a beautiful historical city

Not for: anyone who can't stand Ubisoft's launcher or wants a strong story
