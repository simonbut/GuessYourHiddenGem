What players say

A survival crafting game where you're shrunk to ant size in a backyard, building bases, gathering bug parts and fighting giant insects solo or in co-op. Exploration and scale are the stars, but the late game gets grindy.

Players love

  • wonderful sense of tiny scale
  • detailed backyard world worth exploring
  • fun base building
  • deep combat with parrying and bug weaknesses
  • great co-op with friends or family

What stands out

  • Great with friends

How it feels

  • adventurous
  • tense
  • rewarding

Watch out for

  • grindy late-game material farming
  • clunky combat for some players
  • uneven, punishing difficulty
  • multiplayer lag and disconnects

Best for: survival-craft fans who love exploring and building with friends

Not for: arachnophobes and players who hate grinding
