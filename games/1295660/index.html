What players say

The latest turn-based 4X Civ game, which splits each match into Ages where your civilization changes as history moves on. Players are split: some enjoy the new systems after patches, while many longtime fans feel it isn't Civ anymore.

Players love

  • fresh take after Civ VI sameness
  • noticeably improved since launch
  • interesting leader and civ combos
  • lovely painterly visuals

What stands out

  • Addictive, one-more-run

How it feels

  • strategic
  • ambitious
  • divisive

Watch out for

  • Age transitions break game flow
  • overpriced DLC left out of editions
  • cluttered, confusing UI
  • doesn't feel like classic Civ

Best for: strategy fans open to big changes in the Civ formula, ideally on sale

Not for: series veterans who want a classic one-civ-through-history game
