What players say

A classic survival city builder where you lead exiled settlers and keep them fed, warm and clothed through harsh winters. Slow and absorbing, it can punish one bad decision, and it thins out once your town is stable.

Best for: city builder fans who enjoy careful resource balancing and a real challenge

Not for: players who want lots of buildings, content or ongoing updates
