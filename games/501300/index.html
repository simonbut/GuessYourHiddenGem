What players say

A short first-person story game where you explore a strange family home and relive how each relative died, with every tale played in its own inventive way. It takes roughly two hours to finish.

Players love

  • inventive, varied ways to tell each story
  • haunting mix of whimsy and tragedy
  • strong voice acting and narration
  • beautiful, lovingly detailed house to explore
  • moving story that lingers afterward

What stands out

  • Emotional, tearjerker moments
  • Short

How it feels

  • melancholic
  • whimsical
  • eerie

Watch out for

  • can cause strong motion sickness
  • very short and light on gameplay
  • ending feels unsatisfying to some
  • hype can outweigh the emotional impact

Best for: players who love short, artful narrative games

Not for: players prone to motion sickness or looking for real gameplay challenge
