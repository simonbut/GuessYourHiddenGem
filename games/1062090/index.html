What players say

A cozy but tricky city builder starring lumberpunk beavers who must survive droughts and toxic badwater. Players end up building huge dams, reservoirs and vertical towns with impressive water physics.

Players love

  • fantastic water physics and dams
  • vertical building and terraforming
  • adorable beavers and charming art
  • dedicated devs and mod support
  • addictive survive-the-drought loop

What stands out

  • Addictive, one-more-run
  • Relaxing & cozy

How it feels

  • cozy
  • clever
  • relaxing
  • addictive

Watch out for

  • poor explanation beyond the tutorial
  • beavers' odd, frustrating AI priorities
  • little challenge or goal once established

Best for: engineering-minded builders who love managing water and supply chains

Not for: players who want a strong goal or story to push them forward
