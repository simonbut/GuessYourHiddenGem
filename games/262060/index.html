What players say

A brutal gothic turn-based dungeon crawler where you manage a roster of flawed heroes who suffer stress, disease and permanent death. Grim hand-drawn art and a famous narrator make every expedition feel desperate.

Players love

  • iconic narrator voice
  • tense risk-and-resource decisions
  • rewarding party building and strategy
  • strong mod community

What stands out

  • grim
  • tense

Watch out for

  • harsh RNG can feel unfair
  • slow combat and repetitive dungeons
  • losing heroes can cost hours of progress
  • steep learning curve

Best for: strategy fans who enjoy punishing difficulty and making the best of bad luck

Not for: players who hate randomness or losing progress
