What players say

A free-to-play seasonal action RPG about blasting through maps and theorycrafting builds from skills, talents and loot. Combat is fast and the systems run deep, but paid pets hang over endgame progression.

Players love

Best for: ARPG theorycrafters who enjoy seasonal grinding and don't mind spending or waiting

Not for: players who hate pay-to-win monetization or want a classic Torchlight
