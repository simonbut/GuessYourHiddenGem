What players say

An open-world crime revenge story set in 1968, where Lincoln Clay takes apart the Italian mob racket by racket after it kills his surrogate family. The story, characters and period music pull you in, but the missions follow the same loop over and over.

Players love

  • strong story and charismatic lead
  • great period soundtrack
  • heavy, enjoyable driving
  • punchy gunplay and brutal takedowns
  • strong 1960s atmosphere

How it feels

  • gritty
  • violent
  • atmospheric
  • grindy

Watch out for

  • very repetitive mission structure
  • feels more like GTA than earlier Mafia games
  • bugs and stutter for some players
  • missing quality-of-life features like fast travel

Best for: players who'll put up with repetitive missions for a strong revenge story and 60s atmosphere

Not for: fans wanting the tight, linear storytelling of Mafia I and II
