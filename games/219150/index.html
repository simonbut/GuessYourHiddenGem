What players say

A brutal top-down action game set in neon 1980s Miami. Every level is a fast, bloody puzzle: you burst into rooms, die quickly, restart instantly and try again until you clear the floor, all to a pounding synthwave soundtrack.

Players love

  • legendary synthwave soundtrack
  • fast, violent combat that plays like a puzzle
  • instant restarts make dying fine
  • masks and approaches give each level variety
  • cryptic story worth piecing together

How it feels

  • violent
  • frantic
  • hypnotic
  • stylish

Watch out for

  • some levels feel unfair or frustrating
  • crashes and compatibility problems on some PCs

Best for: players who love hard, twitchy trial-and-error action with great music

Not for: players who dislike graphic violence or repeating a level many times
