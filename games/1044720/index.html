What players say

A medieval survival city builder where you grow a wilderness camp into a town. Crop rotation, disease, winter stockpiles and raiders make every choice matter as the settlement slowly takes shape.

Players love

  • crop rotation, disease and winter prep to plan around
  • satisfying growth from camp to thriving town
  • adjustable difficulty, including a pacifist mode
  • detailed, good-looking settlements
  • developers keep improving it with updates

What stands out

  • Relaxing & cozy

How it feels

  • methodical
  • relaxing
  • challenging

Watch out for

  • clunky military control and dumb defenders
  • lag and crashes with large populations
  • basic trade at the mercy of trader RNG
  • fiddly UI and building tools, like terrain flattening

Best for: Banished fans who want deeper survival and food systems in a medieval town builder

Not for: players wanting strong warfare, trade or diplomacy
