What players say

An open-world action-adventure in feudal Japan, switching between stealthy shinobi Naoe and heavy-hitting samurai Yasuke. Gorgeous to wander and sneak through, though the story and side content wear thin.

Players love

How it feels

Watch out for

  • stutters, bugs and crashes
  • repetitive, bloated open world
  • stiff cutscenes and animations

Best for: stealth fans who want to soak in a beautiful feudal Japan

Not for: players who need a gripping story or a bug-free experience
