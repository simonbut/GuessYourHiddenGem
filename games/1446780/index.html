What players say

A fast, colorful Monster Hunter where you hunt huge beasts, craft gear from their parts, and zip around the map and mid-fight with Wirebugs. Hunts are quicker and snappier than in World, and you can play solo or in co-op.

Players love

  • Wirebug movement and new combos
  • fast, fluid weapon combat
  • vibrant stylized art style
  • runs smoothly, solid PC port
  • Sunbreak expansion adds a lot of content

How it feels

  • energetic
  • colorful
  • satisfying
  • social

Watch out for

  • long, talky tutorial opening
  • clunky controls and menus
  • easier than World/Iceborne

Best for: players who want fast, mobile monster fights, solo or with friends

Not for: fans who want World's realistic visuals and tough endgame challenge
