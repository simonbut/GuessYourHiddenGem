What players say

A free-to-play sci-fi action MMO set on the planet Halpha. Combat is fast and flashy and the character creator is a big draw, but after the story it settles into fashion, lobbies and boss grinding.

Players love

How it feels

Watch out for

  • little to do after the story but repeat bosses
  • pricey cosmetic gacha
  • 100GB-plus install for little content

Best for: character-creator and fashion lovers who enjoy flashy action and hanging out with friends

Not for: players who need a steady stream of new zones and story
