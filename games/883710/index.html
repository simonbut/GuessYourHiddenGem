What players say

A remake of the 1998 survival horror classic. You explore a zombie-filled Raccoon City police station, solve puzzles and manage scarce ammo and a cramped inventory, while a relentless stalker hunts you. Players describe it as tense and genuinely scary.

Players love

  • tense, scary atmosphere
  • Mr. X stalking you through the station
  • scarce ammo and tight inventory management
  • puzzles and exploring the interconnected police station
  • replayable Leon and Claire campaigns

What stands out

  • Genuinely scary

How it feels

  • tense
  • scary
  • atmospheric

Watch out for

  • Leon and Claire stories don't line up
  • brutal extra modes and harder second runs

Best for: players who love tense, methodical horror with puzzles and tight resources

Not for: players who dislike scarce ammo, hiding and being chased
