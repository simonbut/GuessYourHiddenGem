What players say

The 2015 reboot of the street-racing series, set in a rain-soaked, always-night Ventura Bay with car customization and live-action story cutscenes. Moody and stylish to cruise, though the PC version can fight you on controls.

Players love

How it feels

  • stylish

Watch out for

  • broken controller support on PC
  • always-online requirement
  • grippy handling, frustrating drifts
  • toothless police chases

Best for: players who love night-time street-racing vibes and visual car tuning

Not for: controller players unwilling to mod in fixes, or anyone wanting offline play
