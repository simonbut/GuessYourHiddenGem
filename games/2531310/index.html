What players say

A third-person survival action game following Ellie and Abby through a post-apocalyptic revenge story, mixing stealth, crafting and brutal combat. It feels harrowing and cinematic, and its story sharply divides players.

Players love

  • emotionally gripping, unforgettable story
  • stunning visuals and fine detail
  • brutal, satisfying combat and gunplay
  • story told from two perspectives

How it feels

  • emotional
  • tense
  • harrowing
  • bleak

Watch out for

  • bleak story some find exhausting
  • long stretch as Abby frustrates some
  • divisive politics put some players off
  • some find the plot weak or illogical

Best for: players who want a harrowing, cinematic story with tense stealth combat, ideally after Part I

Not for: anyone wanting an uplifting story or who dislikes splitting time between two protagonists
