What players say

A third-person WWII sniping game set in 1944 France, where Karl Fairburne infiltrates semi-open maps and lines up shots shown in a gruesome X-ray kill cam. Methodical stealth with room to go loud.

Players love

  • gory, satisfying X-ray kill cam
  • freedom to sneak or go loud
  • beautiful, atmospheric French settings
  • weapon workbench customization

How it feels

  • tense
  • methodical
  • gory

Watch out for

  • cramped maps with few long shots
  • Dim AI and easy Authentic mode
  • Pricey DLC, Hitler mission included
  • feels like a reskin of Sniper Elite 4

Best for: newcomers chasing the sniper fantasy, ideally on sale

Not for: series veterans wanting long-range shots and a real challenge
