What players say

A historical Total War entry where you steer empires and tribes through the collapse of Rome, mixing turn-based campaigns with real-time battles. It feels grim and desperate, more about surviving invasions than building an empire.

Players love

  • excellent mods like 1212 AD and Dawnless Days
  • desperate fight to hold Rome together
  • tougher than other Total Wars
  • community fixes smooth out performance
  • dark, grim atmosphere

How it feels

  • grim
  • desperate
  • chaotic

Watch out for

  • stutter and crashes without fixes
  • distrust of publisher's DLC practices
  • harsh public order and food systems

Best for: strategy fans who want a grim survival campaign or a base for big total-conversion mods

Not for: players unwilling to install performance fixes before playing
