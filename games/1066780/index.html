What players say

A transport tycoon sim where you build rail, road, sea and air routes to move passengers and cargo and watch towns grow around them. It feels methodical and absorbing, a slow logistics puzzle you tinker with for hours.

Players love

  • satisfying to watch networks and towns grow
  • deep supply-chain logistics
  • workshop mods add lots more
  • great value on sale
  • easy to sink hundreds of hours into

How it feels

  • methodical
  • satisfying
  • calm

Watch out for

  • fiddly track building, missing QoL tools
  • weak tutorials and unclear route errors
  • lag on large late-game networks

Best for: train and logistics fans who enjoy building networks and watching them run

Not for: players wanting fast-paced tycoon action or slick building tools
