What players say

A 2D pixel-art sandbox where you repair your ship and hop between procedurally generated planets, mining, crafting and fighting. Players describe a chill, exploratory feel, and many stretch it to hundreds of hours with mods.

Players love

  • chill, atmospheric planet-hopping exploration
  • huge Steam Workshop mod scene (Frackin' Universe and others)
  • fun to play with friends
  • players keep coming back to it for years

What stands out

  • Lots of content / long
  • Great with friends

How it feels

  • chill
  • charming
  • exploratory

Watch out for

  • vanilla game feels shallow and empty without mods
  • linear, underwhelming main story
  • abandoned by the developers, with no more updates
  • many players boycott over the studio's use of unpaid labor

Best for: sandbox fans who enjoy modding a game into their own space adventure

Not for: players who want a content-rich vanilla experience or an actively updated game
