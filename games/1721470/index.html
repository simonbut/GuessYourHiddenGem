What players say

A free first-person mascot-horror puzzle game set in an abandoned toy factory, using a GrabPack to solve puzzles and escape monstrous toys. Chapter 1 is free; later chapters are sold separately.

Players love

  • intriguing lore and hidden secrets

How it feels

Watch out for

  • later chapters are paid separately
  • bugs, especially later boss fights
  • not very scary for older players
  • puzzles can be confusing or hard

Best for: younger horror fans and lore hunters who enjoy puzzle-driven scares

Not for: adults hoping for genuinely frightening horror
