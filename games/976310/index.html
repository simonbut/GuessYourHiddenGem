What players say

A brutal, cinematic fighting game with a big roster, deep mechanics and over-the-top Fatalities. There's lots of single-player content, including a story mode, Towers and the Krypt.

  • steep learning curve for newcomers

Best for: fighting game fans who want depth plus lots of solo content

Not for: players who want offline progression or a fighter that's easy to pick up
