What players say

A side-scrolling gothic metroidvania from Koji Igarashi. As Miriam, you explore a demon-filled castle and take shards from enemies to gain new powers. It feels like a loving return to classic Castlevania.

Players love

  • huge variety of shard abilities

  • wide enemy and weapon variety

How it feels

Watch out for

  • forced Epic online login on Steam
  • grinding for crafting drops
  • unclear where to go next

Best for: Symphony of the Night fans and anyone craving a classic castle-crawling metroidvania

Not for: players who won't put up with an Epic account login or item farming
