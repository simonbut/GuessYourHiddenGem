What players say

A long-running free-to-play team tank battler where you grind up the tech tree and fight PvP matches in many tank types. Many long-time players have a love-hate relationship with it, enjoying the battles but hating the matchmaking and monetization.

Players love

  • detailed tanks and good graphics
  • huge variety of tanks and roles
  • fun, rewarding battles when things go well
  • keeps players hooked for years

How it feels

  • tactical
  • tense
  • frustrating

Watch out for

  • matchmaking that puts you two tiers below the enemy
  • pay-to-win premium tanks and gold ammo
  • slow, expensive grind for credits and tanks
  • hard to learn with a big skill gap

Best for: patient tank lovers who enjoy a long grind and don't need realism

Not for: newcomers who want fair matchmaking or hate monetization pressure
