What players say

The HD remaster of the 2002 remake of the original Resident Evil, set in a zombie-filled mansion. It's slow, tense survival horror where every bullet, save and inventory slot matters.

Players love

  • oppressive, creepy mansion atmosphere
  • tense scarce-resource survival
  • faithful remake with fresh twists
  • detailed backgrounds that still hold up

What stands out

  • Genuinely scary

How it feels

  • tense
  • creepy
  • dread-filled

Watch out for

  • fixed camera and tank controls
  • heavy backtracking, tiny inventory
  • obscure trial-and-error puzzles
  • cutscene and display glitches

Best for: survival horror fans who enjoy resource management and classic fixed-camera design

Not for: players who want modern controls or action-heavy horror
