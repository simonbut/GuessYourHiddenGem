What players say

A free military shooter with several modes, including Operations (loot-and-extract raids) and Warfare (large Battlefield-style battles with vehicles). Players praise the gunplay and gun customization but report a lot of matchmaking and cheater trouble.

Players love

  • tense loot-and-extract Operations raids
  • free Battlefield-style Warfare battles
  • satisfying weapon customization
  • varied operators and playstyles
  • generous for a free game

What stands out

  • Competitive / ranked play

How it feels

  • tense
  • intense
  • competitive

Watch out for

  • new players matched against top-ranked veterans
  • frequent cheaters, especially in Operations
  • huge downloads and storage use every season
  • bugs, crashes and empty lobbies after updates

Best for: shooter fans who want free extraction and big-battle modes in one game

Not for: casual solo players who hate being stomped by sweaty or cheating squads
