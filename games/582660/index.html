What players say

A huge open-world action MMORPG with fast combo-based combat and many life skills like fishing, trading, cooking and horse training. It is a sandbox with no single goal, and players describe it as beautiful but very grindy.

Players love

  • fast, combo-based action combat
  • beautiful world and graphics
  • detailed character creator
  • huge range of life skills and activities, including idle ones
  • playable without spending money

What stands out

  • Steep learning curve
  • Lots of content / long

How it feels

  • sprawling
  • grindy
  • flashy

Watch out for

  • endless grind for silver and gear enhancement
  • cluttered, confusing UI
  • overwhelming for new players
  • paid convenience items

Best for: players who want a beautiful MMO with flashy combat and plenty of non-combat hobbies to sink hours into

Not for: anyone who hates heavy grinding, cluttered menus or paid convenience items
