What players say

An open-world superhero adventure where you swing through Marvel's New York as an experienced Peter Parker, mixing combo combat with a big story. Players say moving around the city is so much fun that they skip fast travel.

Players love

  • fluid, addictive web-swinging
  • fast, satisfying Arkham-style combat
  • well-written story and characters
  • fun, lively New York to explore
  • so fun to traverse that players skip fast travel

How it feels

  • exhilarating
  • heroic
  • action-packed

Watch out for

  • repetitive side activities and collectibles
  • random crashes and eaten inputs on PC
  • fights get frustrating later on and in the DLC

Best for: players who want to feel like Spider-Man, and fans of the Batman Arkham games

Not for: players who tire of open-world checklists and repeated enemy brawls
