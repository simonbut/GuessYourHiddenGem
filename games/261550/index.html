What players say

A medieval sandbox where you rise from a nobody to a lord or king, raising armies, joining kingdoms and fighting in large battles alongside your troops. Part strategy game, part action RPG.

Players love

  • big battles you fight in personally
  • freedom to rise from nobody to ruler
  • building and commanding your own army
  • large mod scene extends it
  • easily hundreds of hours

How it feels

  • epic
  • brutal
  • grindy

Watch out for

  • shallow diplomacy and repetitive late game
  • feels unfinished versus what was promised
  • weak AI, bugs and crashes
  • poor support for modding and multiplayer

Best for: players who want to command and fight in big medieval battles

Not for: players who want rich story, deep politics or polished systems without mods
