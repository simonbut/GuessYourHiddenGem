What players say

A free-to-play sci-fi FPS where huge numbers of players fight combined-arms battles with infantry, tanks and aircraft across giant continents. Nothing else quite matches its scale, but the population has shrunk and crashes are common.

Players love

  • unmatched scale of massive battles
  • combined-arms infantry, armor and air
  • big fights still happen at prime time
  • fair free-to-play, little need to pay

How it feels

  • epic
  • chaotic
  • nostalgic

Watch out for

  • frequent crashes, even in the tutorial
  • shrinking population outside peak hours
  • cheaters and neglected bugs
  • brutal learning curve against veterans

Best for: players craving giant persistent battles who can play at peak times

Not for: newcomers who want a smooth, welcoming start
