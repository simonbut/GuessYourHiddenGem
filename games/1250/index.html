What players say

A classic co-op wave shooter where up to six players pick perks and hold out against waves of grotesque monsters, buying weapons between rounds. It's gory, tense and best with a group of friends.

Players love

  • brutal, satisfying gunplay and headshots
  • perk classes for different playstyles
  • learnable enemy types like the Fleshpound
  • great with friends, nostalgic
  • heavy soundtrack and grim atmosphere

What stands out

  • Great with friends
  • Dark, mature themes

How it feels

  • gory
  • tense
  • chaotic

Watch out for

  • small playerbase, mostly modded servers
  • DDoS attacks and disconnects on servers
  • dated, clunky and buggy, odd hitboxes
  • fiddly hosting and Workshop setup

Best for: friend groups who want old-school co-op horde shooting

Not for: solo players looking for an active public scene
