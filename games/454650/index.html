What players say

An anime action game where you create your own fighter, learn moves from across Dragon Ball and fix history through time-travel missions, alone or in online co-op. Players report flashy fights and a lot of grinding for rare drops.

Best for: Dragon Ball fans who love building their own character and don't mind grinding

Not for: players who dislike grinding, buying lots of DLC or unreliable online play
