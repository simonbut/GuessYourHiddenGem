What players say

A big-budget action RPG in the Dragon Age world where you fight in real time and lead a team of companions. It looks great and the combat is fun, but long-time fans are split on its lighter tone and writing.

Players love

  • fast, fun real-time combat
  • polished visuals and animation
  • detailed character creator

How it feels

  • colorful
  • heroic
  • lighthearted

Watch out for

  • quippy, Marvel-style dialogue
  • choices barely matter, including those from earlier games
  • shallow, bland companions
  • cartoonish art direction for the setting

Best for: action RPG players, or newcomers to the series, who care more about combat than about lore

Not for: longtime Dragon Age fans who want dark, choice-heavy storytelling like Origins
