What players say

An open-world fantasy action RPG where you travel with AI pawn companions, fight huge monsters and stumble into unplanned adventures. Exploring feels like real travel and many say the Dark Arisen expansion lifts it, though it can grow repetitive over a long playthrough.

Players love

  • fun, satisfying combat
  • Dark Arisen expansion adds a lot

How it feels

  • adventurous

  • chaotic

Watch out for

  • repetitive enemies and caves
  • dull fetch quests and thin story
  • crashes and performance problems for some
  • clunky, dated movement and systems

Best for: players who love wandering a dangerous world and making their own stories

Not for: those who want a strong story or tightly designed quests
