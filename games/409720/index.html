What players say

A remaster of the BioShock sequel where you play a Big Daddy searching Rapture for your bonded Little Sister. Haunting atmosphere and sharper combat, but this PC version crashes a lot.

Players love

  • playing as a Big Daddy
  • plasmids and guns used together
  • emotional father-daughter story
  • haunting atmosphere of Rapture
  • excellent Minerva's Den DLC

How it feels

  • atmospheric
  • haunting
  • emotional

Watch out for

  • frequent crashes, often needing a fix mod
  • you must save manually and often or lose progress
  • remaster buggier than the original
  • feels like more of the same as BioShock

Best for: fans of the first BioShock willing to install a crash fix for a more emotional sequel

Not for: anyone unwilling to save constantly or mod around crashes
