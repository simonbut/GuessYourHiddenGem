What players say

An early-access, physics-driven medieval combat sim with gory duels and a progression mode that takes you from beggar to armored fighter. Fans love the core fighting, but many say the latest update left it buggy and broken.

Players love

  • Physics-driven, in-depth sword fighting
  • challenging, rewarding progression mode
  • lots of cool new features and content
  • detailed graphics and player models

How it feels

  • brutal
  • chaotic

Watch out for

  • recent update made weapons stop cutting
  • game-breaking bugs, crashes and softlocks
  • enemies floor or one-shot you instantly
  • poor performance, especially in crypts

Best for: Medieval combat enthusiasts who enjoy physics sandboxes and can live with early access

Not for: Anyone wanting a stable, polished game right now
