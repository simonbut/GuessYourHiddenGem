What players say

A cinematic third-person shooter that follows a burned-out Max through São Paulo, built on slow-motion dives, brutal gunfights and Max's cynical narration.

Players love

  • weighty, satisfying gunplay
  • bullet time that never gets old
  • detailed enemy reactions and ragdoll physics
  • dark, gripping cinematic story
  • excellent soundtrack and atmosphere

How it feels

  • gritty
  • tense
  • cinematic
  • dark

Watch out for

  • mandatory Rockstar launcher causes login and startup trouble
  • clunky cover system
  • long unskippable cutscenes
  • feels more like GTA than the earlier Max Payne games

Best for: players who want a tight, story-driven action shooter with stylish slow-motion combat

Not for: fans who want the Remedy-era tone of the first two games, or anyone who hates third-party launchers
