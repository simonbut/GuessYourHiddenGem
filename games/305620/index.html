What players say

A quiet first-person survival game in the frozen Canadian wilderness. You manage warmth, food and fatigue, read the weather, and decide whether a trip out of shelter is worth the risk. It has an open-ended survival mode and a separate story mode, Wintermute.

Players love

  • meditative but deadly survival
  • every decision about warmth and food matters
  • beautiful stylized winter art
  • survival sandbox mode is the main draw
  • wolves, bears and blizzards keep you humble

What stands out

  • Relaxing & cozy

How it feels

  • lonely
  • tense
  • peaceful
  • bleak

Watch out for

  • Wintermute's later chapters and ending disappoint
  • paid early-access DLC frustrates longtime fans
  • lots of slow walking
  • developer-community friction

Best for: patient players who want a slow, thoughtful solo survival sandbox

Not for: players after action, base building or a strong story campaign
