What players say

A medieval city builder where you lay out villages freely without a grid, manage families and goods through the seasons, and sometimes fight battles. It's beautiful and absorbing, but still unfinished and thin on late-game content.

Players love

  • beautiful, natural-looking villages
  • free-form, gridless building and plots
  • deep, believable economy
  • atmospheric music and detail
  • easy to lose hours to

How it feels

  • peaceful
  • immersive
  • absorbing

Watch out for

  • unfinished, slow development
  • thin late game and weak combat or diplomacy
  • confusing UI and little tutorial
  • updates that break saves or mechanics

Best for: city builder fans who love realistic medieval towns and slow, detailed planning

Not for: anyone wanting a finished game, a campaign or deep strategy battles
