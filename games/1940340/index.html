What players say

A turn-based roguelike sequel where you drive a stagecoach of doomed heroes across a ruined land, juggling stress, relationships and positioning in brutal fights. Runs are long, grim and tense, and it plays very differently from the first game.

Players love

  • gorgeous art and slick animations
  • dark, moody atmosphere and audio
  • tactical combat built on positioning and tokens
  • many hero and team combinations
  • hard-won boss victories

How it feels

  • grim
  • tense
  • tactical
  • atmospheric

Watch out for

  • runs can take two to four hours
  • drops the first game's dungeon crawling
  • confusing tokens and tooltips
  • slow stagecoach travel and downtime

Best for: tactics fans who enjoy long, punishing roguelike runs and party synergies

Not for: players wanting more of the first game's dungeon delving and estate building
