What players say

A third-person WWII stealth shooter set in Italy where you scout big, open sandbox maps and line up long-range shots, with a slow-motion X-ray kill cam. The campaign can also be played in two-player co-op.

Players love

  • satisfying long-range sniping
  • large open maps with many approaches
  • slow-motion X-ray kill cam
  • big step up from earlier entries

How it feels

  • tense
  • tactical
  • satisfying

Watch out for

  • forgettable story
  • missions get repetitive
  • enemies spot you too easily, even through cover
  • crashes, launch failures or endless loading for some

Best for: patient players who like planning stealth approaches and pulling off perfect long shots

Not for: players who want a strong story or a realistic sniper sim
