What players say

A third-person PvPvE extraction shooter where you raid a hostile future Earth for loot while fighting machines and other players, then try to get out alive. Every run puts your gear on the line.

Players love

  • losing all your gear in one death

Best for: players who thrive on risky PvP and the thrill of extracting with loot

Not for: PvE-only players who hate losing gear to other players
