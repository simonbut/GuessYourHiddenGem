What players say

A free-to-play post-apocalyptic vehicle combat MMO where you build armored machines from parts and fight in PvP and PvE battles. Players love the builder and the chaotic fights, but most complain about pay-to-win monetization and a heavy grind.

Players love

  • deep, creative vehicle builder
  • satisfying, destructive combat
  • huge freedom in designs and weapons
  • fun PvE modes against bots

How it feels

  • chaotic
  • creative
  • competitive
  • grindy

Watch out for

  • pay-to-win with expensive in-game market
  • extremely slow, grindy progression
  • low player count and long or uneven matchmaking
  • graphics update raised hardware requirements

Best for: players who love building and testing their own vehicle designs, especially against bots

Not for: free players who want fair competitive PvP without spending money
