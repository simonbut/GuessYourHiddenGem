What players say

A first-person open-world RPG set in a dark take on Arthurian legend, playing much like Skyrim with grim, Giger-tinged art. Explore dungeons, follow side quests and make choices that shape the story.

Players love

  • Elder Scrolls feel with darker style
  • haunting art direction and music
  • story choices with real consequences
  • varied hand-made dungeons
  • lots of side quests to explore

How it feels

  • dark
  • mysterious
  • atmospheric

Watch out for

  • clunky combat and odd hitboxes
  • glitches and rough edges
  • gets too easy in the late game
  • slow tutorial start

Best for: Skyrim and Oblivion fans craving a darker, story-rich open world

Not for: players expecting Souls-like combat or AAA polish
