What players say

A turn-based tactics game where you run a mercenary company of giant 'Mechs, taking contracts and salvaging parts during an interstellar civil war. Battles are slow and careful, and money is always tight.

Players love

  • deep 'Mech loadout customization
  • rewarding turn-based tactics
  • huge mod scene like RogueTech and BTA
  • mercenary company management
  • faithful to BattleTech lore

How it feels

  • tactical
  • methodical
  • punishing

Watch out for

  • AI wins through numbers and better hit odds
  • swingy, RNG-heavy hit chances
  • money and repair costs make progress a grind
  • crashes and stability problems

Best for: BattleTech and MechWarrior fans and tactics players who love tinkering

Not for: players who hate missed dice rolls and constant money pressure
