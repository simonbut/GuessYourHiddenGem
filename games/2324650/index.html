What players say

A free, short Sonic visual novel where you investigate Sonic's 'murder' aboard a train by questioning the cast, broken up by quick running minigames. Light, silly and over in an afternoon.

How it feels

Watch out for

  • Investigation needs little real deduction

Best for: Sonic fans who want a short, free and funny mystery

Not for: Players wanting a challenging detective game with real deduction
