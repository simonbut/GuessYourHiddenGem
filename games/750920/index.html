What players say

The final chapter of Lara Croft's reboot trilogy, set in lush Latin American jungles and ruins. It leans more on exploration, tombs and puzzles than combat, with plenty of climbing and collectibles.

Players love

Watch out for

  • weakest trilogy story
  • bugs, puzzles break above 60fps
  • clunky climbing and grabbing
  • bloated with collectibles

Best for: players who enjoy exploration and puzzle tombs in beautiful settings

Not for: those wanting lots of combat or a gripping story
