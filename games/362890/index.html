What players say

A fan-made, Valve-approved remake of Half-Life rebuilt in the Source engine, keeping the original's story while modernizing visuals and heavily reworking the alien world of Xen.

Players love

  • faithful and lovingly made
  • best modern way to play Half-Life 1
  • great soundtrack and atmosphere
  • workshop mod support
  • cheap on sale

How it feels

  • atmospheric
  • nostalgic
  • tense

Watch out for

  • Xen drags on too long
  • weak guidance, easy to get lost
  • crashes and Linux/Proton issues
  • weak, inconsistent gunplay

Best for: Half-Life fans and newcomers wanting the classic in modern form

Not for: players who want a strictly 1:1 remake or dislike old-school level design
