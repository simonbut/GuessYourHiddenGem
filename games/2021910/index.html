What players say

A free 1v1 game where every chess capture turns into a first-person shootout between pieces with guns. It plays mostly as a goofy shooter on a chessboard, and players enjoy it most with a friend.

  • buggy, with crashes mid-match
  • very little real chess strategy left

Best for: friends looking for a silly, chaotic free game to trash-talk over

Not for: chess players hoping for real strategy or anyone wanting a balanced competitive shooter
