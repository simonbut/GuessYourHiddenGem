What players say

The free-to-play Unreal Engine 5 sequel to the third-person mythology MOBA. Battles feel more modern than Smite 1 and builds are more flexible, but balance, bugs and toxic matches divide players.

Players love

  • item and aspect builds that change how gods play
  • sharper graphics and new engine over Smite 1
  • steadily improving with patches

How it feels

  • competitive
  • intense
  • chaotic

Watch out for

  • messy balance, tanky gods dealing huge damage
  • toxic teammates, feeders and throwers
  • heavy monetization, rebuying gods and skins
  • bugs, stutter and lag

Best for: Smite veterans and third-person MOBA fans who love tinkering with builds

Not for: players with little patience for toxic teammates, or anyone wanting a stable, balanced game
