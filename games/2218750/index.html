What players say

A Vampire Survivors-style horde roguelite dressed in gritty, old-school Diablo pixel art. Pick a hero, mow through swelling monster waves, and carry loot home to power up the next run.

Players love

  • Diablo 1/2 look and vibe
  • loot that carries between runs
  • large roster of distinct heroes
  • real build variety from gear and upgrades
  • tons to unlock and achieve

What stands out

  • Addictive, one-more-run

How it feels

  • dark
  • addictive
  • laid-back

Watch out for

  • crashes mid-run wipe progress
  • runs get too easy and trivial
  • one dominant build makes choices moot
  • slow, stretched-out achievement grind

Best for: Vampire Survivors fans who want Diablo-style gear and long-term unlocks

Not for: players looking for tough runs full of meaningful decisions
