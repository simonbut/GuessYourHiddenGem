What players say

A samurai-era action RPG from Team Ninja. You fight warriors and yokai through mission-based levels using stances, Ki pulses and a loot-heavy gear system. It plays like a brutal Souls-like crossed with Ninja Gaiden.

Players love

  • deep stance-switching combat
  • huge weapon and build variety
  • deeply satisfying once mechanics click
  • long campaign packed with content

What stands out

How it feels

  • brutal

  • rewarding

Watch out for

  • heavily recycled enemies and levels
  • two-hit deaths and double-boss fights
  • overwhelming loot and murky upgrades
  • janky controls, camera and hitboxes

Best for: patient action fans who love mastering deep, technical melee combat

Not for: players who hate repeated enemies, loot management, or punishing difficulty
