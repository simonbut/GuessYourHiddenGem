What players say

A third-person souls-like shooter where you explore randomized worlds, take on strange bosses and combine two archetypes into builds, solo or in three-player co-op. Each playthrough shows only part of the content, so replays reveal new events and secrets.

Players love

How it feels

  • adventurous

Watch out for

  • obscure secrets that need a guide
  • weak writing and dialogue
  • performance issues and forced upscaling

Best for: co-op groups who like tough bosses, build tinkering and hunting secrets

Not for: players who want clear direction and a strong story
