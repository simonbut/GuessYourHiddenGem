What players say

A single-player, XCOM-style turn-based tactics game where you lead an elite squad in the final days of the Clone Wars. Players love the story and squad, but frequent crashes and soft-locks spoil many runs.

Best for: Star Wars fans who enjoy story-driven, XCOM-style tactics

Not for: tactics veterans wanting deep challenge or anyone with no patience for crashes
