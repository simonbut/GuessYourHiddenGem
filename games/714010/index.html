What players say

A free aim trainer for FPS players, with drills for flicking, tracking and target switching plus stats on your weak spots. Many use it as a daily warm-up for Valorant, CS2 and other shooters.

Best for: competitive shooter players who want a structured daily aim routine

Not for: players who hate upsells or just want a lightweight tool
