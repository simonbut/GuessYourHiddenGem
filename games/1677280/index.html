What players say

A WWII real-time tactics game in the Mediterranean, with squad battles, multiplayer, and an Italian campaign that pairs a turn-based strategic map with real-time fights. Players say it's much better since its rough launch.

Players love

  • much better since the 3.0 update
  • fun AI skirmishes for solo players
  • approachable for newcomers to the series
  • good map design

How it feels

  • tactical
  • strategic
  • intense

Watch out for

  • pricey battlegroup DLC
  • lopsided faction balance and unit spam
  • cramped zoom and unscalable UI
  • few factions, no Soviets

Best for: WWII RTS fans, especially solo players who like AI skirmishes, buying on sale

Not for: CoH2 loyalists and players put off by paid DLC content
