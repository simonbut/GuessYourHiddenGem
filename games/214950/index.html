What players say

A historical Total War game where you run an ancient empire on a turn-based campaign map and command large armies in real-time battles. Players describe a mix of empire building, politics and big battles, and say it's easy to lose whole evenings to it, especially with mods.

Players love

  • huge replay value for history fans
  • strong mod scene, especially Divide Et Impera (DEI)
  • satisfying large real-time battles
  • deep politics and diplomacy
  • holds up years after its rough launch

How it feels

  • epic
  • strategic
  • absorbing

Watch out for

  • crashes, freezes and soft-locks, even in the prologue
  • distrust of CA/SEGA and DLC seen as cash grabs

Best for: history fans who want deep ancient-world grand strategy and don't mind modding

Not for: players with no patience for crashes, bugs or fiddling with mods
