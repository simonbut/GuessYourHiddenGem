What players say

A co-op survival-crafting game for 1-6 players. You play scientists trapped in a huge underground research facility after a containment breach, scavenging, building bases and exploring portal worlds. Players describe a creepy but funny Half-Life-style atmosphere.

Players love

Watch out for

  • fatal errors and connection problems
  • mod tools tied to Epic and mod.io

Best for: co-op groups who love exploring a creepy, funny sci-fi facility together

Not for: players who dislike inventory juggling or long survival grinds
