What players say

A fast first-person shooter-slasher where Lo Wang dashes and double-jumps through demon hordes with katanas and guns while chasing Borderlands-style loot. Goofy, gory and fast-paced, and best with friends.

Players love

  • fast, satisfying combat with dashes and double jumps
  • huge weapon variety with gem mods
  • goofy one-liners and crude humor
  • great fun in co-op
  • runs smoothly, even on modest PCs

What stands out

  • Genuinely funny
  • Great with friends

How it feels

  • fast-paced
  • goofy
  • gory

Watch out for

  • repetitive missions on reused maps
  • thin, forgettable story
  • heavy grind for mods and secrets
  • cluttered, confusing upgrade systems

Best for: fans of Doom-style speed mixed with looter-shooter builds, especially in co-op

Not for: fans of the first reboot who want a linear, story-driven shooter
