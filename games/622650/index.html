What players say

A chapter-based first-person horror puzzle game set in an old cartoon studio, drawn in a sepia ink-and-paper style. It's more eerie and lore-driven than truly scary.

Players love

  • striking ink-and-paper cartoon look
  • intriguing story and lore
  • creepy old-studio atmosphere
  • lovable characters like Bendy and Boris

What stands out

  • Genuinely scary

How it feels

  • eerie
  • nostalgic
  • mysterious

Watch out for

  • bugs, broken saves and achievements
  • camera stutter at high frame rates
  • tedious backtracking and fetch quests
  • clunky, underbaked gameplay

Best for: mascot-horror fans drawn to the cartoon art style and lore

Not for: horror veterans after real scares or polished gameplay
