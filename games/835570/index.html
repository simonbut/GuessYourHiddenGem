What players say

A free-to-play medieval war game where you fight as a third-person hero while commanding your own unit of troops in 15v15 sieges. Players say nothing else plays quite like it, but it comes with heavy grind and monetization baggage.

  • brutal grind and veteran stomping
  • unstable servers and bugs

Best for: patient players who want large-scale medieval sieges and unit command

Not for: newcomers who hate grind or spending money to stay competitive
