What players say

A 1-4 player co-op heist shooter where you rob banks and other targets quietly or fight your way out. After a rough launch, many players say it has improved a lot, though Payday 2 veterans are still divided.

Players love

Watch out for

  • thin content compared to Payday 2
  • many weapons locked behind DLC
  • shallower skills and progression than Payday 2
  • crashes on some maps

Best for: co-op groups who want stealth-or-loud heists with friends

Not for: Payday 2 fans who expect its depth of content and progression
