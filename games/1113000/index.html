What players say

A turn-based JRPG where you spend a school year in a quiet rural town, building friendships by day and entering a TV world at night to solve a string of murders. Players describe it as a warm, long hangout with friends and a mystery underneath, and many say the ending made them cry.

Players love

  • lovable, well-written main cast
  • emotional story that moves many players to tears
  • great soundtrack
  • murder mystery that hooks early and keeps twisting
  • strong bond between the friend group

What stands out

  • Emotional, tearjerker moments
  • Mystery & plot twists
  • Hilarious

How it feels

  • cozy
  • mysterious
  • nostalgic
  • emotional

Watch out for

  • repetitive, simplistic dungeons
  • combat feels dated next to newer Persona games
  • long and dialogue-heavy

Best for: players who want a long, character-driven JRPG with real emotional payoff

Not for: players who want deep combat or dislike lots of dialogue
