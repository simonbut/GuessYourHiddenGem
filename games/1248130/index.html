What players say

A slow, hands-on farming sim where you plant, harvest, raise animals and run production chains with lots of machinery. Players describe a calm, repetitive loop that is easy to sink hours into, alone or with friends.

Players love

  • great co-op with friends

Watch out for

  • some jobs, like baling, are slow without mods
  • confusing menus and controls at first
  • multiplayer lag and costly server hosting

Best for: players who unwind with slow, methodical work and like adding mods

Not for: anyone wanting story, action, or fast progression
