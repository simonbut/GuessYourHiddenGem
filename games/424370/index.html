What players say

An isometric dark-fantasy action RPG where you build your character freely, with no fixed classes. Players describe it as a flashy, good-looking Diablo-style campaign. The developers have stopped working on it and online play has been shut down.

Players love

  • huge, flexible passive skill tree and build freedom
  • satisfying, flashy combat
  • gorgeous visuals and cutscenes
  • great value on deep discount
  • enjoyable single-player campaign

How it feels

  • flashy
  • dark
  • grindy

Watch out for

  • abandoned by the developers
  • multiplayer and co-op shut down
  • story ends unfinished
  • repetitive late acts and endgame

Best for: solo ARPG fans who want a pretty, build-heavy campaign for cheap

Not for: anyone who wants co-op, a long endgame or a finished story
