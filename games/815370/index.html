What players say

A punishing survival game set in the Amazon. You track hunger, thirst, wounds, parasites and sanity while snakes, jaguars and tribes hunt you. You can play the story campaign or open survival, alone or with friends.

Watch out for

  • steep, poorly explained learning curve
  • clunky controls and interface
  • tedious food and water upkeep
  • multiplayer bugs and crashes

Best for: survival fans who enjoy learning through repeated harsh deaths, ideally with friends

Not for: anyone who wants a relaxed or newcomer-friendly survival game
