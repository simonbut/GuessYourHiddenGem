What players say

An open-zone 3D Sonic adventure where you speed across big islands, fight giant bosses and dive into short linear Cyberspace stages. It feels ambitious and fast, with rough edges.

Players love

How it feels

Watch out for

  • repetitive collectible grinding
  • awkward controls and camera
  • janky physics and odd glitches
  • bland, empty-feeling open zones

Best for: Sonic fans who want a bigger, freer 3D Sonic with real boss spectacle

Not for: players who dislike map-marker collectathons or rough polish
