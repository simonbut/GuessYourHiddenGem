What players say

A dark, story-heavy card battler and sequel to Lobotomy Corporation. You defend a strange library from guests in staged turn-based clashes and turn the defeated into books, over a story that can run past 100 hours.

Players love

  • outstanding soundtrack

  • deep deckbuilding and team builds
  • huge amount of content

How it feels

  • dark

Watch out for

  • confusing tutorial and UI
  • harsh difficulty spikes
  • very slow start
  • some love the story but hate the combat

Best for: patient players who love dense lore and demanding, build-heavy card combat

Not for: players who want a smooth difficulty curve or clear explanations
