What players say

A modern remake of the Persona 3 JRPG. By day you go to school and build friendships, and by night you climb the tower Tartarus in turn-based fights. Players call it a slow-burning, emotional story about death and finding meaning in life.

Players love

How it feels

  • emotional

Watch out for

  • repetitive Tartarus floor grinding
  • very slow start to the story
  • some cast members are bland or grating
  • feels weaker than Persona 5 to some

Best for: players who want a long, emotional JRPG with strong characters and music

Not for: anyone with little patience for repetitive dungeon crawling or slow pacing
