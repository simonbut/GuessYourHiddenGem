What players say

A long-running free-to-play MMORPG set across Tolkien's Middle-earth, from the Shire to Mordor. It feels like an old-school, slow-paced world to sink into, with dated mechanics around the edges.

Players love

  • lovely music

Watch out for

  • dated UI and clunky mechanics
  • heavy paywalls and cash shop
  • long downloads and login failures
  • slow travel and repetitive quests

Best for: Tolkien fans who want a slow, story-rich MMO to settle into

Not for: players wanting a modern, polished MMO or full access without paying
