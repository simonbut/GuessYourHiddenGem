What players say

A hardcore, realistic extraction shooter where you gear up, loot raids and must escape alive or lose everything. Brutal to learn, with a separate PvE mode many players prefer.

Players love

  • tense raids where every decision counts
  • deep, realistic gun handling
  • PvE mode for a calmer experience
  • huge payoff once you learn maps
  • nothing else quite like it

How it feels

  • tense
  • punishing

Watch out for

  • widespread cheaters in PvP
  • stuttering, crashes and poor optimization
  • no map or tutorial, punishing learning curve
  • account and support issues

Best for: patient players who want the most punishing, high-stakes shooter around

Not for: newcomers who want guidance or anyone short on time and patience
