What players say

A VR tag game where you move gorilla-style, pushing off the ground and walls with your hands to run, climb and leap. You play tag or infection in online lobbies, and players say the movement is fun and surprisingly skill-based.

Players love

  • fun arm-based movement
  • great first game for new VR players
  • easy to make friends online
  • lots of maps and mods

How it feels

  • energetic
  • chaotic
  • social

Watch out for

  • toxic lobbies full of very young players
  • cheaters and modders
  • paid on Steam but free on Quest
  • pricey cosmetics

Best for: VR owners who want energetic, social movement-based fun

Not for: players who can't stand loud kids and toxic lobbies
