What players say

A top-down online war game where thousands of players fight one persistent war, with every truck of supplies, factory and frontline push made by real people. It plays like a second job, in good and bad ways.

Players love

  • massive player-driven war
  • logistics and crafting really matter
  • many roles, from medic to tank crew
  • regiments teach and welcome new players
  • deep, complex systems

What stands out

  • Steep learning curve

How it feels

  • epic
  • grueling
  • cooperative

Watch out for

  • steep learning curve and heavy grind
  • toxic players and mass-report bans
  • balance swings between factions
  • developers seen as out of touch

Best for: players who enjoy teamwork, logistics and long-term persistent wars

Not for: players who want quick action without a big time investment or a community to deal with
