What players say

A grid-free medieval city builder where you paint zones, design custom monuments and watch a village grow on its own. It feels laid-back and absorbing, though its systems run deeper than the cozy look suggests.

Players love

  • total freedom with no grid
  • modular custom monuments and buildings
  • villagers form their own natural roads
  • relaxed, forgiving pace
  • hours vanish without noticing

What stands out

  • Relaxing & cozy
  • Addictive, one-more-run

How it feels

  • relaxing
  • creative
  • absorbing

Watch out for

  • performance drops as the city grows
  • crashes and game-breaking bugs
  • trade and production poorly explained
  • zoned housing is hard to control

Best for: city builder fans who love freeform layouts without combat stress

Not for: players who want clear tutorials or very large, smooth-running cities
