What players say

A slow, realistic tactical FPS from one of the original Rainbow Six developers, built around room-clearing missions you can play solo, in co-op or in PvP. It feels tense and methodical, and mistakes get punished fast.

Players love

  • realistic, methodical CQB that punishes mistakes
  • deep gunsmith and loadout customization
  • strong mod support
  • rewards coordinated teamwork in co-op
  • solid hardcore option for solo players

How it feels

  • tense
  • methodical
  • hardcore

Watch out for

  • enemy AI sees through walls with aimbot-like aim
  • still janky and unfinished after 1.0
  • slow, thin updates over many years
  • AI teammates removed for solo players

Best for: patient tactical-shooter fans who love tweaking loadouts and mods, solo or with a squad

Not for: players who hate inconsistent AI or want a polished, finished game
