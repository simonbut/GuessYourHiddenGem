What players say

An arcade open-world racer set on a scaled-down map of the entire USA, where you swap between cars, bikes, boats and planes. Players mostly enjoy it as a relaxed road trip, and it can now be played offline.

Players love

  • enormous USA map great for cruising
  • land, sea and air vehicle variety
  • lots of content and events
  • offline mode added
  • good fun on sale

How it feels

  • relaxed
  • sprawling
  • casual

Watch out for

  • floaty, arcade handling physics
  • requires Ubisoft Connect; anti-cheat blocks Linux and Steam Deck
  • dated graphics

Best for: players who like relaxed road trips and varied vehicle types over realistic driving

Not for: sim racing fans who want weighty, precise handling
