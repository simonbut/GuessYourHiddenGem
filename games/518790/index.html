What players say

An open-world hunting sim where you track animals through beautiful wilderness, read signs and sounds, and line up the perfect shot. It's slow, patient and often very peaceful.

Players love

  • breathtaking, detailed maps
  • lifelike, varied wildlife and sound
  • relaxing, patient gameplay loop
  • satisfying hunts for rare trophy animals
  • runs smoothly on most PCs

What stands out

  • Relaxing & cozy

How it feels

  • peaceful
  • patient
  • immersive

Watch out for

  • much of the content locked behind DLC
  • slow walking with limited fast travel
  • Wounded animals vanish or absorb bullets
  • bugs with tracks, saves and multiplayer

Best for: patient players who enjoy calm stalking in beautiful nature

Not for: players who want fast action or hate buying lots of DLC
