What players say

A medieval grand strategy game where you rule a dynasty, not a country. Each heir brings their own traits and schemes, and every campaign turns into a messy family saga of plots, marriages and betrayals.

Players love

  • emergent family drama and betrayals
  • endlessly replayable, every campaign a new story
  • character-driven roleplay over map painting
  • deep, complex interlocking systems
  • big mod scene extends its life

What stands out

  • Steep learning curve

How it feels

  • scheming
  • dramatic
  • absorbing
  • chaotic

Watch out for

  • huge pile of pricey DLC
  • base game feels thin without expansions
  • poor tutorial, steep learning curve
  • cluttered, confusing menus

Best for: patient strategy and history fans who love telling their own stories

Not for: newcomers who want clear guidance, or anyone put off by heavy DLC
