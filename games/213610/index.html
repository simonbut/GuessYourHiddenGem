What players say

A classic 3D Sonic platformer with hero and dark campaigns that mix blazing speed stages, treasure hunts and mech shooting, plus the Chao Garden pet sim. Nostalgic and thrilling when it flows, janky when it doesn't.

Players love

  • blazing Sonic and Shadow speed stages
  • adorable, absorbing Chao Garden
  • iconic, catchy soundtrack
  • memorable hero and dark stories

How it feels

  • fast
  • nostalgic
  • janky

Watch out for

  • tedious Knuckles treasure-hunt stages
  • clunky controls and wild camera
  • rough PC port needing mods and fixes
  • some frustrating, rage-inducing stages

Best for: Sonic fans and nostalgic players who love speed stages and raising Chao

Not for: players unwilling to mod a rough port or put up with an old-school camera
