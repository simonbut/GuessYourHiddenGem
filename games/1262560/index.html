What players say

Criterion's open-world arcade racer set in Fairhaven: hunt down Most Wanted rivals, hop into cars you find around the city and shake off police chases. Breezy, Burnout-style driving rather than a story or tuning game.

Players love

  • great soundtrack
  • Well-designed open-world city
  • lots of detailed cars to find
  • graphics still look good
  • simple pick-up-and-play racing

How it feels

  • fast
  • breezy
  • nostalgic

Watch out for

  • nothing like the 2005 Most Wanted
  • stiff handling and awkward controls
  • little story or visual customization
  • Tied to the EA app and online account

Best for: Burnout Paradise fans wanting relaxed open-world racing

Not for: anyone hoping for the 2005 Most Wanted or deep car tuning
