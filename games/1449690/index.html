What players say

The full Telltale Walking Dead saga in one remastered package: all four seasons plus side stories, following Lee and Clementine through a zombie apocalypse. You talk, choose and survive, and it hits hard.

Players love

  • heartbreaking, memorable characters
  • Lee and Clementine's bond
  • tough choices that hurt
  • great value, especially on sale
  • good-looking comic-style visuals

What stands out

  • Emotional, tearjerker moments

How it feels

  • emotional
  • bleak
  • tense
  • heartfelt

Watch out for

  • more cutscene than gameplay
  • choices rarely change the outcome
  • later seasons weaker than Season 1
  • bugs like lost cloud saves and missing audio

Best for: story lovers ready for a long, emotional choose-your-path journey

Not for: players who want action or choices that truly branch the story
