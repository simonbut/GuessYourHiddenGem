What players say

A hardcore Source-engine tactical shooter with no crosshair or HUD hand-holding and very quick deaths, played in PvP or co-op. Players describe it as tense and unforgiving, though the player base is now small.

Players love

  • lethal, every-bullet-counts gunplay
  • no crosshair or minimap, intense atmosphere
  • immersive sound design
  • runs well on low-end PCs
  • workshop mods still active

How it feels

  • tense
  • gritty
  • unforgiving

Watch out for

  • very small player base, only a few active servers
  • same veteran regulars dominate PvP
  • grenade and RPG spam on spawns
  • feels dated

Best for: tactical shooter fans who want unforgiving, realistic firefights

Not for: newcomers expecting a busy modern online shooter
