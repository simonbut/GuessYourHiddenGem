What players say

A tactical team-based shooter about attacking and defending rooms with destructible walls, gadgets and a long list of operators. Planning and map knowledge matter as much as aim, and players call it both addictive and rage-inducing.

Players love

  • deep strategy beyond raw aim
  • destructible walls and clever small details
  • lots of operators and abilities to learn
  • big adrenaline highs when you win
  • hard to stop playing

What stands out

  • Competitive / ranked play

How it feels

  • tense
  • tactical
  • frustrating
  • intense

Watch out for

  • steep learning curve and weak tutorial
  • frustrating solo-queue teammates
  • cheaters and unexplained bans
  • new CPU requirement locked out older PCs

Best for: patient players who like learning maps and coordinating with a team

Not for: newcomers who want to jump in casually or play solo queue in peace
