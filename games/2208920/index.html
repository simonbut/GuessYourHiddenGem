What players say

A huge open-world RPG where you play a Viking raider in Dark Ages England: raid monasteries, forge alliances, build a settlement and explore a beautiful map. It's more a Viking saga than a stealth game, and very long.

Players love

  • strong Viking atmosphere and setting
  • beautiful, vast world
  • engaging story
  • solid, fun combat
  • relaxing exploration

How it feels

  • epic
  • atmospheric
  • sprawling
  • grindy

Watch out for

  • bloated, very long and repetitive
  • frequent crashes on PC
  • new NG+ achievements upset completionists
  • weak stealth, feels little like Assassin's Creed

Best for: Viking fans who enjoy slowly soaking up a giant open world over many hours

Not for: players wanting classic Assassin's Creed stealth or a tight, focused campaign
