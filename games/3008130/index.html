What players say

A first-person zombie survival game that brings back Kyle Crane for parkour, brutal melee and a new beast mode, set in an Alpine valley of woods, farms and small towns. Players call it a smaller follow-up that is worth it on sale.

Players love

  • beautiful scenery, rain and night atmosphere
  • Kyle Crane's return and story
  • satisfying beast mode
  • tense, scary nights

How it feels

  • tense
  • gory
  • atmospheric

Watch out for

  • short and light on content for the price
  • paid audio DLC upset players
  • sluggish combat and clunky parkour for some
  • woodland map has less rooftop parkour

Best for: Dying Light fans who want Kyle Crane back and catch it on sale

Not for: players expecting a full-size sequel at full price
