What players say

A first-person border-checkpoint sim in a 1980s communist state: check papers, search cars for hidden contraband and deal with smugglers, across a story campaign and an endless mode. Methodical inspection work is broken up by shootouts and car chases.

Players love

  • Papers, Please-style document checks in 3D
  • detailed car searches for hidden contraband
  • inspection work mixed with combat
  • engaging story mode
  • lots of upgrades, especially in endless mode

How it feels

  • methodical
  • tense
  • darkly comic

Watch out for

  • gets repetitive after a few hours
  • clunky driving and car chase sections

Best for: Papers, Please fans who want to physically search cars and deal with smugglers themselves

Not for: players who need lots of variety over long sessions or dislike clunky driving
