What players say

A moon-set Borderlands shoot-and-loot that bridges the first two games and tells Handsome Jack's rise. Low gravity, double jumps and oxygen tanks put a bouncy twist on familiar Borderlands 2 gunplay.

Not for: players expecting a long, polished follow-up to Borderlands 2
