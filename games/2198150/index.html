What players say

A small diorama builder where you doodle castles, cottages and ruins on a grassy glade and the game fills in charming detail. There are no goals or management, just calm, freeform building.

Players love

  • intuitive, easy-to-learn building
  • relaxing with no pressure
  • gorgeous visuals and calming music
  • sheep, ducks and birds bring builds to life
  • frequent updates and Workshop support

What stands out

  • Relaxing & cozy

How it feels

  • cozy

  • creative

Watch out for

  • limited pieces and decoration options
  • no villagers walking around
  • building tools can feel fiddly

Best for: anyone wanting a pressure-free creative outlet

Not for: players who want goals, challenges or management systems
