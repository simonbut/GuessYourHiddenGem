What players say

A slow, cinematic Wild West epic where you ride with Arthur Morgan's outlaw gang through a huge, lived-in frontier. Players say it's about soaking in the world and the story, not rushing.

Players love

  • Arthur Morgan and memorable gang members
  • emotional story that moves many players to tears
  • incredibly detailed, lived-in open world
  • NPCs with their own lives and personalities
  • beautiful soundtrack and atmosphere

How it feels

  • immersive
  • emotional
  • epic
  • slow-burn

Watch out for

  • clunky controls and inventory, especially on keyboard
  • slow pacing in the opening chapters
  • Red Dead Online full of griefers and cheaters
  • stuttering and performance issues on some PCs

Best for: patient players who want a deep story and an immersive world to live in

Not for: players who want fast, snappy action or a polished online mode
