What players say

A short, creepy puzzle-platformer where you guide Six, a tiny girl in a yellow raincoat, through a giant ship of grotesque adults. You hide, sneak and run instead of fighting.

Players love

  • deeply unsettling, dreadful atmosphere
  • grotesque, memorable monster designs
  • gorgeous art and eerie sound design
  • tense chase and stealth sequences

What stands out

  • Genuinely scary

How it feels

  • creepy
  • dark
  • tense
  • melancholic

Watch out for

  • Hard-to-judge depth perspective
  • floaty, awkward controls and grabbing
  • very short, around 3-4 hours
  • repetitive retries and sparse checkpoints

Best for: players who love dark, storybook-style atmosphere in a short sitting

Not for: those who want deep puzzles or precise platforming
