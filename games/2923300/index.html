What players say

A free clicker where you click a banana to make a number go up, and get a banana item every few hours that can be traded on the Steam market. Players treat it as a meme: simple and mindless, with easy achievements, but many call it shallow or a cash grab.

  • items sell for pennies on a flooded market
  • feels like a market cash grab
  • lag, bugs and high RAM use

Best for: achievement hunters and anyone curious about the meme

Not for: players wanting a real incremental game or real money
