What players say

A story-driven post-apocalyptic shooter that leaves the Moscow Metro for a year-long train journey across Russia, mixing semi-open levels with tense scavenging, crafting and gunfights.

Players love

  • thick, immersive atmosphere
  • lovable crew aboard the Aurora train
  • tactile survival details like gas mask filters
  • gorgeous, varied environments
  • moving story with a memorable ending

What stands out

  • Emotional, tearjerker moments

How it feels

  • bleak
  • tense
  • immersive
  • melancholic

Watch out for

  • hidden morality system decides the ending
  • crashes, bugs and lost saves
  • floaty movement and janky controls
  • more linear and hand-holding than it looks

Best for: players who love atmospheric, narrative shooters like S.T.A.L.K.E.R.

Not for: players wanting a free-form open world or fully polished tech
