What players say

Bethesda's open-world RPG set in space: you design your own ships, join faction questlines and hop between planets. Players who like the classic Bethesda formula sink hundreds of hours in. Others find it empty and boring.

Players love

  • detailed ship building
  • strong faction questlines and side quests
  • beautiful worlds and a 'NASA-punk' look
  • lots of freedom and things to explore
  • big mod scene adds a lot

How it feels

  • sprawling
  • exploratory
  • grounded
  • uneven

Watch out for

  • Loading screens everywhere, even for simple travel
  • Procedural planets feel empty and repetitive
  • bland characters and lifeless dialogue
  • shallow, boring systems for some

Best for: Bethesda fans who like open-ended questing, ship tinkering and modding

Not for: players who want seamless space flight or rich, hand-built planets
