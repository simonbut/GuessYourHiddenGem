What players say

A first-person burglary sim: scout houses, learn residents' routines, sneak in, grab valuables and sell them to buy better tools for wealthier neighborhoods. Addictive and tense, but famously janky.

Players love

  • addictive case-break-steal-sell loop
  • tense sneaking around residents' routines
  • lots of tools and loot to unlock
  • good hiding spots and escape options

How it feels

  • sneaky
  • tense
  • addictive
  • relaxed

Watch out for

  • janky physics and general bugginess
  • Police and NPCs spot you unfairly fast
  • gets repetitive after a few hours
  • broken saves and progress-blocking glitches

Best for: players who enjoy casual stealth and planning small heists in a sandbox

Not for: players who need polished, realistic stealth
