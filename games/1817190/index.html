What players say

A compact open-world superhero adventure as Miles Morales, swinging through a snowy, festive New York with new venom and camouflage powers. Players see it as a fun, shorter follow-up to the first game.

Players love

  • smooth, joyful web-swinging
  • snowy holiday New York atmosphere
  • venom and camouflage powers freshen combat
  • Miles as a likeable hero with his own identity
  • great visuals and music

What stands out

  • Short

How it feels

  • festive
  • heartfelt
  • energetic

Watch out for

  • very short, feels like a DLC
  • pricey for the amount of content
  • crashes and performance problems on some PCs
  • much is reused from the first game

Best for: fans of the first Spider-Man game who want more swinging and a tight, short story

Not for: players expecting a full-length open-world game
