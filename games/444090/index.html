What players say

A free-to-play 5v5 fantasy hero shooter with a large roster of champions and a loadout/talent system for customizing builds. Players remember it fondly, but many say its developer has abandoned it.

Players love

  • flexible loadouts and talents
  • free and runs on weak PCs
  • many prefer it to Overwatch and Marvel Rivals

How it feels

Watch out for

  • abandoned by the developer, no updates
  • cheaters with little moderation
  • launch failures and anti-cheat problems

Best for: hero shooter fans curious about a free classic with deep build customization

Not for: anyone wanting an actively supported, fair competitive game
