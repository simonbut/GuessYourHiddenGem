What players say

A free-to-play looter shooter in the vein of Warframe and Destiny. You pick from a roster of Descendants with distinct abilities, fight giant co-op bosses, and grind materials to build out characters and weapons.

Players love

  • flashy abilities and solid gunplay
  • polished visuals for an F2P game
  • varied, well-designed characters
  • much improved since launch
  • fun in short daily sessions

How it feels

  • flashy
  • grindy
  • explosive
  • busy

Watch out for

  • repetitive grind with layered RNG drops
  • aggressive monetization and pay-to-win worries
  • heavy fan service puts some players off
  • poor optimization and high CPU load

Best for: looter shooter fans who enjoy chasing builds a little each day

Not for: players who hate grind and storefront pressure, or who already love Warframe
