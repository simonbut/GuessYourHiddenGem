What players say

An open-world racing game set across Mexico, with a huge range of cars to collect, tune and race, or just drive around and explore at your own pace.

Players love

  • huge, varied car collection
  • lots of tuning and visual customization
  • beautiful graphics and scenery
  • well optimized, runs smoothly even on modest PCs
  • fun free-roam driving around the map

How it feels

  • breezy
  • vibrant
  • relaxing
  • festive

Watch out for

  • forced Xbox/Microsoft account sign-in
  • progression gets repetitive and samey
  • map feels flat, small or lifeless to some
  • paid car packs, DLC ads and premium extras

Best for: car lovers who want a good-looking open world to collect, tune and race cars in

Not for: players who refuse an Xbox/Microsoft account sign-in or want a varied, lively map
