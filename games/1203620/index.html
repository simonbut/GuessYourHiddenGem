What players say

A survival-crafting action RPG, solo or co-op, set in a big handcrafted world swallowed by a deadly fog. You glide off cliffs, grapple up ruins, dive into the timed Shroud for loot, and build very detailed voxel bases.

Players love

  • best-in-genre base building
  • huge, gorgeous handcrafted world
  • rewarding exploration with hidden dungeons
  • fast gliding and grappling movement
  • fun co-op with friends

What stands out

  • Lots of content / long
  • Great with friends

How it feels

  • adventurous
  • cozy
  • tense
  • vast

Watch out for

  • clunky, spongy combat
  • repetitive grind for materials later on
  • awful voice acting on lore notes

Best for: builders and explorers who want a Valheim-style adventure with lots of freedom

Not for: players who care most about deep, satisfying combat
