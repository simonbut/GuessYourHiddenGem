What players say

An open-world fantasy action RPG where you climb onto big monsters mid-fight and travel with AI 'pawn' companions you build yourself and lend to other players. Players love the combat and call it a unique classic, but the world, quests and travel around it feel old-school and rough.

Players love

  • inventive, satisfying combat, including climbing big monsters
  • unique pawn companion system
  • many vocations and builds to experiment with
  • addictive and very replayable
  • still holds up more than a decade later

How it feels

  • epic
  • adventurous
  • old-school
  • grindy

Watch out for

  • lots of backtracking and fetch quests
  • very limited fast travel
  • same enemies over and over
  • weak story and forgettable characters

Best for: players who want inventive, physical combat and don't mind old-school rough edges

Not for: players who want a strong story or convenient travel and quest design
