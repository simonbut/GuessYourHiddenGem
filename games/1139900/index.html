What players say

A fast first-person cyberpunk ninja game where one hit kills you and one slash kills them. You wall-run, dash and slice through rooms, dying constantly and restarting instantly until you nail a clean run.

Players love

  • fluid, flow-state movement
  • instant retries make deaths snappy
  • fantastic synthwave soundtrack
  • stylish cyberpunk visuals
  • satisfying slicing and parrying

What stands out

How it feels

  • intense

  • punishing

Watch out for

  • brutal difficulty, no easy option
  • enemies snipe you instantly
  • frustrating later levels and puzzle sections
  • progress lost when quitting mid-level

Best for: precision-platformer fans who love mastering levels through trial and error

Not for: players who want a relaxed experience after a long day
