What players say

A Lego-like physics sandbox where you build or spawn vehicles from a huge Workshop, then drive, fly and smash them through destructible maps, solo or on multiplayer roleplay servers.

Players love

  • controls and editor take time to learn
  • can get boring without goals

Best for: builders and sandbox tinkerers, especially with friends

Not for: players who need structured goals or tutorials
