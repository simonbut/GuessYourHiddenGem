What players say

A first-person action adventure set in a lavish alternate-history Soviet utopia where robots have turned on people. Players compare it to BioShock and Prey, with a telekinetic glove, melee and gunplay, puzzles and a striking retro-futurist world.

Players love

  • stunning retro-futurist Soviet setting
  • great soundtrack of remixed Soviet music
  • fun combat with glove powers and free respecs
  • BioShock and Prey-like feel
  • DLCs round out the story

How it feels

  • surreal
  • stylish
  • bizarre

Watch out for

  • cringey protagonist and awkward dialogue
  • repetitive lock puzzles and testing grounds
  • bugs, crashes and lost save progress
  • too many cutscenes interrupting play

Best for: fans of BioShock-style shooters who value setting and art direction

Not for: players who can't stand corny writing or a chatty hero
