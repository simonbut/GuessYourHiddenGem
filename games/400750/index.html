What players say

A detailed WWII real-time tactics game where you command infantry, guns and tanks on the Eastern Front in missions, a Dynamic Conquest campaign and PvP/PvE battles. Careful planning that erupts into chaos.

Players love

  • take direct control of any soldier or tank
  • strong mod support
  • Dynamic Conquest campaign
  • deep WWII detail and realism
  • detailed graphics and animations

How it feels

  • immersive
  • challenging
  • chaotic
  • tactical

Watch out for

  • steep learning curve with little in-game teaching
  • exhausting micromanagement
  • unreliable unit AI and pathfinding
  • bugs and performance problems

Best for: WWII buffs who love detailed, hands-on RTS in the Men of War mold

Not for: players who want a streamlined RTS without micromanaging every soldier
