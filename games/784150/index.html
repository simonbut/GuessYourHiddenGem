What players say

A Soviet-themed city builder and logistics sim where you raise a republic by hand: on realistic mode every road, pipe, factory and bus route must be supplied, staffed and built. It is slow and demanding, and very rewarding.

Players love

  • deep simulation where everything has a cause
  • building with real materials, vehicles and workers
  • detailed water, sewage, heating and power networks
  • One-of-a-kind Soviet atmosphere
  • hundreds of hours of depth

How it feels

  • methodical
  • absorbing
  • challenging

Watch out for

  • clunky, dated interface
  • steep learning curve, poorly explained systems
  • fiddly road building and grid snapping

Best for: City-builder and logistics fans who want to plan every supply chain themselves

Not for: Players wanting a relaxing city builder they can learn quickly
