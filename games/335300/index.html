What players say

The definitive version of FromSoftware's divisive Souls sequel: a dark fantasy action RPG with lots of areas and builds, plus all three DLCs. Fans call it the series' black sheep, for better and worse.

Players love

  • big world with more areas than DS1 or DS3
  • wide build, weapon and spell variety
  • excellent DLC areas and bosses
  • beloved PvP and multiplayer
  • a distinct feel unlike other Souls games

What stands out

  • Lots of content / long

How it feels

  • bleak
  • punishing
  • atmospheric

Watch out for

  • clunky rolling, movement and the odd ADP stat
  • enemy ganks and long, annoying runbacks
  • many bosses feel easy or forgettable
  • official online servers went down

Best for: Souls fans who want more content and are curious about the series' strangest entry

Not for: newcomers who want the tightest Souls combat; DS1 or DS3 are safer starts
