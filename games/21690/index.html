What players say

An action-heavy Resident Evil set in Africa, built around two-player co-op with Chris and Sheva. It plays like an arcade third-person shooter, with memorable Wesker moments and very little horror.

Best for: two friends looking for a co-op action campaign

Not for: solo players or fans who want survival horror
