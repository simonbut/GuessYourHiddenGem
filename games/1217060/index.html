What players say

A colorful roguelite FPS where you pick an animal hero and shoot through procedurally generated stages, solo or in four-player co-op. Each run builds into a wild combo of weapons, scrolls and elemental effects.

Players love

  • absurd, game-breaking builds
  • great co-op with friends
  • Borderlands-style looter shooting
  • keeps getting new heroes and content

What stands out

How it feels

  • chaotic

  • breezy

Watch out for

  • poor English translation of descriptions
  • recent use of AI-generated art
  • repetitive stages with little map variety
  • hard to find public co-op matches

Best for: friends looking for a breezy co-op roguelite shooter with silly builds

Not for: players who need clear tooltips or object to AI art
