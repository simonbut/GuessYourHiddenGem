What players say

An open-world action RPG set in a vast Ancient Egypt, following Bayek's revenge story. Exploring tombs, cities and deserts is the highlight, while the level-gated RPG combat divides series fans.

Players love

  • stunning, immersive Ancient Egypt
  • Bayek is a standout protagonist
  • exploring tombs, pyramids and deserts
  • visuals that still impress
  • strong DLC expansions

How it feels

  • epic
  • atmospheric
  • adventurous

Watch out for

  • level gating blocks assassinations
  • repetitive, grindy late game
  • slow start to the story
  • crashes, bugs and Ubisoft Connect login

Best for: players who love exploring a rich historical world at their own pace

Not for: fans who want classic stealth-focused Assassin's Creed
