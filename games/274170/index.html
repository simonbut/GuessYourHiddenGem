What players say

A brutal top-down action sequel that concludes the Hotline Miami story, with one-hit deaths, fast violent combat and multiple playable characters. Players love the soundtrack and expanded story, but many find the larger levels frustrating.

Best for: fans of the first game and fast, hard action games with great music

Not for: players who get frustrated by constant deaths and trial and error
