What players say

An open-world shooter set in the Himalayan land of Kyrat, where you clear outposts, hunt wildlife, glide and grapple around mountains, and face the tyrant Pagan Min. It plays much like Far Cry 3 in a new, prettier setting.

Players love

  • beautiful Himalayan open world
  • satisfying stealth outpost clearing
  • memorable villain Pagan Min
  • freedom to roam and cause sandbox chaos

How it feels

  • adventurous
  • chaotic
  • scenic
  • wild

Watch out for

  • repetitive towers and side activities
  • performance problems on modern CPUs
  • Ubisoft Connect login and launch troubles
  • co-op is hard to connect and excludes story missions

Best for: players who liked Far Cry 3 and enjoy open-world sandbox mayhem

Not for: those tired of formula open-world checklists or wanting a story co-op campaign
