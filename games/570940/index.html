What players say

The remastered original Dark Souls: a tough, grim action RPG set in an interlocking world where shortcuts loop back to the hub. Dying a lot is part of the deal, and players say each victory feels earned.

Players love

How it feels

  • bleak

Watch out for

  • long, tedious runbacks to bosses
  • clunky, dated controls and combat
  • uneven or unfair bosses and areas
  • little guidance on where to go

Best for: patient players who love exploring a tightly built world and learning tough boss fights

Not for: players who want modern polish, clear direction or fair challenge at every step
