What players say

A third-person WWII tactical shooter in North Africa's deserts: sneak or shoot through open levels and land long-range kills shown in slow-motion X-ray. Patient and methodical, with a gory payoff.

Players love

  • feels dated next to later entries

Best for: WWII stealth snipers who love bloody kill cams, ideally picked up on a deep sale

Not for: newcomers to the series, who reviewers say should start with Sniper Elite 4
