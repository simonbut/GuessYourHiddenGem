What players say

An open-world shooter where a stranded tourist becomes a killer on a lawless tropical island. The story, Vaas and the open-world action still hold up for players, but the PC version crashes badly on modern systems.

Players love

  • Vaas is an iconic villain
  • Jason's transformation story arc
  • beautiful, explorable island
  • fun gunplay and lots to do
  • reasonable length, easy achievements

How it feels

  • wild
  • tense
  • darkly funny

Watch out for

  • frequent crashes on modern hardware
  • needs community fixes to run
  • Ubisoft Connect asks for login repeatedly

Best for: fans of classic open-world shooters who are willing to tinker to get it running

Not for: anyone who wants a game that works smoothly right out of the box
