What players say

A huge open-world action RPG set in Ancient Greece, where you play a Spartan mercenary, sail between islands, fight in big battles and take on myths. It plays more like an RPG than a classic stealth Assassin's Creed.

Players love

  • stunning, detailed Ancient Greece
  • hundreds of hours of content
  • rich historical and mythological detail
  • Deep RPG systems and loot
  • addictive exploration loop

What stands out

  • Lots of content / long

How it feels

  • epic
  • sunny
  • adventurous

Watch out for

  • repetitive, copy-paste side quests
  • grindy leveling and spongy enemies
  • Hardly feels like Assassin's Creed
  • overlong story and oversized map

Best for: players who love exploring a huge historical world for 100+ hours

Not for: fans of classic stealth Assassin's Creed or players who want a tight, short story
