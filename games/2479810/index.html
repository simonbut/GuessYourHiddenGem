What players say

An early-access, PvE-first open-world tactical shooter on a quarantined Southeast Asian island, played solo or in squads. Players describe slow, methodical firefights against tough AI in an immersive world, but many say the latest update made progression a heavy grind.

  • AI can be inconsistent or absurdly tanky
  • clunky movement

Best for: patient tactical shooter fans who enjoy fighting AI in PvE, solo or with friends

Not for: players short on time or who hate grinding for loot and reputation
