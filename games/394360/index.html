What players say

A grand strategy game where you lead any nation through World War II. You manage production, research, politics and armies on a huge map, and history can go off course.

Players love

  • complex systems that reward learning
  • excellent mods

How it feels

  • complex

  • challenging

Watch out for

  • expensive, ever-growing DLC
  • brutal learning curve, especially naval warfare
  • launcher and mod errors, bugs after updates

Best for: history and strategy fans willing to learn complex systems

Not for: newcomers who want quick, easy-to-learn strategy or dislike paying for many DLCs
