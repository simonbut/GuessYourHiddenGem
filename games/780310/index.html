What players say

A top-down base-building survival game with action-RPG combat, solo or in co-op for up to four players. You mine, build defenses and research tech on alien worlds, then hack and shoot through hordes that crash against your walls.

Players love

  • base building meets tower defense and ARPG
  • satisfying tech tree progression
  • fun co-op with friends
  • steady developer updates
  • hordes smashing into your defenses

How it feels

  • frantic
  • strategic
  • explosive

Watch out for

  • maps too small for huge bases
  • tutorial and quests explain too little
  • attacks and disasters come too often
  • fiddly resource and energy management

Best for: fans of base building and tower defense who also want to fight hands-on

Not for: players who hate juggling resources while constantly putting out fires
