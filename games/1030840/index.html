What players say

A ground-up remake of the 2002 classic: cab driver Tommy Angelo falls in with the Salieri crime family in a 1930s city. A linear, cinematic mob story with gorgeous visuals rather than a sandbox.

Players love

How it feels

Watch out for

  • infamous frustrating race mission
  • dated, basic shooting and driving
  • little to do in the open world
  • crashes and bugs for some

Best for: players who want a movie-like crime story with great characters

Not for: anyone expecting a GTA-style sandbox or the original's freedom
