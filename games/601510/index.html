What players say

A free-to-play Yu-Gi-Oh! card game built on Speed Duels, with smaller fields and character Skills. You collect cards, build decks and duel anime characters or other players in short matches that lean on nostalgia.

Players love

  • nostalgia for the anime and its characters
  • character Skills add anime flavor
  • simpler and more casual than Master Duel
  • lots of cards and decks to build

How it feels

Watch out for

  • long summon chains and slow opponent turns
  • the same meta decks in most matches
  • hard to keep up without spending

Best for: Yu-Gi-Oh! fans who want faster, lighter duels with anime nostalgia

Not for: players who dislike meta-heavy ladders and pay-to-compete card economies
