What players say

A dedicated aim trainer with a huge library of community-made scenarios, playlists and benchmarks. You click, track and flick at targets over and over, chasing scores to sharpen your aim for other shooters.

Best for: competitive FPS players willing to build a consistent daily training routine

Not for: players who want a game rather than a practice tool, or won't research which scenarios to use
