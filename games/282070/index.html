What players say

A survival game about civilians, not soldiers, trapped in a besieged city. By day you manage hunger, illness and morale in your shelter, and by night you scavenge, where every choice weighs on your conscience.

Players love

  • agonizing, meaningful moral choices
  • strong atmosphere and art style
  • fresh civilian view of war
  • heartbreaking, realistic depiction of war

What stands out

  • Dark, mature themes

How it feels

  • bleak

Watch out for

  • crashes on some systems
  • random events that punish you no matter what
  • can feel tedious or frustrating

Best for: players who want a heavy, thoughtful survival game where every decision hurts

Not for: anyone looking for light fun or sensitive to depressing themes
