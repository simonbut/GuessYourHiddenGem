What players say

A short, wordless puzzle platformer about a boy in a black-and-white world full of deadly traps. Its eerie forest atmosphere is memorable, but expect lots of trial-and-error deaths.

Players love

  • striking monochrome art style
  • haunting atmosphere and sound design
  • clever physics puzzles
  • still holds up years later

What stands out

  • Dark, mature themes
  • Genuinely scary
  • Challenging

How it feels

  • eerie
  • lonely
  • dark

Watch out for

  • trial-and-error deaths get frustrating
  • second half is weaker, with machinery and timing puzzles
  • clunky controls
  • very minimal story

Best for: players who want a short, atmospheric indie classic to finish in an evening or two

Not for: players who hate dying repeatedly
