What players say

An open-world tactical shooter on a huge island where you sneak, plan and fight a rogue spec-ops faction, solo or in co-op. Players say it plays slow and methodical. It has looter-shooter gear scores unless you switch on Immersive Mode.

Players love

  • deep military gear customization
  • satisfying tactical stealth combat
  • Immersive Mode and adjustable difficulty
  • large world for solo or co-op
  • beautiful environments and weather

What stands out

  • Great with friends

How it feels

  • tactical
  • methodical
  • tense
  • grindy

Watch out for

  • clunky, slow movement and controls
  • long-standing bugs and crashes
  • Ubisoft Connect launch and co-op errors
  • repetitive outposts in an empty world

Best for: patient stealth fans who like customizing loadouts and tuning difficulty

Not for: Wildlands fans expecting the same feel, or anyone who hates launcher trouble
