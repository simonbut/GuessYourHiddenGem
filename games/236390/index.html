What players say

A free-to-play military vehicle game where you fight in tanks, planes, helicopters and ships, unlocking machines from different nations through long research trees. Players describe it as addictive and infuriating at once.

Players love

  • addictive pull to grind up the tech trees

  • fun, satisfying battles when they go your way

How it feels

Watch out for

  • long grind and expensive premium shortcuts
  • balance complaints about nations and top tier
  • frustrating, often toxic matches
  • developers seen as ignoring player feedback

Best for: military vehicle fans who don't mind a long grind and frequent frustration

Not for: players who hate grinding or who get easily frustrated
