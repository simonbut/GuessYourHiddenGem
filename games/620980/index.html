What players say

The classic VR rhythm game: slice colored blocks to the beat with two sabers while dodging walls. It doubles as a sweaty workout, and with community custom songs it becomes nearly endless.

Players love

  • addictive, physical slicing rhythm
  • surprisingly good workout
  • custom song mods add endless content
  • still a must-own VR game

How it feels

  • energetic
  • sweaty
  • addictive

Watch out for

  • paid song DLC adds up fast
  • thin, samey base song list
  • updates break mods and mod loaders
  • no Steam Cloud saves or Workshop support

Best for: VR owners who love music and moving, especially those willing to mod in custom songs

Not for: players who will not mod and do not want to pay per song pack
