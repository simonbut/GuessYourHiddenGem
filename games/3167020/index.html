What players say

A top-down PvE extraction shooter starring ducks: raid maps, loot, upgrade your gear and build your hideout, with no PvP. Players describe Tarkov-like tension in a lighter, more relaxed package.

Players love

  • Tarkov-style looting without PvP stress
  • solid gunplay and weapon customization
  • satisfying progression
  • addictive one-more-raid loop
  • workshop mods add useful QoL features

What stands out

How it feels

  • tense
  • quirky
  • addictive

Watch out for

  • grindy, especially after losing gear
  • later maps get huge and tedious
  • bugs such as crashes and footstep audio pops
  • anger over a malicious-mod incident and the team's response

Best for: solo players who want extraction-shooter tension at their own pace

Not for: players who hate grinding back lost gear
