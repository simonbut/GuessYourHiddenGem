What players say

A short, funny first-person story game about an office worker and a narrator who reacts to every choice you make. You replay it to find its many endings and jokes about how games work.

Players love

  • witty, reactive British narrator
  • genuinely funny writing
  • clever commentary on games and choice
  • many endings to find across quick runs
  • memorable endings like the broom closet

What stands out

  • Genuinely funny
  • Hilarious
  • Multiple endings & choices

How it feels

  • funny
  • surreal
  • thoughtful

Watch out for

  • just walking, almost no real gameplay
  • short for the price, better on sale
  • crashes on modern CPUs with many cores
  • confusion with the separate Ultra Deluxe edition

Best for: players who love meta humor and experimental story games

Not for: players who want real mechanics or challenge
