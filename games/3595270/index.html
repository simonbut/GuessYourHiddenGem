What players say

A modern military shooter with a story campaign, competitive multiplayer and an extraction-style Zombies mode. Multiplayer is fast and slick, with lots of guns and camos to grind, while the campaign is widely seen as the weak spot.

Players love

  • fast, smooth movement
  • well-balanced time-to-kill
  • big, varied map selection
  • fun camo challenges to grind
  • chill PvE Zombies to grind in

How it feels

  • competitive
  • fast-paced
  • intense

Watch out for

  • short, open-world-padded campaign
  • cheaters in multiplayer
  • sweaty lobbies and strict skill matchmaking
  • crashes and online-only connection errors

Best for: multiplayer grinders who love fast movement and unlocking camos

Not for: players buying it mainly for the campaign
