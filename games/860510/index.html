What players say

A dark 2.5D horror-puzzle adventure where you play Mono, with Six at your side, through a warped world of huge, grotesque adults. Players report constant dread, tense chases, simple puzzles and an emotional, wordless story.

Players love

Watch out for

  • frustrating combat and flashlight sections
  • bugs in some sequences, even in the Enhanced Edition

Best for: players who want atmospheric, story-rich horror without cheap jump scares

Not for: players who need tight, responsive platforming controls
