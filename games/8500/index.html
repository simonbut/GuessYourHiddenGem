What players say

A huge single-server space MMO and sandbox where players run the economy, wars and politics. You command ships, mine, trade, explore or fight, and learning it is a long climb, more like a hobby than a game.

Players love

  • vast player-driven universe
  • huge depth and skill ceiling
  • freedom to pick any role
  • generous free-to-play start
  • stories from player politics, scams and fleet fights

What stands out

  • Steep learning curve

How it feels

  • vast
  • ruthless
  • complex

Watch out for

  • new players get ganked by veterans
  • multiboxing dominates and feels required
  • pay-to-win feel of the paid subscription
  • steep learning curve, big time investment

Best for: players who want a deep, long-term sandbox MMO and enjoy risk and politics

Not for: casual or solo players who hate losing what they worked for
