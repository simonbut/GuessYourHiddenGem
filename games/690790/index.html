What players say

A demanding rally sim where you tear through gravel, mud and snow stages while your co-driver calls pace notes. One mistake can end a run. Fans still call it the best rally game around, and praise it with a wheel or in VR.

Players love

  • convincing, punishing rally handling
  • intense, sweaty stage runs
  • great VR and wheel support
  • beautiful, varied stages

What stands out

  • Steep learning curve

How it feels

  • intense
  • demanding
  • thrilling

Watch out for

  • save or championship progress resets
  • little guidance for newcomers
  • lingering bugs and launch problems

Best for: sim racers who want a real challenge on loose surfaces

Not for: casual players who want an easygoing arcade racer
