What players say

A free-to-play dark fantasy action RPG known for its huge passive tree and gem-based skill system, with new leagues every few months and near-endless build crafting.

Players love

  • enormous skill tree and build freedom
  • free and not pay-to-win
  • constant new league content and mechanics
  • nearly limitless endgame depth

What stands out

  • Steep learning curve
  • Lots of content / long

How it feels

  • dark
  • overwhelming
  • grindy
  • rewarding

Watch out for

  • brutal, confusing learning curve
  • recent leagues and balance changes disliked
  • can feel grindy and monotonous
  • account bans and region locks with poor support

Best for: theorycrafters who want a deep ARPG they can sink thousands of hours into

Not for: casual players wanting simple, pick-up-and-play hack and slash
