What players say

An online social deduction game in the Mafia/Werewolf tradition: around 15 players get secret roles, then argue, bluff, and vote in chat to hang the killers before night falls again.

Players love

  • the classic Mafia-style formula
  • wide cast of town and evil roles
  • bluffing your way through as evil
  • custom games with a big friend group

How it feels

  • paranoid
  • chaotic
  • social

Watch out for

  • toxic chat full of slurs
  • throwers and early leavers are common
  • weak, inconsistent moderation
  • shrinking player base since ToS2

Best for: groups of friends who can fill a 15-player custom lobby

Not for: solo players hoping for friendly public lobbies, or younger kids
