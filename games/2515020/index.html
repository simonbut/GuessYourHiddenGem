What players say

A dark fantasy action RPG following Clive Rosfield's quest for revenge. Fast real-time combat and towering Eikon battles deliver huge spectacle, broken up by very long cutscenes and slow stretches.

Players love

How it feels

  • epic

Watch out for

  • endless cutscenes and slow pacing
  • dull, padded side quests
  • poor PC optimization, crashes and FPS drops
  • thin RPG systems; combat becomes cooldown spam

Best for: action fans who want cinematic boss battles and a grim story and don't mind long cutscenes

Not for: fans wanting turn-based combat, party RPG depth or open exploration
