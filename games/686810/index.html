What players say

A hardcore 50-vs-50 World War II shooter with tanks, artillery, and a commander layer. Matches feel like real battlefield chaos: long runs across fields, sudden deaths from far away, and squads that live or die by voice comms.

Players love

  • unmatched WWII atmosphere and sound
  • large-scale battles with artillery and tanks
  • rewards real teamwork and communication
  • realistic, historically grounded feel

How it feels

  • intense
  • chaotic
  • immersive
  • punishing

Watch out for

  • random teams that won't talk or cooperate
  • micro-stutters and unstable FPS
  • lots of walking and frequent deaths with no clear cause
  • hard for new players to find good servers

Best for: patient players with a mic who want a tactical, team-driven war shooter

Not for: anyone after quick, run-and-gun action like Call of Duty
