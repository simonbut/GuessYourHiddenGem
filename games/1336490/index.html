What players say

A dark fantasy roguelite city builder: you found short-lived settlements in a rain-soaked wilderness, balancing humans, beavers, lizards and more against the Queen's patience. Each town is a roughly hour-long strategy puzzle.

Players love

  • every settlement plays differently
  • hour-long runs that skip the solved-city slog
  • complexity introduced in gentle layers
  • deep strategy with lots to juggle
  • lovely rainy art and music

What stands out

  • Addictive, one-more-run
  • Relaxing & cozy
  • Challenging

How it feels

  • tense
  • cozy
  • strategic

Watch out for

  • grindy meta progression gets repetitive
  • top difficulties feel punishing and formulaic

Best for: city-builder fans who want short, replayable strategy runs instead of one endless city

Not for: players who want a timer-free builder or one permanent city to perfect
