What players say

A grim survival city builder: you run the last city in a frozen world, keep the generator burning and pass harsh laws to keep people alive. It's tense and punishing, and the choices often feel morally heavy.

Players love

  • unmatched bleak, freezing atmosphere
  • excellent soundtrack
  • Laws and choices that make you feel guilty
  • tense, high-stakes resource juggling
  • gripping scenarios and story

What stands out

  • Challenging
  • Addictive, one-more-run

How it feels

  • bleak
  • tense
  • oppressive
  • thoughtful

Watch out for

  • steep learning curve with a weak tutorial
  • scripted events reward memorizing more than adapting
  • punishing: one mistake can wreck a run
  • performance issues on some PCs

Best for: strategy players who like hard choices and getting pushed to the edge

Not for: anyone who wants a relaxed, forgiving city builder
