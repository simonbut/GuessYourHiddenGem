What players say

A very long, stylish turn-based JRPG where you live a Tokyo school year, building friendships and juggling free time while raiding the twisted minds of corrupt adults with your Phantom Thieves.

Players love

  • memorable, lovable cast
  • incredible soundtrack
  • slick, stylish menus and UI
  • rewarding calendar and friendship system
  • huge amount of content

How it feels

  • stylish
  • energetic
  • emotional

Watch out for

  • slow start and heavy dialogue
  • overlong and padded for some
  • combat is fairly easy
  • some uncomfortable sexualized content

Best for: players ready to sink 100+ hours into a character-focused anime RPG

Not for: those who dislike long visual-novel-style stretches
