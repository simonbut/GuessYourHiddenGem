What players say

A first-person mining sandbox where you dig, pan and smelt ore by hand, then build contraptions to automate it all. It starts slow and fiddly, then turns into a satisfying tinkering playground once the machines take over.

Players love

  • satisfying automation once machines run
  • hours vanish without noticing
  • freedom to mess with physics and explosions
  • steady updates from a dedicated dev

What stands out

  • Couch co-op / party & family fun

How it feels

  • quirky
  • tinkery
  • relaxed

Watch out for

  • fiddly item handling, carry one thing at a time
  • slow, tedious early game
  • lag and crashes as loose items pile up
  • aimless endgame, shallow progression

Best for: tinkerers who enjoy hands-on, physical automation and don't mind a slow start

Not for: players who want smooth controls, an inventory or deep automation progression
