What players say

A low-poly sandbox FPS where you fight huge bot battles with infantry, tanks and helicopters. The base game is simple, but Workshop mods let you stage almost any war imaginable.

Players love

  • massive, chaotic bot battles
  • incredible Steam Workshop mod scene
  • mods turn it into any era or universe
  • tanks, helicopters and other vehicles
  • runs on modest hardware without heavy mods

How it feels

  • chaotic
  • goofy
  • epic

Watch out for

  • lags with many bots or mods
  • dim or suicidal AI allies
  • very long early access with thin base content

Best for: sandbox fans who love tinkering with mods to build their own wars

Not for: players wanting a polished campaign or online multiplayer
