What players say

A co-op action RPG where you track and fight huge monsters, then carve them up to craft better weapons and armor. Each hunt is a demanding boss fight you learn through practice and preparation.

Players love

How it feels

  • epic

Watch out for

  • heavy grinding for gear
  • lost progress from Steam Cloud save bugs
  • some late-game fights are long and frustrating

Best for: players who enjoy mastering a weapon and grinding for gear against tough bosses

Not for: players who dislike repetition or long fights against one enemy
