What players say

A top-down survival sandbox where you explore an endless world, fight bosses and build a settlement. Feels like Terraria's gear-and-boss loop mixed with light RimWorld colony management, solo or co-op.

Players love

  • Terraria-style bosses with RimWorld-style colony
  • settlers that automate your base's chores
  • settlers can join you in boss fights
  • clear class-based gear progression
  • fun to play through with a friend or partner

What stands out

  • Pixel art

How it feels

  • adventurous
  • cooperative
  • satisfying

Watch out for

  • midgame turns into a repetitive boss-and-gear loop
  • shallow settler relationships and colony depth
  • feels like a top-down Terraria clone
  • item bloat with many items going unused

Best for: Terraria fans who want a top-down twist and a self-running colony, especially co-op duos

Not for: players seeking deep RimWorld-style colony sim or a long, varied endgame
