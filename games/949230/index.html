What players say

A big, detailed city builder where you lay out roads, zones and services and watch a simulated metropolis grow. Many players find it fun, especially with mods, but it is heavy to run.

Players love

  • satisfying freedom to design cities
  • strong, growing mod support
  • many quality-of-life gains over the first game
  • noticeably improved since launch
  • addictive, time-swallowing building loop

How it feels

  • absorbing
  • creative
  • sprawling

Watch out for

  • very poor performance even on high-end PCs
  • crashes, especially in large or modded cities
  • Simulation feels shallow and hard to read
  • feels like a step down from Cities: Skylines 1 to many

Best for: city-builder fans with a strong PC who are happy to lean on mods

Not for: players on mid-range hardware or those happy with Cities: Skylines 1
