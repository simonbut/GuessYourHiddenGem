What players say

A colorful vehicle-building sandbox where you snap together blocks to make cars, planes, boats and mechs, then test them in an open world, alone or with friends. It feels like playing with Lego that actually drives and flies.

Players love

  • build anything from cars to mechs
  • easy to learn, hard to master
  • deep logic system for advanced builds
  • workshop for sharing and downloading builds
  • chaotic fun with friends

How it feels

  • creative
  • playful
  • chaotic

Watch out for

  • much of the content locked behind pricey DLC
  • runs out of things to do after a while
  • occasionally janky physics

Best for: creative tinkerers who love designing and testing their own machines

Not for: players who won't buy DLC or who need structured goals to stay engaged
