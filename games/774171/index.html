What players say

A colorful anime rhythm game with just two lanes: your character runs through levels while you hit notes in time to the music. It's easy to pick up, but the hardest charts get very demanding.

Players love

  • simple to learn, hard to master
  • bright, cute art and animations
  • good music and fun collabs
  • great value once you own the DLC
  • easy to dip into for a few relaxing songs

How it feels

  • cheerful
  • energetic
  • cute
  • colorful

Watch out for

  • most songs locked behind the Muse Plus DLC
  • Can't buy individual song packs
  • busy visuals hide notes
  • Input drops and music desync for some

Best for: casual rhythm fans who will buy the full DLC

Not for: players who don't want to pay extra for most of the songs
