What players say

A first-person narrative adventure where you relive a life's memories, and your real blinks, read by webcam, push the story forward. A short interactive movie that hits hard by the end.

Players love

  • blinking as both control and metaphor
  • story that leaves players sobbing
  • moving music
  • heartfelt, delicate writing
  • short, roughly two-hour length

What stands out

  • Emotional, tearjerker moments
  • Wholesome & heartwarming

How it feels

  • emotional
  • bittersweet
  • melancholic
  • intimate

Watch out for

  • blinks skip dialogue and strain eyes

Best for: anyone wanting a short, deeply moving story to finish in one sitting

Not for: players wanting real gameplay, or anyone sensitive to themes of death, illness and grief
