What players say

A story-driven first-person shooter set in Rapture, a collapsing underwater city, mixing guns with plasmid powers. Exploring it feels eerie and immersive, though the PC port often needs fixes to run well.

Players love

  • eerie underwater city atmosphere
  • gripping story about a failed utopia
  • rich environmental storytelling
  • creative plasmid powers and traps
  • still holds up nearly two decades later

What stands out

  • Genuinely scary

How it feels

  • eerie
  • immersive
  • tense

Watch out for

  • PC port crashes and stutters
  • audio glitches and cut-outs
  • needs tweaks for mouse and FOV
  • dated gun handling

Best for: Story and atmosphere lovers willing to tinker with an old PC port

Not for: Players who want a plug-and-play PC game or punchy modern gunplay
