What players say

An open-world survival RPG where you're an ordinary traveler, not a hero: manage food, disease and your pack while trekking Aurai alone or in co-op. Slow, harsh and janky, but it delivers a real sense of adventure.

Players love

  • survival where preparation matters
  • no hand-holding exploration
  • online and split-screen co-op
  • old-school sense of adventure
  • rewarding once it clicks

What stands out

  • Great with friends
  • Couch co-op / party & family fun

How it feels

  • adventurous
  • unforgiving
  • immersive

Watch out for

  • clunky stamina-heavy combat
  • lots of slow walking between places
  • no player marker on map, vague quests
  • co-op progress and rewards favor the host

Best for: patient players who want an old-school survival adventure, ideally with a co-op partner

Not for: anyone who wants polished combat, fast travel or quest markers
