What players say

A long-running fantasy MMORPG where you train many skills, quest and fight bosses across a big open world at your own pace. Players describe it as a laid-back, nostalgic time sink, though the cancelled Reignited fresh-start mode colors many recent reviews.

Players love

  • huge range of skills and quests
  • AFK-friendly skilling while watching shows
  • easy to return after months away
  • strong nostalgia for longtime players
  • in-game cash shop removed

How it feels

  • nostalgic
  • laid-back
  • grindy

Watch out for

  • much content locked behind membership
  • bot-ridden, inflated economy
  • cancelled fresh-start servers upset many
  • hard for newcomers to get into

Best for: returning veterans and players who love slow, open-ended skilling

Not for: newcomers hoping for a fresh economy or anyone unwilling to pay for membership
