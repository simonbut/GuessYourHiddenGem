What players say

A very fast, demanding shooter where you dash, double-jump and swap weapons constantly to tear through demons. Different enemies call for different weapons, and a heavy metal soundtrack drives it all.

Players love

  • very fast, aggressive combat
  • weapon swapping to counter each demon
  • chainsaw and glory-kill resource loop
  • pounding metal soundtrack
  • wide range of difficulty options

How it feels

  • intense
  • brutal
  • frantic

Watch out for

  • too much platforming and jumping puzzles
  • ammo scarcity forces constant weapon swaps
  • too fast and busy compared to Doom 2016

Best for: players who want a hard, fast shooter that rewards mastery

Not for: fans of slower, heavier Doom 2016-style shooting
