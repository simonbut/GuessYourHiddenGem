What players say

A VR multiplayer shooter with hands-on gun handling and Counter-Strike style modes like search and destroy, plus community modes. The gunplay feels great, but many veterans say the game's best days are behind it.

Watch out for

  • toxic players and kick-happy lobbies

Best for: VR owners who want tight, tactile gunplay and CS-like matches

Not for: players hoping for the old modded custom game modes or busy servers
