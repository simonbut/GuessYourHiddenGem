What players say

A first-person stealth puzzle game about sneaking into your neighbor's house to find out what he's hiding in the basement while he hunts you. Players find it more goofy than scary, and many keep playing mainly for community mods.

Watch out for

  • illogical puzzles that need guides
  • dumb neighbor AI that gets stuck
  • less scary than the alpha builds

Best for: younger players and fans who enjoy goofing around with community mods

Not for: anyone wanting logical puzzles, tight controls or real stealth horror
