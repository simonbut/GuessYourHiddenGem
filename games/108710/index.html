What players say

A psychological thriller where a novelist searches for his missing wife in a dark small town, burning away shadowy enemies with a flashlight before shooting them. Told like an episodic TV series with a moody Stephen King flavor.

Best for: story lovers who enjoy cinematic mysteries and Remedy's style

Not for: players who need varied, modern combat to stay engaged
