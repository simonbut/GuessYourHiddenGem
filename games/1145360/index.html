What players say

A fast hack-and-slash roguelite where you fight your way out of the Greek underworld. Every death sends you back home to a cast of gods and characters, and the story moves forward with each run.

Players love

  • smooth, responsive fast combat
  • story that advances every time you die
  • excellent voice acting and music
  • varied god boon builds each run
  • gorgeous art style

What stands out

  • Addictive, one-more-run

How it feels

  • energetic
  • witty
  • stylish
  • heartfelt

Watch out for

  • repetitive once the novelty fades
  • long grind to finish the story and unlock everything

Best for: players who want snappy action and a story that rewards repeated runs, even roguelike newcomers

Not for: players who dislike repeating the same areas over and over
