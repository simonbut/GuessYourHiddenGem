What players say

The roguelike deckbuilder that made the genre popular. You climb the Spire, picking cards and relics as you go, and every run pushes you toward a different build. It's easy to learn, deep to master, and losing makes you want to start another run.

Players love

Watch out for

  • bad luck with cards and relics can end runs
  • frustrating, frequent early deaths
  • plain, unattractive art style

Best for: strategy fans who love building combos and adapting each run

Not for: players who hate losing runs to bad luck
