What players say

A dark-fantasy 2D metroidvania with souls-like combat. A silent girl fights through a ruined kingdom using the spirits of fallen knights as her attacks. Players describe it as melancholic and atmospheric, with a tragic story you piece together.

Not for: players who hate getting lost or need tight, snappy movement
