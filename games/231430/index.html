What players say

A tactical WWII real-time strategy game where small squads fight over cover, resources and territory on the Eastern and Western fronts. Units die fast, so positioning and quick reactions matter more than mass production.

Players love

  • tense, cover-based tactical combat
  • still one of the best RTS games to many
  • long-lived multiplayer and co-op with friends
  • custom maps and mods add replay value

How it feels

  • tense
  • gritty
  • tactical

Watch out for

  • factions and content locked behind paid DLC
  • weak AI in skirmishes and as allies
  • Soviet campaign criticized as clichéd and one-sided

Best for: RTS fans who like tactical squad play and matches with friends

Not for: players who want everything in the base game or a historically careful campaign
