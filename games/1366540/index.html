What players say

A 3D factory-automation game where you build production lines across planets and star systems, working toward a Dyson sphere around a star. Players find it beautiful, absorbing and a huge time sink.

Players love

  • beautiful planets and megastructures
  • deeply addictive factory building
  • multi-planet logistics on small round worlds
  • easier to learn than many factory games
  • ranked alongside Factorio and Satisfactory

What stands out

  • Addictive, one-more-run

How it feels

  • absorbing
  • cosmic
  • methodical

Watch out for

  • still in early access after years, slow updates
  • quality-of-life gaps and tiny UI
  • machine-translated text, missing languages
  • performance and loading problems for some

Best for: factory-game fans who love space and long, ever-growing builds

Not for: players who want a finished, fast-updating game or polished UI
