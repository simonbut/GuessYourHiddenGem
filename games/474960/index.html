What players say

A third-person shooter from Remedy where you fight with time powers, with live-action TV episodes between acts that change with your choices. Players describe it as a short, cinematic sci-fi story you half play and half watch.

Players love

  • inventive time-power combat
  • live-action episodes woven into the game
  • strong cast and performances
  • twisty time-travel story
  • good-looking visuals that still hold up

What stands out

  • Mystery & plot twists

How it feels

  • cinematic
  • twisty
  • intriguing

Watch out for

  • short campaign
  • stutter and frame drops on PC
  • episodes stream online and buffer
  • shooting feels bland to some

Best for: fans of Remedy's story-driven games like Control and Alan Wake

Not for: players who want a long, mechanically deep shooter
