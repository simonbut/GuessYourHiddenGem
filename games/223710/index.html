What players say

A free psychological survival horror game set in a dark Swedish city, built on the old Half-Life engine. You scavenge scarce supplies through a bleak story about depression, alone or in co-op.

Players love

  • oppressive, unforgettable atmosphere
  • haunting soundtrack and sound design
  • dark story that tackles depression
  • genuinely scary and completely free
  • co-op campaign to play with a friend

What stands out

  • Genuinely scary
  • Dark, mature themes
  • Emotional, tearjerker moments

How it feels

  • bleak
  • terrifying
  • melancholic

Watch out for

  • crashes and freezes on modern PCs
  • resolution settings that misalign or cut off the screen
  • confusing puzzles and frustrating jumping sections
  • few quality-of-life features and sparse saves

Best for: horror fans who can forgive old-engine roughness for real atmosphere and story

Not for: players who need polish and stability, or who are struggling with heavy themes
