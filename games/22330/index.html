What players say

The Game of the Year Edition of Bethesda's classic open-world fantasy RPG set in Cyrodiil. Players remember it for its guild questlines, its atmosphere and its goofy charm.

Players love

  • excellent guild and side quest writing
  • memorable atmosphere and soundtrack
  • huge world with endless quests
  • hilarious quirky NPC moments
  • deeper mechanics than Skyrim, like equipment decay

How it feels

  • whimsical

  • nostalgic
  • epic

Watch out for

  • crashes and launch problems on modern PCs
  • game-breaking bugs that need fan patches
  • no proper controller support
  • level-scaled enemies and dated mechanics

Best for: RPG fans who enjoy classic, quirky open worlds and don't mind patching

Not for: players who want a plug-and-play experience without tinkering
