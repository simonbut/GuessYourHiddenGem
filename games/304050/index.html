What players say

A free-to-play voxel MMO where you hop through portals into procedurally generated worlds, clear block-tower dungeons for loot, and build your own realms. Easy to pick up and addictive to grind, but the late game is a very long slog.

Players love

  • addictive loot grinding loop
  • easy to learn and jump into
  • always something to do or discover
  • still fun for players returning after years

What stands out

How it feels

Watch out for

  • invasive new kernel-level anti-cheat
  • duping and cheaters wrecking the economy
  • heavy monetization, marketplace now paywalled
  • endless late-game stat grind

Best for: players who like loot grinding in a blocky, Minecraft-like world

Not for: anyone wary of intrusive anti-cheat software or pay-to-skip monetization
