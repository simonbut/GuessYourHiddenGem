What players say

A free-to-play arena shooter that mixes Halo-style gunfights with Portal-style portals for flanks and mind games. Official servers have been shut down, so what is left is mostly custom games, bots and peer-to-peer matches.

Best for: former players or friends setting up custom matches for nostalgia

Not for: anyone looking for active online matchmaking
