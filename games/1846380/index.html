What players say

An arcade street racer that pairs realistic cars with cel-shaded, graffiti-style characters and effects. You enter weekly qualifiers, bet on races and escape the cops to keep your winnings.

Players love

  • bold graffiti and anime-style effects
  • deep visual and performance customization
  • large selection of cars
  • fun arcade driving
  • high-stakes risk-and-reward career

How it feels

  • stylish
  • flashy
  • intense

Watch out for

  • cringey dialogue and weak story
  • drift-heavy handling that feels odd
  • relentless, overtuned police chases
  • crashes and bugs that can cost races and money

Best for: arcade racing fans who love car customization and stylish presentation

Not for: players who want grounded handling or a strong story
