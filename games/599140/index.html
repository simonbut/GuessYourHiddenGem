What players say

A darkly funny pixel-art management and crafting game where you run a medieval graveyard: preparing bodies, farming, crafting and running errands for the locals. It's a slow, grindy climb that players say pays off if you are patient.

Players love

  • dark, unexpected sense of humor
  • satisfying unlocks and automation later on
  • lots of activities and content
  • morbid twist on Stardew-style routines
  • intertwined story and quests

What stands out

  • Genuinely funny
  • Addictive, one-more-run
  • Relaxing & cozy

How it feels

  • morbid
  • grindy
  • quirky

Watch out for

  • heavy grind and constant backtracking
  • poorly explained mechanics, wiki needed
  • progress walls and recipes locked behind unclear gates
  • typos, jank and old bugs that can softlock

Best for: patient players who like long crafting chains and Stardew-style routines with a morbid edge

Not for: players who want a relaxing, guided farming sim
