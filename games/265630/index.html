What players say

A free Wild West multiplayer shooter on the Source engine, with slow but punchy revolvers, rifles and throwables across deathmatch and team modes. It feels like a rowdy, nostalgic old-school shooter in cowboy boots.

Players love

  • one-of-a-kind cowboy setting
  • Old-school Source deathmatch feel
  • quirky loadouts with hatchets, bows and kicks
  • simple and easy to pick up
  • great for game nights with friends

How it feels

  • chaotic
  • funny
  • nostalgic
  • rowdy

Watch out for

  • dated, clunky controls and menus
  • occasional crashes and bugs
  • small player base, hard to find full servers

Best for: players who want a goofy, old-school Wild West deathmatch with friends

Not for: players who want modern polish or always-busy servers
