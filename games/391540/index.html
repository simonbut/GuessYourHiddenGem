What players say

A short pixel-art RPG where you can spare every monster instead of fighting, and bullet-dodging battles respond to your choices. Players call it funny, heartfelt and memorable, with routes that change based on how you play.

Players love

Not for: players looking for deep, mechanical RPG combat
