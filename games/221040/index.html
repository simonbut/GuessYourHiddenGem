What players say

A big, explosive, cinematic action game with several linked co-op campaigns. Players call it a bombastic shooter-brawler full of QTEs and set pieces rather than classic survival horror, and at its best with a friend.

Players love

  • great fun in co-op with a friend
  • fluid movement and melee combat
  • over-the-top, explosive set pieces
  • long story split across several campaigns

What stands out

  • Great with friends

How it feels

  • chaotic
  • bombastic
  • silly
  • frantic

Watch out for

  • Constant QTEs and scripted instant deaths
  • clunky controls and a cramped camera/FOV
  • messy, over-the-top story
  • confusing inventory system

Best for: co-op partners who want over-the-top action

Not for: classic survival-horror fans and solo players
