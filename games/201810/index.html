What players say

A linear, story-driven shooter set in an alternate 1960s where the Nazis won. You blast through set pieces with heavy guns or sneak past enemies, between surprisingly character-driven cutscenes.

Players love

  • punchy, satisfying gunplay
  • surprisingly compelling story and cast
  • striking alternate-history setting and art

How it feels

  • pulpy
  • grim
  • explosive

Watch out for

  • lots of long, unskippable cutscenes
  • uneven difficulty and bullet-sponge enemies
  • poor optimization and 60fps cap
  • story doesn't land for some players

Best for: fans of linear single-player shooters with a strong story

Not for: players who dislike long cutscenes or dated tech
