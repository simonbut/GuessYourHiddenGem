What players say

A hand-drawn psychological horror visual novel: you're sent to a cabin to slay a princess, and each choice loops you into a stranger, shifting version of the story. Players say it's best played blind and call it eerie, funny, tender and heartbreaking.

  • lots of reading and slow stretches

Best for: visual novel and choose-your-own-adventure fans who love replaying for new endings

Not for: players who dislike heavy reading or abstract, symbolic stories
