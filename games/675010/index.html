What players say

A slow, physics-heavy off-road sim where you drive trucks through deep Siberian mud to deliver logs. You fight the terrain inch by inch with a winch, a map and a lot of patience.

Players love

  • convincing mud and vehicle physics
  • runs well on modest PCs
  • hard but fair, rewarding challenge
  • satisfying slow, patient progress
  • mods and DLC add trucks and maps

What stands out

  • Great with friends

How it feels

  • slow
  • atmospheric
  • challenging
  • gritty

Watch out for

  • lots of getting stuck and winching
  • few missions beyond log hauling

Best for: patient players who enjoy overcoming tough terrain at a crawl

Not for: anyone expecting fast off-road racing
