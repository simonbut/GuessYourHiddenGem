What players say

A turn-based RPG set in a painterly, Belle Epoque-inspired fantasy world, where enemy attacks are dodged or parried in real time. Players praise the story, art and music; the timing-heavy fights divide opinion.

Players love

  • stunning, unique art direction
  • outstanding soundtrack
  • emotional story and lovable cast
  • parry and dodge twist wins over people who dislike turn-based combat

What stands out

  • Emotional, tearjerker moments
  • Mystery & plot twists

How it feels

  • emotional
  • melancholic
  • beautiful
  • tragic

Watch out for

  • combat feels like a rhythm game of memorized timing
  • dodge and parry inputs feel delayed or unresponsive to some
  • Unreal Engine flicker, dithering and shadow artifacts
  • linear, confusing levels with no map

Best for: players who want a heartfelt story and enjoy mastering timing-based defense

Not for: players who want slow, purely strategic turn-based combat
