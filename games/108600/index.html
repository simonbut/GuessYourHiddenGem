What players say

An isometric, highly detailed zombie apocalypse sim where you loot houses, barricade windows, farm, craft and eventually die in a lonely Kentucky. It's hard, permadeath-driven and very customizable, with a huge modding scene.

Players love

  • incredible depth and realism
  • every run becomes a unique death story
  • deeply customizable sandbox settings
  • massive, game-changing mod scene
  • great fun with friends

How it feels

  • bleak
  • tense
  • immersive
  • darkly funny

Watch out for

  • bugs and crashes, especially with mods
  • poor performance even on strong PCs
  • still in early access after many years
  • updates that break mods

Best for: players who love slow, punishing survival sims and tinkering with mods

Not for: those who want a polished, stable game or fast zombie action
