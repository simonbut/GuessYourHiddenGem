What players say

A pixel-art indie platform fighter in the Smash style where elemental fighters battle with up to four players locally or online. Players say the Steam Workshop turns it into a near-endless crossover roster.

Players love

  • huge Workshop roster of crossover characters
  • unique, well-designed base characters
  • fast Smash-style movement
  • runs well on low-end PCs
  • fun to play with friends locally

How it feels

  • silly
  • competitive
  • fast-paced

Watch out for

  • online matchmaking mostly empty
  • uneven, sometimes broken or overpowered mods

Best for: Smash fans on PC who want to mod in wild characters and play with friends

Not for: players who want a busy online ranked scene
