What players say

An open-world co-op shooter set in 1980s Sweden overrun by hostile machines. You scavenge, craft and sneak or fight past big robots, alone or with up to three friends, and early on it feels genuinely eerie.

Players love

  • menacing, well-designed robot enemies
  • excellent sound design
  • eerie 1980s Swedish countryside
  • genuinely scary when outmatched early on
  • best with friends in co-op

What stands out

  • Great with friends

How it feels

  • eerie
  • tense
  • nostalgic

Watch out for

  • repetitive fetch-and-shoot loop
  • bugs and multiplayer desync
  • weak story and grating voiced characters
  • too much locked behind DLC

Best for: co-op groups who enjoy hunting big robots in an eerie open world

Not for: players who need a strong story or varied missions
