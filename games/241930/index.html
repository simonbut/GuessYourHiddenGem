What players say

An open-world action game set in Mordor where you hunt orc captains with free-flowing, Arkham-style combat and stealth. Its Nemesis system makes orcs remember you, rise in rank and come back for revenge.

Players love

  • Nemesis system creates memorable rivals
  • Flowing Arkham-style combat
  • still looks good and runs on modest PCs
  • fun orc-slaying power fantasy

How it feels

  • brutal
  • epic
  • dark

Watch out for

  • repetitive mission and collectible loop
  • weak final bosses heavy on QTEs
  • combat can feel one-button and shallow
  • Captains and enemies pile into fights

Best for: players who loved Arkham combat and want emergent orc rivalries

Not for: players who want deep, challenging combat or a strong story
