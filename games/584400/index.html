What players say

A brand-new 2D Sonic adventure in classic Genesis style, mixing remixed old zones with new ones. It feels like a lovingly made 90s sequel, all momentum, bright pixel art and catchy music.

Players love

  • incredible soundtrack
  • detailed, vibrant sprite work
  • satisfying speed and momentum physics
  • true return to classic Sonic
  • Mighty, Ray and Encore mode

How it feels

  • nostalgic
  • fast
  • colorful
  • upbeat

Watch out for

  • frustrating Blue Sphere and special stages
  • many zones recycled from older games
  • difficulty spikes in some zones and bosses

Best for: fans of Genesis-era Sonic and fast pixel-art platformers

Not for: players frustrated by trial-and-error retro platforming
