What players say

A harsh multiplayer survival game where you gather, build a base and fight other players who can raid everything you own. Servers wipe regularly, and players describe it as thrilling, addictive and bad for your free time.

Players love

  • exciting, high-stakes fights and raids
  • unforgettable stories with a group
  • satisfying loot-and-build loop
  • addictive and hard to quit

How it feels

  • brutal
  • tense
  • chaotic

Watch out for

  • cheaters on many servers
  • extremely toxic players and chat
  • heavy time sink, offline raids
  • poor optimization, lag and crashes

Best for: groups with lots of free time who enjoy high-stakes PvP

Not for: solo or casual players who hate losing everything
