What players say

A turn-based historical 4X where you guide a civilization from ancient to modern times, picking a new culture each era. It plays like a fresh, more flexible take on Civ, with tactical map battles and a strong one-more-turn pull.

Players love

How it feels

Watch out for

  • battles and sieges drag on
  • endgame drags
  • confusing, poorly explained start

Best for: Civ players who want a different twist on 4X and enjoy mixing cultures

Not for: players who want a polished endgame and a fair AI
