What players say

A grand strategy game set from 1836 to 1936, focused on running an economy and keeping political factions happy. You build industry, pass laws and steer society, with war mostly happening on its own.

Players love

  • deep economic and social simulation
  • great for alternate-history sandboxes
  • much improved since launch
  • rewarding once you learn it

How it feels

  • cerebral
  • sprawling
  • methodical

Watch out for

  • expensive DLC is needed for the full experience
  • warfare is automated and shallow
  • bugs and game-breaking issues
  • campaigns can feel samey with little flavor

Best for: players who love economics and building a nation from the inside

Not for: players who want hands-on military campaigns or dislike paying for many DLCs
