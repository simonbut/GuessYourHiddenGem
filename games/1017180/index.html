What players say

A janky, randomly generated road trip through a semi-post-apocalyptic wasteland. You keep a fragile car running by managing fuel, oil and parts, scavenge as you go, and laugh at the physics chaos, especially with friends.

Players love

  • hilarious chaos in multiplayer
  • endless road with no borders
  • hands-on car upkeep and refueling
  • goofy physics and bugs that add charm
  • weird, oddly immersive vibe

How it feels

  • absurd
  • funny
  • laid-back
  • chaotic

Watch out for

  • no updates for years, feels abandoned
  • dismissive developer communication
  • poor optimization, especially in multiplayer
  • thin content that gets old fast

Best for: friend groups who want a silly, unpredictable sandbox drive, ideally bought on sale

Not for: anyone who wants a polished, actively updated game
