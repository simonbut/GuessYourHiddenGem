What players say

Kratos and Atreus journey across the Nine Realms before Ragnarok in this cinematic action-adventure sequel to God of War (2018). Melee combat alternates with long story beats, exploration and puzzles, and players are split on the pacing.

Players love

  • moving Kratos-Atreus story
  • combat deepened by weapon switching
  • stunning visuals and realms
  • superb voice acting and cinematics
  • rewarding side quests and exploration

How it feels

  • epic
  • emotional
  • cinematic
  • heartfelt

Watch out for

  • too many puzzle breaks
  • slow Atreus sections and walk-and-talk stretches
  • combat can feel bloated and unfair in crowds

Best for: players who loved God of War (2018) and want a big cinematic continuation

Not for: players who want nonstop action without puzzles or slow story segments
