What players say

A classic party-based fantasy RPG with deep turn-based combat, two custom heroes, and a big world of quests, playable solo or in two-player co-op. Rewarding but often cryptic.

Players love

  • freedom to build your party

  • holds up well for its age

How it feels

  • tactical

Watch out for

  • unclear quests with no markers
  • obscure puzzles and hidden mechanics
  • main story feels weak or unfocused

Best for: CRPG fans who love planning turn-based fights, especially with a co-op partner

Not for: players who want clear quest guidance and a gripping story
