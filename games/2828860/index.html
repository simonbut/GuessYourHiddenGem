What players say

An early-access PvE extraction shooter where your squad scavenges loot beneath colossal war machines in a dystopian forever-war. Feels like being a tiny, outgunned scavenger picking through someone else's battlefield.

Players love

  • tense, high-stakes loot-and-extract runs

What stands out

How it feels

  • eerie

Watch out for

  • bugs and clunky, stiff movement
  • stunlocks and sudden unfair deaths
  • poor onboarding and steep learning curve
  • dumb, easily stuck enemy AI

Best for: patient players who love bleak worldbuilding and don't mind early-access roughness

Not for: players wanting a polished, fair-feeling shooter
