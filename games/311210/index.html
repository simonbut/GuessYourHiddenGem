What players say

A futuristic Call of Duty with wall-running multiplayer, a confusing story campaign and a much-loved Zombies mode. On PC today it lives mostly through Zombies and workshop custom maps.

Players love

  • one of the best Zombies modes in the series
  • iconic Zombies maps and Chronicles content
  • huge custom zombies workshop
  • smooth movement and gunplay

How it feels

  • chaotic
  • nostalgic
  • frantic

Watch out for

  • hackers and serious security risks online
  • still full price for a decade-old game
  • near-dead multiplayer and matchmaking
  • confusing campaign story

Best for: Zombies fans and players who enjoy custom community maps

Not for: anyone wanting a safe, populated competitive multiplayer scene
