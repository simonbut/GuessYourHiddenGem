What players say

A fantasy farming and life sim with magic, monsters and dragons, played solo or in co-op. It feels like Stardew Valley with much more to do: spells, combat, several farms and a big cast to befriend or romance.

Players love

  • far more content than Stardew Valley
  • magic spells for combat and farming
  • no energy bar, adjustable day length
  • large, varied romanceable cast
  • lots of character customization

What stands out

  • Lots of content / long
  • Relaxing & cozy

How it feels

  • cozy
  • whimsical
  • relaxing

Watch out for

  • lingering bugs, some blocking quests
  • co-op feels tacked on and desyncs
  • tedious back-and-forth quest travel
  • rough non-English translations

Best for: Stardew fans who want more magic, combat and content to sink hundreds of hours into

Not for: co-op groups or players who want a polished, bug-free farming sim
