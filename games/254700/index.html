What players say

The classic over-the-shoulder survival action game where Leon fights through a hostile Spanish village, a castle and beyond to rescue the president's daughter. It's tense, campy and still a blast, though its controls and PC port show their age.

Players love

  • iconic, memorable village section
  • addictive, nonstop action
  • campy dialogue and charming Leon
  • still holds up 20 years later
  • fan mods that greatly improve the PC version

How it feels

  • tense
  • campy
  • action-packed

Watch out for

  • stiff, dated tank-style controls
  • weak PC port without fan mods
  • launch and display issues on modern PCs

Best for: players curious about an all-time classic who don't mind old-school controls

Not for: anyone who wants modern movement or a polished port out of the box
