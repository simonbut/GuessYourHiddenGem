What players say

A classic turn-based 4X strategy game: grow a civilization from one city to a world power through science, culture, diplomacy or war on a hex map. Players describe it as the 'one more turn' game that eats whole nights.

Players love

  • highly addictive 'one more turn' loop
  • many call it the best game in the series
  • clean, easy-to-learn systems with depth
  • endless replay value
  • runs on older PCs, Macs and Steam Deck

Watch out for

  • AI cheats and handles war and diplomacy poorly
  • crashes and launcher issues on newer systems

Best for: strategy fans who want a deep, replayable 4X many consider the best in the series

Not for: players who want smart AI opponents or a trouble-free launch on new systems
