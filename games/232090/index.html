What players say

A wave-based co-op horde shooter where up to six players pick perks and weapons and gun down gory mutant Zeds to heavy metal. Players describe it as chaotic, simple and very replayable.

Players love

  • satisfying, punchy gunplay and gore
  • huge weapon roster across many perk classes
  • addictive wave-survival loop
  • great with friends, still solid solo
  • cheap on sale

What stands out

  • Great with friends

How it feels

  • chaotic
  • gory
  • intense

Watch out for

  • massive ~100GB install for a simple game
  • black screens, crashes and launch problems
  • hard to find or host matches now
  • progression and aim bugs left unfixed

Best for: co-op groups who want quick, chaotic horde-blasting sessions

Not for: players short on disk space or after a story campaign
