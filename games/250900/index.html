What players say

A grim, darkly funny top-down roguelike shooter where you descend through randomly generated rooms and stack bizarre items that transform Isaac. Every run plays differently, and players report it being extremely hard to put down.

Players love

  • huge item pool with wild synergies
  • every run feels different
  • hundreds of hours of unlocks, achievements and secrets
  • extremely addictive

How it feels

  • dark
  • addictive
  • chaotic
  • funny

Watch out for

  • steep difficulty, especially some challenges
  • a lot of content, including co-op, sits behind paid DLC
  • lag and crashes in heavy late runs, with mods or online

Best for: roguelike fans who love chasing broken builds and long-term unlocks

Not for: players put off by gross-out humor, disturbing religious themes or punishing difficulty
