What players say

A huge LEGO adventure covering all nine Skywalker saga films, with explorable planets, space battles and hundreds of characters. It's packed and charming, but many prefer the older games' longer levels.

Best for: Star Wars and LEGO fans who enjoy relaxed exploration and collecting

Not for: fans wanting the tight, level-focused feel of The Complete Saga
