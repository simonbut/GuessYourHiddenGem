What players say

Kojima's slow, strange open-world delivery game. You haul cargo across a broken America, plan routes over rough terrain and build roads with other players, all wrapped in a long, cinematic sci-fi story.

Players love

  • meditative, rewarding traversal and route planning
  • beautiful landscapes and perfectly timed music
  • emotional, unique story
  • Asynchronous help from other players' structures

What stands out

  • Mystery & plot twists
  • Emotional, tearjerker moments

How it feels

  • melancholic
  • meditative
  • strange
  • lonely

Watch out for

  • very heavy on cutscenes and exposition
  • slow, repetitive walking for some
  • confusing jargon and story early on
  • clunky combat sections

Best for: patient players who enjoy calm journeys and strange, cinematic stories

Not for: anyone who wants constant action or little story
