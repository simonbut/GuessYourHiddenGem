What players say

A 2014 driving simulator with realistic physics and force feedback, kept alive by an enormous modding scene. With mods it covers everything from drifting and traffic servers to custom tracks and cars.

Players love

  • realistic physics and great force feedback
  • endless cars and tracks via mods
  • excellent in VR
  • runs well on low-end PCs
  • cheap on sale

How it feels

  • immersive
  • technical
  • sandbox

Watch out for

  • vanilla game feels limited
  • mods and Content Manager setup is fiddly
  • online play mostly relies on mods

Best for: sim racers with a wheel who enjoy tinkering with mods

Not for: players wanting a polished, plug-and-play racer
