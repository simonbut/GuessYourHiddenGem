What players say

A fast 2D action roguelite with metroidvania structure: you fight through a shifting castle, die, unlock new weapons and try again. Players say runs feel quick and fluid once you learn to play aggressively.

Players love

  • crisp, fluid combat and movement
  • huge variety of weapons and builds
  • smooth animations and art style
  • great soundtrack
  • addictive run-based loop

What stands out

  • Addictive, one-more-run

How it feels

  • fast
  • intense
  • addictive
  • stylish

Watch out for

  • steep difficulty at higher levels
  • slow progression and tedious restarts

Best for: players who like fast, skill-based action and experimenting with builds

Not for: people who dislike repeating runs or roguelike permadeath
