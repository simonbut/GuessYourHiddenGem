What players say

A large-scale modern military shooter with big multiplayer battles, drivable tanks, jets and boats, destructible maps, and a short single-player campaign. Many players still call it the best Battlefield, and its multiplayer feels intense and nostalgic.

Players love

  • punchy, satisfying gunplay
  • large battles mixing infantry and vehicles
  • map destruction and Levolution events
  • graphics still hold up years later
  • seen by many fans as the best Battlefield

How it feels

  • intense
  • explosive
  • chaotic
  • nostalgic

Watch out for

  • buggy campaign, notably the swimming section
  • short campaign with weak writing
  • useless AI squadmates in the campaign
  • tough start for new players against veterans

Best for: players who want big vehicle-and-infantry battles and enjoy older Battlefield

Not for: newcomers buying mainly for the campaign or expecting a fair start against veterans
