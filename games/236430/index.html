What players say

FromSoftware's sequel to Dark Souls, a punishing old-school action RPG of tough enemies, bosses and bonfires. Players call it the weird, overhated middle child of the series: strong on builds, lore and atmosphere, rough on hitboxes and level design.

Players love

  • wide build variety
  • deep, engaging lore
  • great atmosphere and aesthetic
  • highly replayable
  • better than its bad reputation suggests

How it feels

  • challenging
  • atmospheric
  • frustrating
  • quirky

Watch out for

  • wonky hitboxes
  • clunky, delayed-feeling combat
  • forgettable, easy bosses
  • frustrating areas packed with enemy swarms

Best for: Souls fans who want the full series and love build experimentation

Not for: players expecting the tighter combat of Dark Souls 3 or Elden Ring
