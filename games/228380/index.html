What players say

A demolition derby racer with soft-body damage, where cars crumple, parts fly off and any race can turn into a pileup. Arcade-leaning but weighty, it mixes track races with derby arenas.

Players love

  • Crunchy, satisfying crash damage
  • chaotic, unpredictable races
  • Oddball vehicles like buses and harvesters
  • great for short sessions to unwind
  • Fitting rock soundtrack

How it feels

  • chaotic
  • funny
  • adrenaline-fueled

Watch out for

  • AI drivers ram you constantly
  • thin online servers, hard to join friends
  • some prefer the older FlatOut games

Best for: players who want messy, crash-heavy racing in short bursts

Not for: players who want clean races against AI or easy online sessions with friends
