What players say

A third-person cover shooter and looter RPG set in a snowed-in, post-pandemic Manhattan. Players praise its bleak but strangely cozy atmosphere and co-op, while many find enemies spongy and missions repetitive.

Players love

  • incredibly atmospheric snowy New York
  • satisfying guns and sound design
  • fun co-op with friends
  • still holds up a decade later

What stands out

  • Great with friends

How it feels

  • atmospheric
  • bleak
  • cozy

Watch out for

  • bullet-sponge enemies that hit hard
  • repetitive side missions you're pushed into
  • always-online even when playing solo
  • endgame needs a team and matchmaking is quieter

Best for: looter-shooter fans who love atmosphere and co-op

Not for: solo players who dislike grind and spongy enemies
