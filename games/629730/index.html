What players say

A VR medieval fantasy sandbox with physics-driven swords, bows and magic, where every swing and grab feels physical. Has a dungeon progression mode, but the sandbox and mods are the main draw.

Players love

Watch out for

  • performance drops and stuttering
  • bugs, soft locks and crashes with mods
  • gets stale without mods

Best for: VR owners who want physical sword fights and a deep mod library

Not for: players with weaker PCs or who want smart enemy AI
