What players say

A hardcore combat flight sim where you learn real cockpit procedures, from cold starts to firing weapons. The base game is free with two aircraft. Most other planes are paid modules.

Players love

  • extremely realistic flight models
  • highly detailed aircraft and cockpits
  • deep, rewarding learning curve

What stands out

  • Steep learning curve

How it feels

  • technical
  • immersive
  • demanding

Watch out for

  • expensive aircraft modules and paid training content
  • huge download for very little free content
  • overwhelming controls and tedious keybinding
  • basically needs a joystick and a strong PC

Best for: aviation fans with patience, a joystick and a budget for modules

Not for: anyone wanting a pick-up-and-play arcade flying game
