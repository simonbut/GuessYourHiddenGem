What players say

A dark, wordless 2.5D puzzle platformer from the makers of Limbo, where a boy is hunted through a sinister facility. Players praise its oppressive atmosphere, sound and visual storytelling, and its shocking ending, over a short run of a few hours.

Players love

  • unsettling, gripping atmosphere
  • excellent sound design
  • story told entirely through visuals, with no dialogue
  • striking art style
  • intuitive puzzles with no tutorials needed

What stands out

  • Dark, mature themes
  • Genuinely scary

How it feels

  • dark
  • unsettling
  • mysterious
  • tense

Watch out for

  • very short with little replay value
  • trial-and-error deaths and precise timing
  • linear gameplay can feel boring

Best for: players who like atmospheric, short story-driven puzzle games and interpreting stories themselves

Not for: players wanting long playtime, clear explanations or deep gameplay
