What players say

A space sim with a 1:1 scale Milky Way where you fly your own ship to trade, mine, fight, hunt bounties or explore unknown star systems. It's very immersive, but complex and built around a long grind.

Players love

  • unmatched sense of scale and immersion
  • thrill of discovering new worlds
  • great with HOTAS and VR
  • helpful, friendly player community
  • freedom to trade, mine, fight or explore

What stands out

  • Steep learning curve

How it feels

  • vast
  • immersive
  • relaxing

Watch out for

  • brutal learning curve, weak tutorials
  • heavy grind for credits and engineering
  • confusing menus and reliance on third-party sites
  • pricey cosmetics and paid shortcuts

Best for: patient space fans who enjoy learning complex systems and self-directed play

Not for: players who want quick, guided action or hate grinding
