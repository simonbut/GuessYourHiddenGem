What players say

A free-to-start racing sim with realistic physics, DTM and WTCC touring cars, and the Nordschleife. Players say recent force feedback and VR updates made it one of the best sims around, though some can't get it to launch or start a race.

Players love

  • much better force feedback after recent updates
  • Touring cars and DTM, not just GT3
  • free base game, pay only for content you want
  • excellent in VR
  • good online and ranked racing

How it feels

  • immersive
  • competitive
  • realistic

Watch out for

  • freezes, crashes or races that won't start
  • fiddly wheel and triple-screen setup
  • best cars and tracks cost extra

Best for: sim racers with a wheel or VR headset who are tired of the usual GT3 grid

Not for: anyone who wants the full car and track list without buying content
