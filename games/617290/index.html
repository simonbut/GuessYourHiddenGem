What players say

A third-person shooter with Souls-like bosses, dodging and character builds, played alone or with up to two friends across randomly generated worlds. Many describe it as Dark Souls with guns.

Players love

  • satisfying gunplay
  • tough, memorable boss fights
  • great fun in co-op
  • randomized worlds encourage replays
  • weapons and mods to experiment with

What stands out

How it feels

  • tense
  • gritty
  • challenging

Watch out for

  • enemies spawn endlessly and swarm you
  • solo play feels poorly balanced
  • repetitive areas and reused enemies
  • melee feels weak

Best for: friends who want a challenging co-op shooter with boss fights

Not for: players who hate being swarmed by endless enemies or want a strong story
