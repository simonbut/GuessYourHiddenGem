What players say

A huge, realistic military sandbox with big maps and many weapons and vehicles. Players say it shines in multiplayer with organized groups and mods, where operations play out slowly and tactically.

Players love

  • huge scale and immersive realism
  • much better with mods
  • many modes on community servers, like KotH and Antistasi
  • memorable teamwork with friends

What stands out

  • Steep learning curve

How it feels

  • tactical
  • immersive
  • janky

Watch out for

  • clunky controls and steep learning curve
  • bad or unfairly accurate AI
  • bugs, crashes and poor performance
  • official servers dead; best play needs a group or unit

Best for: players who want slow, serious military sim with a community and mods

Not for: players wanting quick, polished solo shooter action
