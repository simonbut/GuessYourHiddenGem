What players say

The original Flash version of the twin-stick roguelike where Isaac fights through his mother's basement with random items. It is brutally hard and addictive, but most players say Rebirth replaces it.

Players love

How it feels

  • dark

Watch out for

  • locked to 30 FPS, poor performance
  • forced 4:3 aspect ratio
  • stingy, unbalanced item drops
  • Rebirth makes it obsolete

Best for: Isaac fans who want to experience where the series began

Not for: newcomers, who are better served by Rebirth
