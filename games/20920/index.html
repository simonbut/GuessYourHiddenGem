What players say

A dark, choice-driven fantasy RPG where Geralt gets tangled in political conspiracies, and a key decision splits the story into very different paths. The writing still holds up. The combat and the PC port show their age.

Players love

  • excellent story, writing and characters
  • choices that branch the story
  • high replay value with two paths
  • still-impressive visuals for its age
  • great soundtrack

What stands out

  • Multiple endings & choices

How it feels

  • dark
  • political
  • gritty
  • immersive

Watch out for

  • clunky, sluggish combat
  • crashes and launch problems on modern PCs
  • awkward controls and save system
  • tough early fights even on easy

Best for: Witcher fans who want the full story and care more about plot than combat

Not for: players spoiled by Witcher 3's smooth gameplay or anyone without patience for technical fixes
