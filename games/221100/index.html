What players say

A brutal open-world zombie survival sandbox where you scavenge a huge post-Soviet countryside for food, gear and guns, and other players are a bigger threat than the infected. Any life can end in seconds, which keeps every encounter tense.

Players love

  • heart-pounding, high-stakes player encounters
  • detailed survival systems like disease, hunger and thirst
  • every life turns into its own story
  • community servers for RP, PvE and modded play
  • great with a group of friends

How it feels

  • tense
  • harsh
  • unpredictable
  • paranoid

Watch out for

  • lots of cheaters on official servers
  • BattlEye kicks and server connection failures
  • no tutorial and a punishing learning curve
  • dated, buggy engine and crashes

Best for: patient players who love hardcore survival, betrayal stories and unpredictable PvP

Not for: anyone who hates losing hours of loot to one bullet or wants a guided experience
