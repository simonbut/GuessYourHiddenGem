What players say

A first-person stealth-action immersive sim where you play Emily or Corvo, sneaking or fighting through large, intricate levels with supernatural powers. Your level of violence changes the outcome.

Players love

  • brilliant level design like the Clockwork Mansion
  • freedom to play stealthy or violent
  • striking art direction
  • high replay value with two characters
  • detailed world full of secrets and lore

How it feels

  • atmospheric
  • sneaky
  • dark
  • inventive

Watch out for

  • story and characters weaker than the first game
  • a voiced Corvo who comments on everything
  • crashes or launch problems on some PCs

Best for: stealth and immersive sim fans who love experimenting with tools and replaying levels

Not for: players looking for a deep, gripping story
