What players say

Capcom's latest Street Fighter: a polished 2D fighter with Modern and Classic controls, an RPG-style World Tour story mode, the Battle Hub and the Fighting Ground. Players call it fun and deep but often frustrating.

Players love

  • welcoming to fighting game newcomers
  • fun, polished core fighting
  • surprisingly good World Tour mode
  • deep, rewarding to learn and master

What stands out

  • Competitive / ranked play

How it feels

  • competitive
  • intense

Watch out for

  • balance swings, with dominant characters each patch
  • Drive Rush, throw loops and Modern controls frustrate veterans
  • slow trickle of new content and stages
  • Expensive DLC characters and microtransactions

Best for: newcomers and veterans who want a deep, polished competitive fighter

Not for: players who dislike aggressive, mix-up-heavy play or paying for DLC fighters
