What players say

A grand strategy game where you play a medieval dynasty, not a nation, through marriages, plots, wars and inheritance across generations. It turns into a chaotic family saga full of strange stories.

Players love

  • endless emergent family drama
  • deep, interlocking systems
  • many ways to play
  • hugely addictive, hundreds of hours
  • great mod support

How it feels

  • scheming
  • chaotic
  • funny
  • epic

Watch out for

  • huge, expensive DLC catalog
  • balance now seems to assume you own DLC
  • bugs pile up in long campaigns
  • steep learning curve and weak tutorial

Best for: players who enjoy roleplaying and scheming over long, story-generating campaigns

Not for: those unwilling to buy DLC or learn a menu-heavy game
