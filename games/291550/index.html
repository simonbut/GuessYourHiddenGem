What players say

A free-to-play platform fighter in the style of Smash Bros for up to 8 players, with over sixty Legends and many weapons. It's easy to pick up and surprisingly deep, and players say it makes them rage as much as it hooks them.

Watch out for

  • signature-move spammers in lower ranks
  • very sweaty, experienced playerbase

Best for: fans of Smash-style brawlers who want a free game to play with friends

Not for: anyone who tilts easily or wants a relaxing casual game
