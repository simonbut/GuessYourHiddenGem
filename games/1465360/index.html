What players say

An off-road trucking sim where you haul cargo through deep mud, snow and rivers across big open maps, alone or with up to three friends. It is slow and methodical: every delivery is a small puzzle of winches, tires and route planning.

Players love

  • convincing mud and terrain physics
  • big variety of trucks and upgrades
  • relaxing, slow-paced hauling
  • great fun in co-op
  • beautiful, detailed maps

What stands out

  • Great with friends
  • Relaxing & cozy

How it feels

  • patient
  • relaxing
  • frustrating
  • rewarding

Watch out for

  • rough, punishing first hours
  • frequent flips and getting stuck
  • long, repetitive delivery contracts
  • many trucks and upgrades locked behind DLC

Best for: patient players who enjoy logistics, slow problem-solving and chilled co-op sessions

Not for: anyone who wants fast driving or gets frustrated by constant setbacks
