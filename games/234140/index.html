What players say

An open-world post-apocalyptic action game where you build up a war car, raid enemy camps and brawl through a dusty, hostile wasteland. Players describe it as Arkham-style fistfights mixed with Far Cry-style map clearing.

Players love

  • weighty, satisfying vehicular combat
  • bleak, gritty wasteland atmosphere
  • heavy Arkham-style melee brawling
  • upgrading and customizing your car
  • great value on sale

How it feels

  • gritty
  • brutal
  • desolate

Watch out for

  • repetitive camps and map activities
  • bland story with a rushed ending
  • PC crashes, broken saves and launch bugs

Best for: players who love car combat and soaking in a harsh post-apocalyptic world

Not for: players who quickly tire of checklist-style open worlds
