What players say

A World War II shooter with a cinematic campaign following a squad from D-Day through Europe, plus multiplayer and Zombies. The campaign plays like an interactive war movie, with a grounded soldier's-eye feel.

Players love

  • immersive, emotional campaign
  • stunning graphics and cutscenes
  • Saving Private Ryan-style boots-on-the-ground feel
  • punchy, satisfying gunplay

How it feels

  • cinematic
  • gritty
  • intense
  • emotional

Watch out for

  • QTEs too fast unless you lock the frame rate
  • clunky tank and plane sections
  • short campaign
  • overpriced at full price

Best for: players who want a cinematic, story-driven WWII campaign, ideally on sale

Not for: players who dislike scripted set pieces and quick-time events, or want a long campaign
