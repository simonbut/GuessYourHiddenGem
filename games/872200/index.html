What players say

A free third-person hero shooter where agents with gadgets and abilities fight in 4v4 and 6v6 objective modes. Matches feel fast and snappy, but updates have stopped and the game is nearly empty now.

Players love

  • fast, satisfying third-person gunplay
  • fun agents with distinct abilities
  • very easy to run, well optimized
  • great with a premade squad

What stands out

  • Competitive / ranked play

How it feels

  • fast
  • competitive
  • tactical

Watch out for

  • abandoned, no updates in years
  • tiny player base, mostly only Strikeout
  • rubber-banding, desync and connection issues
  • veteran-heavy lobbies punish newcomers

Best for: friend groups wanting a free, lightweight team shooter to play together

Not for: players looking for an active, supported competitive scene or ranked queues
