What players say

A cinematic, story-driven stealth game in which Agent 47 goes rogue. It is more linear and dramatic than other Hitman entries, with polished presentation and a gritty, sleazy tone.

Players love

Watch out for

  • disguise system that barely works

Best for: players who want a story-heavy stealth action thriller

Not for: fans who want the open sandbox assassinations of other Hitman games
