What players say

A cinematic sci-fi story game following three androids in near-future Detroit. You make choices, often under time pressure, and the story branches heavily, with main characters able to die for good.

Players love

  • choices that truly reshape the story
  • memorable characters, especially Connor and Hank
  • many endings worth replaying
  • movie-like graphics and music
  • emotionally gripping

What stands out

  • Multiple endings & choices

How it feels

  • emotional
  • tense
  • cinematic

Watch out for

  • PC performance issues, crashes and a 60 fps cap
  • replaying branches is slow with no scene skipping

Best for: players who love interactive stories and moral decisions

Not for: players who want gameplay beyond quick-time events and choices
