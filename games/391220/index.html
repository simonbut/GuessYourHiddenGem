What players say

Lara Croft's sequel to the 2013 reboot, sending her across snowy Siberia after a legend of immortality. It mixes climbing, stealthy bow combat, crafting and optional tombs in a cinematic adventure.

Players love

  • stunning snowy environments that still hold up
  • fun optional tombs and puzzles
  • satisfying bow and stealth combat
  • big improvement over the 2013 game
  • smooth climbing and traversal

How it feels

  • adventurous
  • cinematic
  • atmospheric
  • immersive

Watch out for

  • weak plot and forgettable characters for some
  • cutscene-heavy stretches can get boring
  • occasional bugs and glitches

Best for: players who enjoyed Tomb Raider 2013 and want a bigger, more polished sequel

Not for: players who want a strong, original story
