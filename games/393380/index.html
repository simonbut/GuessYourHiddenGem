What players say

A large-scale tactical military shooter with 100-player battles, vehicles and base building, where victory depends on voice comms and squads working together.

Players love

  • real teamwork and squad communication
  • tense, immersive sound and atmosphere
  • sweet spot between arcade shooter and milsim
  • chaotic, funny moments with teammates
  • great with organized friends or a clan

How it feels

Watch out for

  • poor optimization and long-standing bugs
  • steep learning curve, rough on new players
  • quality depends heavily on your server and squad
  • toxic players in some lobbies

Best for: players who enjoy slow, coordinated battles and talking on voice chat

Not for: solo players who want fast action without communicating
