What players say

A 2D sandbox where you dig, build, craft gear and fight a long ladder of bosses and events across melee, ranged, magic and summoner playstyles. Packed with content and easy to sink hundreds of hours into.

Players love

  • huge replay value and mod support

How it feels

  • adventurous

Watch out for

  • confusing start with little tutorial; you'll need the wiki
  • rough early game

Best for: players who love progression, boss fights and building with friends

Not for: players who want clear guidance and hate looking things up
