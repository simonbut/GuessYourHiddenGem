What players say

A top-down bullet hell roguelike where you dodge roll and flip tables through floors packed with bullet-themed enemies and bosses. Runs are hard and chaotic, and the huge pile of strange guns and secrets keeps players coming back.

Players love

  • enormous variety of weird guns
  • tight shooting and dodge-roll combat
  • tons of secrets and unlockables
  • hundreds of hours of replay
  • very hard but rewarding once it clicks

How it feels

  • chaotic
  • tense
  • addictive

Watch out for

  • punishing difficulty that can feel unfair
  • runs depend heavily on random drops
  • grindy unlocks and progression

Best for: players who love tough twin-stick shooting and learning through many failed runs

Not for: players who want control over their build or dislike heavy randomness
