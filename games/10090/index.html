What players say

A dark, brutal WWII shooter with a short campaign that alternates between the Soviet push on Berlin and the US Marines in the Pacific. It also has the original round-based Nazi Zombies mode. Players describe it as grim, tense and relentless.

Players love

  • grim, brutal campaign atmosphere
  • standout Soviet campaign with Reznov
  • fun Nazi Zombies mode
  • custom zombies maps and mods
  • runs fine on low-end and modern PCs

How it feels

  • grim
  • intense
  • brutal
  • nostalgic

Watch out for

  • online needs CD keys that aren't available
  • endless enemy grenade spam on Veteran
  • short campaign for the asking price

Best for: players who want a gritty WWII campaign and classic round-based zombies

Not for: anyone buying it mainly for official online multiplayer
