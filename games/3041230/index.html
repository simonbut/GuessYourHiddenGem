What players say

An early-access pirate survival-crafting game: gather, build a base, fight tough souls-lite bosses and sail between islands, solo or in co-op. A gorgeous, promising start that some feel has stalled.

Players love

  • excellent, intuitive base building
  • beautiful islands and art

  • rewarding exploration

How it feels

  • adventurous
  • grindy
  • scenic

Watch out for

  • months without meaningful content updates
  • co-op mechanics feel awkward for groups
  • clunky melee and naval combat
  • loop turns repetitive after the first hours

Best for: fans of Valheim-style survival crafting who want a pirate setting

Not for: players who want a finished game or polished ship combat now
