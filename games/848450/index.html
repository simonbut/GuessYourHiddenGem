What players say

A standalone follow-up to Subnautica set on a frozen region of the same alien planet. You explore underwater and icy land biomes, gather resources, build bases and follow a more story-driven plot with a voiced lead. Players find it beautiful but smaller and less scary than the first game.

Players love

  • same addictive explore-and-craft loop

  • icy

Watch out for

  • shorter with a smaller map than the original
  • much less scary and mysterious
  • tedious land sections and Seatruck
  • protagonist talks too much

Best for: fans of the first Subnautica who want more of its exploration in a new setting

Not for: players hoping for the isolation and horror of the original
