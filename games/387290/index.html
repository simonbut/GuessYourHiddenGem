What players say

A hand-painted 2D platformer metroidvania where you guide a little forest spirit through a beautiful but deadly world. It looks and sounds gentle, but plays as a demanding, trial-and-error platformer.

Players love

  • gorgeous painted art
  • outstanding orchestral soundtrack
  • emotional, tear-jerking story
  • satisfying new abilities to unlock
  • varied areas

What stands out

  • Emotional, tearjerker moments
  • Challenging
  • Wholesome & heartwarming

How it feels

  • beautiful
  • emotional
  • challenging
  • melancholic

Watch out for

  • punishing one-hit hazards and chase sequences
  • save system you must manage yourself
  • weak, shallow combat
  • busy visuals hide hazards; stutter for some

Best for: platformer fans who enjoy hard jumps wrapped in a touching story

Not for: players with little patience for repeated deaths
