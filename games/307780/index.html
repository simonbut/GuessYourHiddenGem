What players say

A fast, brutal 2D fighter with the series' trademark gore, several fighting styles per character and a story mode. Players say it shines most with a friend on the couch, and many call it the best modern Mortal Kombat.

Players love

  • great roster and guest fighters like Jason and Predator
  • gory, over-the-top fatalities
  • fun local versus with friends
  • runs well on modest PCs
