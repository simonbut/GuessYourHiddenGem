What players say

A gritty, grounded open-world crime story following Niko Bellic, an immigrant chasing a fresh start in a bleak Liberty City, bundled with two story expansions. Players love the story but report a rough PC port.

Players love

  • Niko is a standout protagonist
  • gripping story, often called the best in the series
  • atmospheric Liberty City with lots to do
  • excellent DLC episodes, especially Gay Tony
  • driving feels great once you get used to it

How it feels

  • gritty
  • melancholic
  • grounded
  • bleak

Watch out for

  • unstable PC port that crashes or loads forever
  • mods like FusionFix almost required
  • final mission helicopter bug blocks some players
  • Rockstar launcher and account verification hassles

Best for: players who want a serious, story-first GTA and don't mind tinkering with mods

Not for: anyone who wants a polished PC experience straight out of the box
