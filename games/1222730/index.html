What players say

A first-person Star Wars starfighter game with a story campaign and multiplayer, flown entirely from the cockpit of X-wings and TIE fighters. At its best it feels like sitting inside the films, especially in VR.

Players love

  • convincing X-wing and TIE cockpits
  • great in VR when it works
  • fast, satisfying dogfights
  • rich visuals and sound design
  • short but cinematic campaign

How it feels

  • immersive
  • intense
  • cinematic

Watch out for

  • often won't launch on modern CPUs without workarounds
  • EA two-factor login on every launch
  • multiplayer is mostly empty
  • VR mode hard to get running

Best for: Star Wars fans with a VR headset or flight stick who want a cockpit campaign

Not for: players wanting busy multiplayer, a deep space sim, or a hassle-free launch
