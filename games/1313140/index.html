What players say

A cute but dark mix of action roguelite and cult management. You run combat crusades through dungeons, then go home to feed, preach to and sometimes sacrifice your woodland followers.

Players love

  • charming, cute-but-dark art style
  • addictive loop of combat and base building
  • dark humor and silly follower moments
  • great music
  • engaging story

What stands out

  • Addictive, one-more-run

How it feels

  • cute
  • dark
  • funny
  • cozy

Watch out for

  • base upkeep turns tedious later on
  • both halves feel shallow to some

Best for: players who want a cozy management sim with a dark sense of humor

Not for: roguelite veterans after deep, demanding combat
