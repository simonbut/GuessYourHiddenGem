What players say

A prequel in the Arkham series following a younger Batman through a snowy Christmas Eve Gotham as assassins hunt him. It plays much like Arkham City, with freeflow combat, gadgets and detective work, and is known for its boss fights.

Players love

  • best boss fights in the series
  • cinematic story and Joker scenes
  • moody snowy Christmas Gotham
  • Solid Arkham City-style combat
  • strong voice acting for Batman

How it feels

  • dark
  • cinematic
  • wintry

Watch out for

  • Progress-blocking bugs and soft-locks
  • feels like a recycled Arkham City
  • Clunkier combat and counter glitches

Best for: Arkham fans who want more of City's gameplay with strong bosses and story

Not for: players with low tolerance for old, unfixed bugs
