What players say

A huge story-rich CRPG from Owlcat where you lead a Rogue Trader dynasty in the Warhammer 40K universe, commanding a crew and making choices that shape the story. Deep character builds and long turn-based battles.

Players love

  • faithful, rich 40K atmosphere
  • deep build variety and talent choices
  • fun, crunchy turn-based combat
  • choices with real consequences
  • strong writing and characters

How it feels

  • epic
  • grim
  • strategic

Watch out for

  • overwhelming systems at first
  • some acts drag and overstay
  • too many or poorly designed fights

Best for: CRPG fans who love deep buildcrafting and grim space opera, 40K fans or not

Not for: players who want a short, streamlined RPG or dislike number-heavy systems
