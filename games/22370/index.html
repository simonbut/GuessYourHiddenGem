What players say

A classic open-world post-apocalyptic RPG set in the ruins of Washington D.C., full of side quests, exploration and DLC stories. Many players love it, but the Steam version is often hard to get running.

Players love

Watch out for

  • needs manual fixes or mods to run
  • dated combat and visuals

Best for: RPG fans who love exploring a grim open world and don't mind modding to make it work

Not for: anyone who wants a plug-and-play game on modern Windows
