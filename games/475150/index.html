What players say

A remaster bundling Titan Quest and Immortal Throne: a classic Diablo-style isometric action RPG through Greek, Egyptian and Chinese myth. It feels comfy and old-school, built around mixing two masteries and chasing loot.

Players love

  • flexible dual-mastery class builds
  • Greek, Egyptian and Chinese mythology setting
  • addictive loot hunting
  • comfy, nostalgic old-school feel
  • runs well even on weak hardware

How it feels

  • nostalgic
  • relaxed
  • adventurous

Watch out for

  • slow start and dragging pacing
  • occasional crashes and bugs
  • finicky click-to-attack combat
  • simple loop that some find repetitive

Best for: Diablo 2 and Grim Dawn fans who love mythology and experimenting with hybrid builds

Not for: players who want fast, modern-feeling combat or a quick start
