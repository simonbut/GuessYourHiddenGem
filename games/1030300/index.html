What players say

The sequel to Hollow Knight: a vast, haunted 2D metroidvania where you play the agile Hornet and climb through the kingdom of Pharloom. Movement is fast and fluid, the world is beautiful, and players find the difficulty brutal.

Players love

  • fluid, acrobatic movement and combat
  • stunning art and atmosphere
  • rich world and lore to uncover
  • memorable, demanding boss fights
  • huge amount of content

What stands out

  • Challenging
  • Lots of content / long
  • Punishing, rage-inducing

How it feels

  • atmospheric
  • intense
  • challenging
  • rewarding

Watch out for

  • much harsher difficulty than Hollow Knight
  • enemies often deal two masks of damage
  • long, punishing runbacks to bosses
  • frustrating flying enemies and cheap hits

Best for: players who loved Hollow Knight and want an even tougher challenge

Not for: players who dislike punishing difficulty or repeated runbacks after deaths
