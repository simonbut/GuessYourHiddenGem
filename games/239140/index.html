What players say

A first-person zombie action game in an open city where parkour is the star: you run across rooftops, grapple and dropkick the infected by day, then flee terrifying Volatiles at night. Players say co-op with friends makes it even better.

Players love

  • fluid, satisfying rooftop parkour
  • terrifying night chases with Volatiles
  • great atmosphere that still holds up
  • fun co-op with friends
  • dropkicks and gory, goofy zombie mayhem

How it feels

  • tense
  • thrilling
  • gory
  • playful

Watch out for

  • clunky melee combat with tanky enemies
  • weapons break or get outclassed fast
  • slow, repetitive early hours

Best for: players who want fast movement, zombie horror and a co-op sandbox to goof around in

Not for: anyone who wants deep, precise melee combat
