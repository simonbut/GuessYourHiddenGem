What players say

A realistic flight simulator covering the whole planet, with a large fleet of aircraft, training and a career mode. It feels awe-inspiring and true to life in the air, though it takes time to learn and a strong PC.

  • pricey add-on aircraft and content

Best for: aviation fans and aspiring pilots with a capable PC

Not for: players on weak hardware or wanting a bug-free career mode
