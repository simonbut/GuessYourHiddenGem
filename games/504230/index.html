What players say

A demanding 2D precision platformer about Madeline climbing a mountain while dealing with anxiety and self-doubt. Players describe dying constantly but pushing on until each room finally clicks.

Players love

  • tight, satisfying controls built around the dash
  • tough but rewarding difficulty
  • moving story about anxiety and self-acceptance
  • standout soundtrack
  • lots of extra post-game levels (B-sides, C-sides, Farewell)

What stands out

  • Wholesome & heartwarming
  • Challenging
  • Pixel art

How it feels

  • challenging
  • heartfelt
  • triumphant

Watch out for

  • late optional content and Chapter 9 are brutally hard

Best for: players who love tough platforming and a story with emotional weight

Not for: anyone easily frustrated by dying over and over, though Assist Mode helps
