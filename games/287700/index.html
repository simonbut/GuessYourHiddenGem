What players say

An open-world stealth action game where you plan infiltrations however you like, with a wide toolkit and a growing Mother Base. Players love the moment-to-moment play but widely say the story feels unfinished.

Players love

  • huge freedom in how to tackle missions
  • addictive core stealth gameplay
  • great 80s music soundtrack
  • still looks and runs great
  • 100+ hours of content for many players

How it feels

  • tactical
  • atmospheric
  • melancholic
  • cinematic

Watch out for

  • story feels unfinished, no proper ending
  • repeated missions in chapter 2
  • real-time waits for base development
  • large, empty open world

Best for: stealth fans who love experimenting with gadgets and approaches

Not for: players who come mainly for a complete, tightly told Metal Gear story
