What players say

A colorful action RPG dungeon crawler with four classes, randomized areas, loot and pets, solo or in online and LAN co-op. Players still call it a classic loot grind, but recent AI-assisted updates from a new owner have split them.

Players love

  • long-awaited controller and Steam Deck support
  • four distinct classes with deep builds
  • pet companions
  • colorful, stylized art that has aged well
  • addictive, replayable loot grind

How it feels

  • colorful
  • lighthearted
  • addictive

Watch out for

  • controversial AI-assisted updates
  • online play now tied to a third-party account
  • distrust of the new owner's plans

Best for: ARPG and Diablo fans who want a classic loot crawl, especially on controller or Steam Deck

Not for: players who object to AI-assisted patches or want hassle-free online co-op
