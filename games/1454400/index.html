What players say

The classic idle game about baking an absurd number of cookies with grandmas, buildings and upgrades. You click for a while, then mostly leave it running and check back as the numbers climb.

Players love

  • perfect to leave running in the background
  • huge achievement chase
  • later systems like the garden, stock market and pantheon
  • simple start that slowly grows more complex

What stands out

  • Addictive, one-more-run

How it feels

  • addictive
  • absurd
  • laid-back

Watch out for

  • late game progress becomes very slow
  • time-gated mechanics like sugar lumps

Best for: idle and incremental fans who like checking in on ever-growing numbers

Not for: players who want active, hands-on gameplay
