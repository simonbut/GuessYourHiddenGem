What players say

A free-to-play Star Wars MMORPG built around fully voiced, choice-driven class stories with companions. It often plays like a single-player BioWare RPG wrapped in an aging MMO.

Players love

  • huge amount of free story content
  • strong Star Wars atmosphere
  • companions and dialogue choices
  • plays well solo, like a single-player RPG

What stands out

How it feels

  • epic
  • cinematic
  • nostalgic

Watch out for

  • account and login verification headaches
  • dated, grindy MMO parts and filler mobs
  • free-to-play limits and pricey cash shop

Best for: Star Wars fans who want a big story-driven RPG to play for free

Not for: players seeking modern, bustling MMO gameplay
