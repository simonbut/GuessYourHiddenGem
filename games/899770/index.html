What players say

An isometric loot-driven action RPG with 15 mastery classes, where each skill has its own tree and crafting lets you shape gear for your build.

Players love

  • sweet spot between Diablo and PoE
  • per-skill trees enable creative builds
  • viable builds without a guide
  • easy to learn with clear in-game guides

How it feels

  • grindy
  • satisfying
  • explosive

Watch out for

  • forced new terms and offline mode changes
  • server trouble at season launches
  • thin endgame and rare drop grind
  • too easy, little challenge

Best for: ARPG fans who want deep buildcrafting without PoE-level complexity

Not for: players wanting a fully offline game or long-lasting endgame
