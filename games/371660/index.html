What players say

Far Cry moves to the Stone Age: spears, bows and clubs instead of guns, plus tamed beasts like sabretooth tigers that fight at your side. The setting feels fresh, but underneath it is the familiar Ubisoft outpost-and-collectibles loop.

Players love

  • unique, immersive Stone Age setting
  • taming beasts to fight alongside you
  • invented ancient language for dialogue
  • gorgeous, dangerous wilderness

How it feels

  • primal
  • immersive
  • savage
  • adventurous

Watch out for

  • repetitive loop, especially in the second half
  • basic, thin story
  • Ubisoft Connect launch and login trouble
  • occasional crashes and bugs

Best for: Far Cry fans looking for a different flavor of open-world hunting and survival

Not for: players worn out on the Ubisoft open-world formula
