What players say

A colorful, cartoonish 2D beat 'em up for up to four players. You pick a knight, smash through levels, level up stats and unlock lots of characters. It's full of silly Newgrounds-style humor and catchy music.

Players love

  • chaotic couch and online co-op
  • charming cartoon art style
  • lots of unlockable characters
  • catchy, iconic soundtrack
  • easy to pick up and replay

What stands out

  • Great with friends
  • Genuinely funny

How it feels

  • funny
  • chaotic
  • nostalgic

Watch out for

  • combat gets repetitive, especially solo
  • a few tedious bosses, like the corn boss
  • shaky online play and lost progress

Best for: friends looking for a silly, nostalgic co-op brawler

Not for: players who dislike simple, repetitive beat 'em up combat
