What players say

A theme park builder with extremely detailed coaster design and scenery tools, plus a huge Steam Workshop. A spiritual heir to RollerCoaster Tycoon, best at creative sandbox building.

Players love

Watch out for

  • fiddly, frustrating path placement
  • scenery takes hours to build by hand
  • lots of content locked behind DLC

Best for: creative builders who love designing coasters and themed parks

Not for: players who want deep management or hand-holding
