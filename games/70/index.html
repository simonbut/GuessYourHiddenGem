What players say

Valve's 1998 sci-fi shooter where a scientist fights through a research facility after an experiment goes horribly wrong. It tells its story in-game rather than through cutscenes, and many say it still holds up.

Players love

  • groundbreaking storytelling without cutscenes
  • eerie, immersive Black Mesa atmosphere
  • memorable level design
  • fun gunplay and weapon variety

How it feels

  • tense
  • nostalgic
  • eerie
  • classic

Watch out for

  • dated jank and fiddly platforming
  • Xen and later chapters feel like a slog
  • easy to get lost without guidance
  • harsh, sometimes cheap difficulty

Best for: players curious about FPS history who can tolerate old-school design

Not for: players expecting modern controls and clear signposting
