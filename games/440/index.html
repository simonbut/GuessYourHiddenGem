What players say

Valve's classic free-to-play class shooter: nine distinct, funny characters fighting over objectives, plus a co-op wave mode. Fast respawns, chaotic fights, hats and plenty of goofing around with other players.

Players love

  • toxic chat at times
  • stutters and lag on some hardware

Best for: players who want a goofy, chaotic class shooter with lots of personality

Not for: anyone who needs a cheater-free, actively updated competitive game
