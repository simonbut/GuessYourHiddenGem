What players say

Valve's Dota 2 trading card game, now bundling Artifact Classic and the streamlined Foundry. Plays like a brainy MOBA in card form across three lanes, but today it is a quiet, abandoned game.

Players love

  • three-lane boards demanding real strategy
  • Dota heroes, items and lanes as cards
  • polished UI and attractive card animations
  • a fresh, clever take on the card game genre

What stands out

  • Competitive / ranked play

How it feels

  • cerebral
  • tactical
  • competitive

Watch out for

  • player base is all but gone
  • abandoned with no updates in years
  • very complex and hard for newcomers to get into
  • launch pay-for-cards model burned early buyers

Best for: card game veterans curious about a deep, unusual design who can live without a busy community

Not for: players wanting an active community or an easy entry into card games
