What players say

A janky, punishing Finnish life sim where you build a car from hundreds of loose parts, bolt by bolt, then try to keep it, and yourself, alive. Permadeath, drinking and engine fires turn every drive into a disaster story.

Players love

  • deeply detailed car assembly
  • endless hilarious mishaps
  • oddly addictive once it clicks
  • hard, with no hand-holding

How it feels

  • chaotic
  • funny
  • frustrating
  • absurd

Watch out for

  • unintuitive mechanics with no guidance
  • permadeath can erase hours of work
  • clunky physics, UI and bugs
  • rage-inducing difficulty

Best for: car nerds and patient tinkerers who enjoy chaotic, emergent stories

Not for: anyone who gets frustrated easily or wants clear instructions
