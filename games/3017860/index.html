What players say

A dark-medieval prequel to the modern Doom games where you tear through Hell's armies with a shield, inventive guns and brutal melee. It plays heavier and slower than Eternal, like a stomping tank wading through hordes.

Players love

  • Shield parries and bash-heavy combat
  • creative, distinct weapons
  • pounding metal soundtrack
  • gorgeous dark-medieval visuals
  • Strong Revelations DLC

How it feels

  • brutal
  • epic
  • intense
  • heavy

Watch out for

  • gets repetitive in later chapters
  • slower, parry-heavy combat doesn't click for all
  • Dragon and mech levels drag
  • steep specs, ray tracing can't be turned off

Best for: Doom fans open to a heavier, parry-focused take on the formula

Not for: Players wanting Eternal's speed or running GPUs without ray tracing
