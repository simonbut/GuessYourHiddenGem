What players say

A remaster of the 1940s-50s mob drama where war vet Vito Scaletta climbs a crime family to pay off his father's debts. It plays like a story-first crime film, mixing lots of driving with cover shootouts.

Players love

  • gripping, well-written mob story
  • memorable characters, especially Vito and Joe
  • convincing 1940s-50s atmosphere
  • stylish vintage cars

How it feels

  • atmospheric
  • cinematic
  • dramatic

Watch out for

  • bugs, crashes and frame drops
  • lazy remaster, not a ground-up remake
  • dated, poorly upscaled visuals
  • bland bonus DLC campaigns

Best for: players who want a cinematic, story-driven mob drama

Not for: anyone expecting a full remake like Mafia: Definitive Edition
