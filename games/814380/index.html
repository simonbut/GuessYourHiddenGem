What players say

A punishing FromSoftware action game where you play a shinobi in a mythic Sengoku Japan, winning fights by deflecting and countering attacks rather than building a character.

Players love

  • razor-sharp deflect-based swordplay
  • boss fights that feel like a rhythm dance
  • huge payoff once the combat clicks
  • inventive prosthetic tools and combat arts
  • striking setting and visuals

How it feels

  • tense
  • demanding
  • elegant
  • triumphant

Watch out for

  • brutally hard, especially early on
  • dying dozens of times on a single boss
  • frustrating fights against groups of enemies
  • dodging and controls can feel clumsy to some

Best for: players who love mastering tough boss fights through pure skill and patience

Not for: anyone who wants an easy, relaxed game or RPG-style character building
