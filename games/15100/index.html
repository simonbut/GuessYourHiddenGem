What players say

The original Assassin's Creed: third-person stealth-action where you climb, investigate and assassinate targets across medieval Damascus, Acre and Jerusalem. Atmospheric and story-driven but dated, and on PC it often needs fixes to run.

Players love

  • intriguing, morally ambiguous story
  • atmospheric cities that still hold up
  • parkour that feels good
  • combat that still feels satisfying
  • a classic worth playing as the series' start

How it feels

  • atmospheric
  • mysterious
  • nostalgic

Watch out for

  • crashes on launch without fan patches
  • online check causes stutter and freezes
  • same mission loop repeated
  • clunky controls, camera and keyboard support

Best for: patient players who want to see where the series began and don't mind tinkering to get it running

Not for: anyone expecting it to run out of the box on modern PCs, or wanting varied missions
