What players say

A village builder played entirely with cards: stack villagers on bushes, rocks and buildings to gather food, craft, and fight off creatures. It feels like a chill alchemy puzzle that slowly grows into a busy little settlement.

Players love

  • relaxing, low-pressure pace

  • late game turns repetitive

Best for: players who love discovery-driven crafting and relaxed resource juggling

Not for: those who want strong strategic pressure or a tidy, automated late game
