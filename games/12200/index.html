What players say

Rockstar's open-world school story: you play Jimmy, a troublemaker at a boarding school, going to classes, pulling pranks and fighting cliques. It feels like a funny, smaller GTA set in a school, but the PC port is unstable.

Players love

  • lively, detailed school setting
  • funny, memorable characters
  • classes that grant real upgrades
  • lots of side activities
  • GTA-style fun in a school

How it feels

  • funny
  • nostalgic
  • mischievous

Watch out for

  • frequent crashes on modern PCs
  • needs fan patches like SilentPatch
  • audio bugs, like NPCs blaring at full volume
  • no autosave, so crashes cost progress

Best for: fans of classic Rockstar games ready to install community fixes

Not for: players who want a PC port that works without modding
