What players say

A sci-fi grand strategy 4X where you design an alien species and guide its empire across a galaxy through exploration, diplomacy and war. Players say it captures the feel of running an interstellar empire, and each run plays out differently.

Players love

  • every playthrough feels different
  • lots of roleplay freedom

  • still getting new content after 10 years

How it feels

Watch out for

  • expensive, ever-growing DLC pile
  • big reworks keep changing the rules
  • late-game lag and slowdown
  • developers' use of generative AI

Best for: strategy fans who like to roleplay a galactic empire over hundreds of hours

Not for: players who want a complete experience without buying many DLCs
