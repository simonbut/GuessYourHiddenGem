What players say

A free-to-play online card battler set in the Witcher world, where you win two of three rounds by outscoring your opponent. Matches feel tactical and interactive, though official development has ended.

Players love

  • best-of-three rounds reward smart, strategic play
  • generous rewards, not pay-to-win
  • gorgeous card art and design
  • many viable factions and decks
  • community-voted monthly balance changes

How it feels

  • tactical
  • competitive
  • thoughtful

Watch out for

  • no new content since official support ended
  • very different from Witcher 3 Gwent
  • rough for new players facing meta decks

Best for: card game fans who like outthinking opponents, and Witcher fans

Not for: players expecting the Witcher 3 version of Gwent or fresh expansions
