What players say

A first-person FNAF game where you play as Gregory, sneaking and running through a huge Pizzaplex mall overnight while animatronics hunt you. It moves away from the series' sit-in-one-room style, and players call it buggy but lovable.

Players love

  • Explorable Mega Pizzaplex atmosphere
  • striking animatronic character designs
  • fresh open-world take on FNAF
  • fun and charming despite its bugs
  • fun to replay and keep exploring

What stands out

  • Genuinely scary

How it feels

  • tense
  • goofy
  • colorful
  • janky

Watch out for

  • poor optimization and stuttering, even years later
  • lots of bugs that can kill or break progress
  • few save points, so a death means long backtracking
  • confusing map, objectives and story

Best for: FNAF fans who want to roam a big, colorful horror mall and can laugh off jank

Not for: players who want real scares, smooth performance or a tightly written story
