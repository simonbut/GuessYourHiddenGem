What players say

A hardcore multiplayer dinosaur survival sim (most players recommend the Evrima branch). You spawn small, grow up slowly, and hunt or hide on a huge island where any bush could be hiding a player who wants to eat you.

Players love

  • constant fear of being hunted
  • great with friends or a pack
  • realistic, immersive dino-life fantasy
  • strong sound and good graphics
  • good community servers are worth finding

How it feels

  • tense
  • primal
  • harsh
  • janky

Watch out for

  • buggy: getting stuck, broken food and physics
  • poor optimization and lag
  • cheaters and toxic players
  • little guidance: no in-game map or tutorial

Best for: dinosaur fans who enjoy slow, high-risk survival and community servers

Not for: players who want a polished, guided game or hate losing hours of progress
