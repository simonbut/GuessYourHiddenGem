What players say

A free, short interactive story told through an early-2000s instant messenger, where you chat with your friend Emily across high school and college. Quiet, nostalgic and gently heartbreaking.

Players love

  • Authentic AIM-era messenger nostalgia
  • bittersweet story about drifting apart
  • Conversations that feel real
  • free and finished in under an hour
  • easy achievements and secret icons

How it feels

  • nostalgic
  • bittersweet
  • melancholy
  • intimate

Watch out for

  • choices all lead to the same ending
  • Emily can feel frustrating and unfair
  • very little gameplay beyond picking replies

Best for: Anyone nostalgic for AIM days or an old friendship

Not for: Players wanting real branching choices or a happy ending
