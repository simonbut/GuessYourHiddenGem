What players say

A top-down management sim where you design and run a prison. You build cells, set schedules, hire guards and add services, then deal with riots, escapes and chaos. Players still find it deep and fun, but many dislike what its newer owners have done.

Watch out for

  • DLC-heavy with features moved behind paywalls

Best for: management fans who like designing systems and watching them work or break

Not for: players who hate launchers or DLC-split content
