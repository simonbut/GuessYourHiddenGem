What players say

A first-person shop sim where you stock shelves, set prices, run the till and slowly grow a small store into a big supermarket. Players find it relaxing and addictive, like a calm job you keep clocking back into, though the late game turns grindy.

Players love

  • relaxing, addictive store-running loop
  • satisfying expansion and new product licenses
  • freedom to do tasks yourself or hire staff
  • fun pricing and profit tinkering
  • fun to play with a partner or friends

What stands out

  • Addictive, one-more-run
  • Relaxing & cozy

How it feels

  • chill
  • addictive
  • satisfying

Watch out for

  • grindy, repetitive late game
  • AI-generated art and models
  • paid DLC that players feel belongs in the base game
  • buggy multiplayer with desync

Best for: players who like chill, routine management games and slow, steady progress

Not for: anyone who wants variety or story, or who plans to play mostly in multiplayer
