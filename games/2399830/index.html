What players say

An Unreal Engine 5 remake of ARK, the dinosaur survival sandbox. You tame creatures, build bases, form tribes and fight to survive. It can be solo or PvE, or brutal PvP where your base can be raided while you are offline.

Players love

  • taming and keeping dinosaurs
  • huge sandbox with many ways to play
  • great with friends or a tribe
  • hard-earned progression that feels rewarding

How it feels

  • epic
  • punishing
  • chaotic

Watch out for

  • heavy performance demands and crashes
  • frequent patches need huge free disk space
  • creatures locked behind paid content

Best for: dinosaur fans with a strong PC and lots of time to sink in with friends

Not for: players on mid-range hardware or limited storage
