What players say

A first-person survival horror game where you sneak through a crumbling space station while a single Xenomorph hunts you. It's mostly hiding, listening and holding your breath, not fighting.

Players love

  • unpredictable, adaptive alien AI
  • relentless tension and dread
  • faithful to the original Alien film's atmosphere
  • excellent sound design
  • still looks great years later

What stands out

  • Genuinely scary

How it feels

  • tense
  • claustrophobic
  • atmospheric
  • terrifying

Watch out for

  • campaign feels padded and overlong
  • repetitive keycard and fetch objectives
  • long waits in hiding get tedious
  • alien can feel unfair and over-aggressive

Best for: patient stealth-horror fans and anyone who loves the original Alien film

Not for: players who want action or get frustrated by trial-and-error stealth
