What players say

A rebooted Mortal Kombat with a multiverse story mode, Kameo assist fighters and the series' gory fatalities. It is a flashy, brutal 2D fighter that is fun to pick up and hard to master online.

Players love

  • surprisingly fun story mode
  • smooth, satisfying core fighting
  • returning air combos and longer strings
  • sharp, detailed graphics
  • guest fighters like Omni-Man and Ghostface

How it feels

  • brutal
  • flashy
  • competitive

Watch out for

  • online full of experts, brutal for newcomers
  • PC stutters, shader loads and heavy memory use
  • pricey, with lots of paid DLC
  • divisive Kameo assist system

Best for: fighting game fans who enjoy learning combos and watching over-the-top fatalities

Not for: casual players who only want relaxed online matches
