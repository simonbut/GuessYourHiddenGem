What players say

A large-scale Cold War military sandbox with huge maps, combined-arms multiplayer battles and a deep library of community mods. Players describe it as slow, tense and team-dependent, with long trips to the fight and chaotic, funny moments along the way.

Players love

  • immersive, realistic combined-arms battles
  • extensive mod support and varied community servers
  • rewarding teamwork and tactics with friends
  • chaotic, memorable moments on busy servers

How it feels

  • tense
  • immersive
  • chaotic
  • gritty

Watch out for

  • Paid Rally DLC cars replace free vehicle spawns
  • long runs to the fight, then sudden death
  • Team-killing and toxic players
  • weak performance, crashes and bugs

Best for: patient players who want team-based milsim and a modded multiplayer sandbox

Not for: solo players looking for an AI-driven single-player sandbox, or anyone who wants fast action
