What players say

A turn-based tactical RPG where you run a mercenary company in a gritty, procedurally generated medieval world. You hire, gear and lose men in brutal fights, and every run tells its own story.

Players love

  • growing attached to your brothers
  • deep, satisfying tactical combat
  • brutal difficulty that rewards learning
  • flavorful events and world writing
  • highly replayable sandbox campaigns

What stands out

  • Punishing, rage-inducing

How it feels

  • brutal
  • gritty
  • tactical
  • tense

Watch out for

  • swingy RNG, missing 95% shots
  • missions and fights get repetitive
  • harsh losses can feel unfair

Best for: tactics fans who enjoy losing hard-won mercenaries and learning from it

Not for: players who get tilted by bad dice rolls
