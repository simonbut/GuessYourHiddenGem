What players say

A physics-driven sandbox where you design and build ships, stations and planet bases block by block, then mine, fly and fight with them. It's endlessly creative, but you have to climb a steep learning curve first.

Players love

  • near-limitless ship and base building
  • big modding and workshop scene
  • great with friends on servers
  • easy to lose hundreds of hours in

What stands out

  • Steep learning curve

How it feels

  • creative
  • absorbing
  • sprawling

Watch out for

  • too many DLCs gating blocks and ads in menus
  • steep learning curve with little tutorial
  • bugs, crashes and wonky physics
  • clunky UI and controls

Best for: tinkerers who love engineering puzzles and building in space

Not for: players who want guided progression or hate piles of DLC
