What players say

A first-person parkour game where courier Faith sprints, wall-runs and leaps across the gleaming rooftops of a clean, controlled city. When you hit a rhythm, it feels fast, free and stylish.

Players love

  • Free-flowing rooftop parkour
  • bright art style that still holds up
  • excellent soundtrack
  • unique, vibey atmosphere

How it feels

  • exhilarating
  • stylish
  • freeing

Watch out for

  • clunky forced combat and gunfire sections
  • inconsistent controls and missed ledge grabs
  • weak guidance, easy to get lost
  • crashes and performance drops on modern PCs

Best for: Movement-game fans who love learning a route and nailing the flow

Not for: Players who want solid combat or clear direction
