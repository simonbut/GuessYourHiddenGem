What players say

The first Total War set in the Warhammer Fantasy Old World, mixing turn-based empire building with large real-time battles full of monsters and magic. Players describe it as a leaner, more focused campaign than its sequels.

Players love

  • faithful take on Warhammer Fantasy
  • Leaner, more focused campaigns than Warhammer 3
  • addictive campaigns players return to for years
  • runs smoothly, lighter than Warhammer 3

How it feels

  • epic

Watch out for

  • steep learning curve, weak tutorial
  • AI throws multiple full armies at you
  • Pricey DLC, often bought just to unlock factions in Warhammer 3

Best for: Warhammer Fantasy fans who want tighter campaigns than the sequels, or players with older PCs

Not for: strategy newcomers who want a gentle tutorial
