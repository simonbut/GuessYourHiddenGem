What players say

The Ezio sequel to Assassin's Creed II: open-world stealth action in Renaissance Rome, where you climb landmarks, fight Borgia forces and build your own band of assassins. Players describe it as a more polished AC2.

Players love

  • Refines nearly everything from AC2
  • recruiting and calling in your own assassins
  • Ezio's charisma and growth
  • Renaissance Rome as an immersive setting
  • engaging story continuing Ezio's saga

How it feels

  • epic
  • adventurous
  • nostalgic

Watch out for

  • Bugged full-sync objectives and final fight
  • clunky controls and traversal

Best for: AC2 players who want to keep following Ezio's story in Renaissance Rome

Not for: Completionists chasing 100% sync, who may hit bugged objectives
