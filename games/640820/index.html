What players say

An isometric, party-based CRPG built on the Pathfinder tabletop rules, where you explore the Stolen Lands and rule your own kingdom. It's a huge, demanding campaign that rewards careful planning and knowing the rules.

Players love

  • deep, faithful Pathfinder tabletop rules
  • memorable, morally varied companions
  • compelling main story
  • huge campaign that runs 100+ hours
  • wide build and gear variety

How it feels

  • epic
  • tactical
  • challenging

Watch out for

  • tedious kingdom management
  • quest-breaking bugs
  • opaque rules poorly explained
  • punishing spikes and late-game slog

Best for: CRPG veterans who love tabletop rules depth and very long campaigns

Not for: players who want a quick, forgiving RPG
