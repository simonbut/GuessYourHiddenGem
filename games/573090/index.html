What players say

A deep vehicle-building sandbox where you design boats, aircraft and more from parts and logic, then run search-and-rescue missions or just explore a stormy ocean world.

Players love

How it feels

  • creative

Watch out for

  • laggy, unstable multiplayer
  • poor performance even on decent PCs
  • steep learning curve with little tutorial
  • clunky controls and frequent bugs

Best for: patient tinkerers who love engineering their own vehicles

Not for: players who want a guided, easygoing builder like Trailmakers
