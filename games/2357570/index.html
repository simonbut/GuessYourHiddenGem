What players say

Blizzard's free-to-play 5v5/6v6 team-based hero shooter. Players describe a love-hate relationship: a good match is a huge rush, while bad matchmaking, toxic teammates and balance changes can make it miserable.

Players love

  • addictive highs when a match goes well
  • polished hero shooter players keep coming back to
  • much more fun with friends or a voice-chat group
  • 5v5 and recent changes have improved the game

What stands out

How it feels

  • competitive

  • addictive

Watch out for

  • unpopular balance changes, like the Wrecking Ball nerf called a 'bug fix'
  • feels balanced around selling skins, with pricey cosmetics
  • lopsided matchmaking and toxic players
  • server disconnects that get punished with suspensions

Best for: players who love team-based hero shooters, especially with a regular group

Not for: solo players who tilt easily at teammates or hate balance whiplash
