What players say

A free-to-play, S.T.A.L.K.E.R.-inspired extraction shooter set in a blocky Chernobyl Zone. You head out from safe zones, farm loot and quests through anomalies and rival-player territory, then haul it home. You keep your gear when you die.

Watch out for

  • harsh balance, with tough NPCs and bosses

Best for: Stalker fans who like slow, MMO-style farming loops

Not for: players who hate long grinds or getting ganked by geared veterans
