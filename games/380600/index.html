What players say

A free-to-play online fishing sim with detailed tackle, line tension and fish fights across waters in many countries. Players find it relaxing and realistic, but progress is a long grind and the game pushes paid DLC hard.

Players love

  • great graphics, water and sound

How it feels

Watch out for

  • slow, grindy progression for free players
  • costly travel, licenses and gear
  • long waits for bites

Best for: patient anglers who want a realistic, laid-back fishing sim

Not for: players who hate mobile-style monetization and grinding
