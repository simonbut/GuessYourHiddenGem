What players say

An expanded remake of the meta narrative game about Stanley, an office worker, and the Narrator who tells his story. You choose whether to obey or defy him, and that choice leads to many strange, funny outcomes.

Players love

  • many endings and hidden content to find
  • sharp satire of choice and free will
  • new content that riffs on the original

Watch out for

  • short for its full price

Best for: players who love meta humor and poking at a game's rules to see what happens

Not for: players short on time or wanting gameplay beyond walking and listening
