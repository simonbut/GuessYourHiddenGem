What players say

A single-player survival and exploration game on an alien ocean world: you dive, gather resources, build bases and subs, and slowly uncover a mystery as you push into deeper, scarier waters. Wonder and dread go hand in hand.

Players love

  • gorgeous, mysterious underwater world
  • real fear of the deep and leviathans
  • rewarding expedition-and-upgrade loop
  • engaging story told through exploration
  • excellent sound design

What stands out

  • Genuinely scary

How it feels

  • wondrous
  • eerie
  • immersive
  • tense

Watch out for

  • unclear objectives, easy to get lost
  • slow early game and fragment hunting
  • occasional game-breaking glitches
  • repetitive resource gathering mid-game

Best for: explorers who like discovering things themselves and enjoy a little ocean dread

Not for: players who need clear goals or dislike survival resource gathering
