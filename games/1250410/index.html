What players say

A flight simulator with detailed aircraft from gliders to airliners and a recreation of the whole planet to fly over. Players call it breathtaking and relaxing once running, but many struggle with the huge download through Microsoft's client.

Players love

How it feels

  • peaceful

Watch out for

  • huge, slow and often failing download through Microsoft's installer
  • download can eat the Steam refund window
  • forced Microsoft account sign-in that often fails
  • best add-ons are expensive

Best for: aviation and geography fans with a strong PC and patience for setup

Not for: players with slow internet or weak hardware, or who want quick action
