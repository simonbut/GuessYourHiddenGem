What players say

A classic flight simulator where you can fly everything from small props to airliners anywhere in the world, with lessons, missions and free flight. It's an old, low-cost, relaxing way to play pilot.

Players love

How it feels

Watch out for

  • crashes or won't launch on Windows 11
  • trouble with multi-monitor, ultrawide and controller setups

Best for: flight sim fans on older or low-end PCs

Not for: players who want modern visuals or a hassle-free install on Windows 11
