What players say

A free-to-play online zombie survival shooter, formerly The War Z, where you scavenge an open world for loot while fending off zombies and other players. Players remember it fondly, but today it feels rough, dated and underpopulated.

Players love

  • nostalgia for the old WarZ days
  • solid loot-and-survive zombie concept
  • scrappy, arcade-style take on DayZ

How it feels

  • nostalgic
  • janky

Watch out for

  • official servers nearly empty
  • frequent connection and server errors
  • cheaters on PvP servers
  • dated and rougher than it used to be

Best for: former WarZ players chasing nostalgia

Not for: anyone wanting busy servers, stable connections or a polished modern survival game
