What players say

A first-person driving survival game: scavenge a strange exclusion zone in a beat-up station wagon, then limp back to your garage to repair and upgrade it before the next tense run.

Players love

  • thick, eerie atmosphere
  • upgrading and customizing your wagon
  • truly original concept
  • weird car quirks to diagnose
  • great sound design and radio songs

What stands out

  • Relaxing & cozy

How it feels

  • eerie
  • tense
  • mysterious

Watch out for

  • loot-and-upgrade loop gets repetitive
  • fiddly menus and slow interactions
  • car needs constant repairs
  • bugs, stuck spots and weak performance settings

Best for: survival fans who love tinkering with a car between eerie runs

Not for: players hoping for a relaxed road trip or streamlined controls
