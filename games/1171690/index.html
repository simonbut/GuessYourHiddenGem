What players say

A co-op action RPG, once live-service, now a finished game you can play solo offline or with friends. Bright stylized world, hack-and-slash dungeons and bosses, and build tinkering, but no further updates are coming.

Players love

Watch out for

  • stutters and hitching even on strong PCs
  • repetitive dungeons and material grind
  • buggy co-op with desyncs and crashes
  • busywork fetch-quest side content

Best for: players after a cheap, colorful ARPG to grind solo or with a couple of friends

Not for: players sensitive to stutter or wanting a real endgame and future updates
