What players say

A big open-world Monster Hunter where you track and fight huge monsters, carve their parts into gear, and ride between shifting biomes on a mount.

Players love

  • fluid, best-in-series weapon combat
  • imposing, cinematic monster fights
  • great music and gorgeous environments
  • much more beginner-friendly
  • performance improved since launch

How it feels

  • epic
  • cinematic
  • accessible
  • vibrant

Watch out for

  • poor optimization and crashes on many PCs
  • too easy, with a thin monster roster
  • weak, cutscene-heavy story
  • awkward co-op during story missions

Best for: newcomers and action fans who want flashy, accessible monster fights

Not for: veterans wanting tough hunts and deep endgame, or players on modest PCs
