What players say

A short single-player fairytale adventure where you steer two brothers at once, one per controller stick, to find a cure for their ailing father. A wordless, light-puzzle journey that turns darker than it looks.

Best for: players who want a short, emotional story to finish in one sitting, ideally with a controller

Not for: players wanting challenging puzzles or lots of game for the money
