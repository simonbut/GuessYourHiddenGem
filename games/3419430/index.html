What players say

A free desktop companion: a little cat sits on your taskbar and slaps it every time you type or click. Your inputs earn points that unlock hats and cosmetics, so it turns everyday typing into a small idle game.

Players love

  • lots of cosmetics to collect
  • makes writing and homework feel rewarding
  • runs quietly in the background
  • free, with lobbies to idle alongside others

How it feels

Watch out for

  • added achievements and rare drops feel grindy
  • can get in the way of games or slow them down

Best for: people who spend hours typing and want a cute buddy on screen

Not for: anyone looking for an actual game with depth
