What players say

A free-to-play space-ninja looter shooter where you slide, flip and slash through hordes, then grind for new frames and weapons. It has years of content to dig into, and it can swallow hundreds of hours.

Players love

  • fluid, satisfying movement and combat
  • everything earnable without paying
  • huge variety of frames and weapons
  • devs who listen to players
  • friendly, helpful community

What stands out

  • Lots of content / long

How it feels

  • fast
  • flashy
  • sprawling
  • addictive

Watch out for

  • overwhelming, poorly explained start
  • long resource grinds
  • real-time crafting and rank-up timers
  • harsh chat moderation and bans

Best for: players who want a huge, generous free game to sink hundreds of hours into

Not for: players who hate grinding or learning systems from a wiki
