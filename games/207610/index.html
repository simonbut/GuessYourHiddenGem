What players say

Telltale's episodic zombie adventure where you guide Lee and a young girl, Clementine, through hard choices and dialogue. It plays like an interactive movie, and players remember it for the story and characters more than for the gameplay.

Players love

  • deeply emotional story
  • characters that feel human, especially Lee and Clementine
  • tough decisions with painful consequences
  • still holds up years later

What stands out

  • Emotional, tearjerker moments

How it feels

  • emotional
  • bleak
  • tense

Watch out for

  • choices rarely change the outcome
  • graphical bugs and save problems for some

Best for: players who want a story that will hit them hard

Not for: those who want real branching paths or action gameplay
