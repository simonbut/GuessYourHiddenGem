What players say

The HD edition of the classic medieval RTS: build a village, gather resources and raise armies across the ages. Players love it for nostalgia, but it is now retired in favor of the Definitive Edition.

Players love

  • timeless, nostalgic RTS gameplay
  • closer to the original than the Definitive Edition
  • many civilizations with distinct strengths
  • adjustable AI difficulty for solo play
  • lots of Workshop mods

How it feels

  • nostalgic
  • strategic
  • classic

Watch out for

  • retired and no longer updated
  • launch, crash and validation issues
  • no free upgrade to the Definitive Edition

Best for: longtime fans who want the classic Age II feel without the Definitive Edition's changes

Not for: newcomers, who are better served by the Definitive Edition
