What players say

A huge procedurally generated space sandbox where you hop between planets, scan alien life, gather resources, build bases and upgrade ships. Players describe it as a relaxed, open-ended game to wander in, kept growing by years of free updates.

Players love

  • huge variety of planets and creatures
  • relaxing, open-ended exploration
  • base, ship and freighter building
  • years of free updates, no microtransactions
  • lots to collect and do

What stands out

  • Lots of content / long

How it feels

  • relaxing
  • vast
  • wondrous

Watch out for

  • repetitive resource grinding
  • planets start to feel samey
  • bugs and glitches, some game-breaking
  • blurry, poorly performing VR mode

Best for: players who like to wander, collect and build at their own pace in space

Not for: players who want a strong story or clear, focused goals
