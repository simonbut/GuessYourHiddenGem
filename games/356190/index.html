What players say

An open-world Lord of the Rings action game where you fight, dominate and recruit orc captains to build an army and storm fortresses. The Nemesis system makes orcs remember you, hold grudges and sometimes betray you.

Players love

  • brilliant Nemesis system with memorable orcs
  • fluid, Arkham-style combat
  • recruiting and managing an orc army
  • cinematic fortress sieges
  • funny, trash-talking orc captains

How it feels

  • epic
  • chaotic
  • brutal

Watch out for

  • repetitive missions and activities
  • long, unskippable captain introductions
  • awkward controls and key bindings

Best for: players who love emergent rivalries and building an army of personalities

Not for: players who tire quickly of repetitive open-world content or constant interruptions
