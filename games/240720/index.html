What players say

You climb a surreal mountain as a man in a cauldron, steering a hammer with the mouse while the developer narrates. One slip can send you all the way back down, which is the whole point.

Players love

  • huge satisfaction when you finally finish
  • hilariously infuriating rage game
  • teaches persistence: you only lose if you quit
  • addictive enough to replay for 50 climbs

How it feels

  • frustrating
  • philosophical
  • funny

Watch out for

  • falls can wipe out hours of progress
  • awkward, stiff hammer controls
  • built mainly to frustrate you

Best for: players who love brutal skill tests and the payoff of persistence

Not for: anyone who wants a relaxing game or hates losing progress
