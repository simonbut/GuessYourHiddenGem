What players say

An open-world action sandbox on a huge tropical island. You grapple, parachute and blow up government property in creative ways. The story is a thin excuse for big explosions.

Players love

  • grapple-and-parachute movement is a joy
  • enormous open world to explore
  • creative, explosive sandbox chaos

How it feels

  • explosive
  • silly
  • freewheeling

Watch out for

  • frequent crashes on modern systems
  • repetitive completion tasks like blowing up the same targets
  • short, thin story
  • clunky vehicles and controls

Best for: players who want to switch off and cause action-movie mayhem

Not for: completionists or anyone wanting a strong story
