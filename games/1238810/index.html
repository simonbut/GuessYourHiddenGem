What players say

A World War II shooter with large-scale multiplayer battles, tanks and planes, plus short standalone War Stories in its single-player mode.

Watch out for

  • often fails to launch, EA account/anti-cheat issues
  • crashes, bugs and long loading times
  • hackers and unbalanced matches
  • short campaign for the price

Best for: WW2 shooter fans who want gritty large battles at a sale price

Not for: players wanting a long campaign or a hassle-free launch
