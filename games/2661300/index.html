What players say

A shrunk-down survival-crafting sequel in early access, set in a sprawling park, where you gather, build and fight giant bugs solo or in co-op. Exploring feels big and dangerous again, especially from the saddle of your own bug mount.

Players love

  • expands everything from Grounded 1
  • rideable bug buggies
  • huge park to explore
  • punchier combat and many new bugs
  • gorgeous, detailed visuals

How it feels

  • adventurous
  • playful
  • tense

Watch out for

  • stutters, crashes and heavy hardware demands
  • clipping and glitches
  • thin, unclear story
  • grindy material gathering

Best for: Grounded 1 fans and survival-crafters, solo or with friends

Not for: players on weaker PCs or anyone wanting a polished, finished release
