What players say

An open-world survival shooter where you loot, craft, build a base and fight AI bandits and other players. Players describe it as a lighter, easier take on DayZ, Rust and Tarkov.

Players love

  • more accessible DayZ/Rust-style survival
  • official PvE servers for relaxed play
  • challenging AI gunfights

How it feels

  • tense
  • gritty
  • desolate

Watch out for

  • low player counts, mostly empty servers
  • AI enemies with near-perfect aim
  • tedious blueprint-based weapon crafting
  • empty world with long runs between spots

Best for: survival shooter fans who want a less punishing DayZ or Rust with PvE options

Not for: players looking for busy, populated PvP servers
