What players say

A dark action RPG about slaying hordes and chasing loot across Sanctuary, with a skippable campaign and regular seasons that reset progress.

Players love

  • satisfying horde-clearing combat
  • easy, flexible build experimenting
  • gorgeous visuals and sound
  • strong story and cinematics
  • much improved since launch

How it feels

  • dark
  • grindy
  • satisfying

Watch out for

  • key features locked behind paid expansion
  • lag and rubber-banding online
  • huge, frequent patches
  • repetitive, RNG-heavy gear grind

Best for: loot-hunters who enjoy seasonal grinding and relaxed demon-slaying

Not for: players who dislike always-online play or pricey expansions
