What players say

A story-driven action adventure where an older Kratos travels the Norse realms with his son Atreus. Weighty combat is mixed with exploration and puzzles around an emotional father-son story.

Players love

  • emotional father-son story
  • weighty, satisfying combat with the Leviathan Axe
  • gorgeous realms worth exploring
  • rich Norse lore and world
  • memorable boss fights, especially the Valkyries

How it feels

  • epic
  • emotional
  • cinematic

Watch out for

  • frequent environmental puzzles break the pace
  • repetitive enemies and reused bosses

Best for: players who want a cinematic, emotional single-player adventure with satisfying melee combat

Not for: those who want nonstop action without puzzle breaks
