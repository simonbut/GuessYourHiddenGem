What players say

A big-budget, very cinematic reimagining of the Midgar section of FFVII, with real-time combat mixed with pause-to-command abilities. Intergrade adds a Yuffie side story. It is linear and heavy on story and cutscenes.

Best for: fans of the original and players who want cinematic action-RPG combat

Not for: players who hate linear corridors, padding and long cutscenes
