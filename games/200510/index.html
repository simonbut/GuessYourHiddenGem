What players say

A turn-based tactics game where you lead a small squad against an alien invasion and manage the base, research and funding back home. Players describe it as tense, punishing and hard to put down, with plans that often fall apart.

Players love

  • tense squad tactics that reward careful planning
  • hard but rewarding difficulty
  • base, research and economy layer between missions
  • Enemy Within expansion adds a lot
  • addictive 'one more mission' pull

How it feels

  • tense
  • punishing
  • dramatic

Watch out for

  • swingy hit chances, missed 'sure' shots
  • bugs, softlocks and camera glitches
  • slow pace with lots of camera movement
  • confusing split between base and Enemy Within versions

Best for: players who like hard, punishing tactics where losses and comebacks make the story

Not for: anyone who can't stand RNG deciding a fight
