What players say

An asymmetric horror multiplayer game: four survivors repair generators and escape while one player hunts them as a killer, including famous horror movie icons.

Players love

  • tense chases around pallets and windows
  • big roster of horror icons
  • play as either survivor or killer
  • much more fun with friends
  • addictive one-more-match loop

What stands out

  • Great with friends

How it feels

  • tense
  • chaotic
  • stressful
  • funny

Watch out for

  • endless balance arguments and frustrating matches
  • toxic players and bully squads
  • slow grind and lots of paid DLC
  • bugs, connection problems and crash penalties

Best for: horror fans who want a stressful but funny game with friends

Not for: solo players who get easily tilted by toxic matches
