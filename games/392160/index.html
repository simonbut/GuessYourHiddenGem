What players say

A sprawling space sandbox where you start with one ship and can trade, mine, fight, build stations and command fleets in a simulated galaxy. Slow and confusing to learn, then hugely absorbing.

Players love

  • living economy that runs on its own
  • freedom to be pilot, trader, pirate or tycoon
  • huge scale and fleet-sized battles
  • rewarding once it finally clicks
  • strong mods, including Star Wars overhauls

What stands out

  • Steep learning curve

How it feels

  • vast
  • absorbing
  • methodical

Watch out for

  • steep learning curve, weak tutorials
  • clunky, cluttered menus and UI
  • awkward flight controls and docking
  • buggy or stuck missions

Best for: patient sandbox fans wanting a single-player EVE

Not for: anyone who wants clear guidance or quick gratification
