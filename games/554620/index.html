What players say

A three-episode prequel to Life is Strange where you play rebellious Chloe Price, three years before the first game, as she copes with her father's death and forms a bond with Rachel Amber. Players describe a slower, slice-of-life drama driven by dialogue choices, with an emotional, bittersweet ending.
