What players say

The co-op version of Don't Starve: a gothic, hand-drawn wilderness survival game where you gather, farm, build a base and survive seasons and bosses with friends. It teaches you almost nothing and punishes mistakes, but players say it opens up once you learn it.

Players love

  • charming, distinctive hand-drawn art
  • unique characters with different playstyles
  • huge amount of content and bosses
  • excellent with friends
  • adjustable world settings and many mods

What stands out

  • Great with friends
  • Steep learning curve

How it feels

  • quirky
  • challenging
  • punishing
  • addictive

Watch out for

  • explains nothing, so you'll need the wiki
  • clunky, frustrating combat
  • steep, exhausting difficulty curve for new players

Best for: friends who enjoy learning a deep, unforgiving survival game together over hundreds of hours

Not for: players who want clear guidance or satisfying action combat
