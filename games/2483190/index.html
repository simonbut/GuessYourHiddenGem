What players say

An open-world racing game set across Japan, where you start as a tourist and climb the festival ranks with a big garage of real cars. Cruising and exploring the gorgeous map is the highlight, but content runs thin.

Players love

How it feels

  • scenic

Watch out for

  • thin content once the story is done
  • repetitive weekly playlists that feel like chores
  • stutters and poor optimization
  • feels too similar to Forza Horizon 5

Best for: players who love exploring a beautiful map at their own pace

Not for: veterans hoping for a big leap over Forza Horizon 5 or lasting endgame content
