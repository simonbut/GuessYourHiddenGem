What players say

A hardcore open-world survival game on a hostile island, with deep body simulation down to nutrition and bodily needs, zombies, PvP and base building. Players praise the depth but many long-time fans complain about bugs, DLC focus and server problems.

Best for: patient survival fans who want detailed, unforgiving simulation

Not for: casual players or anyone bothered by bugs and losing progress
