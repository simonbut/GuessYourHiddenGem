What players say

A classic Star Wars party RPG set five years after the first KOTOR, where you play a lone Jedi facing the Sith. It plays as a somber, conversation-heavy adventure that keeps questioning what the light and dark sides really mean.

Players love

  • sharp, philosophical writing and dialogue
  • thoughtful deconstruction of Star Wars
  • memorable, well-written companions
  • worth replaying many times over
  • restoration mod brings back cut content

How it feels

  • thoughtful
  • somber
  • nostalgic

Watch out for

  • visibly rushed, with cut content
  • bugs, crashes and softlocks
  • clunky, dated combat

Best for: Star Wars and classic RPG fans who care more about writing than polish

Not for: players who can't stand dated combat or installing a restoration mod
