What players say

A space survival crafting game for 1 to 10 players where you gather resources, build a base and terraform a barren planet into a living world. Players call it relaxing and satisfying to watch the planet change, though many feel the loop gets repetitive later.

Watch out for

  • repetitive, grindy gather-and-craft loop
  • late stages drag on with slow progress
  • rough terrain and bugs
  • early oxygen and inventory limits feel tedious

Best for: players who enjoy relaxed survival crafting and slow, steady progress, especially in co-op

Not for: players who want combat, deep systems or fast progression
