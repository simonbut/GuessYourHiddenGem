What players say

A dark-fantasy action RPG and the sequel to Path of Exile, with a huge passive skill tree, tough boss fights and an endless loot-driven endgame, playable solo or in co-op. It is in early access.

Players love

  • endless build variety and theorycrafting
  • Smooth WASD movement and combat
  • massive endgame with regular updates
  • no pay-to-win
  • hundreds of hours of depth

How it feels

  • dark
  • punishing
  • addictive

Watch out for

  • account bans with unhelpful support
  • punishing for players without build guides
  • heavy grind and harsh death penalties
  • performance drops, crashes and lag

Best for: dedicated ARPG fans who love complex builds and long grinds

Not for: casual players who want to play without guides
