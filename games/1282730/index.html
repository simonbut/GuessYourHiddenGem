What players say

A roguelite where the hero walks a looping road on his own while you place cards (monsters, buildings, terrain) to shape each run, then rebuild your camp with the loot. Semi-idle tinkering that many players find addictive.

Players love

  • inventive build-the-loop card placement
  • discovering hidden tile combos
  • surprisingly addictive
  • beautiful pixel art and music
  • mysterious lore and worldbuilding

What stands out

  • Addictive, one-more-run
  • Pixel art

How it feels

  • chill
  • addictive
  • strategic

Watch out for

  • slow resource grind for upgrades
  • goes stale once the novelty fades
  • limited control over what happens

Best for: tinkerers who enjoy semi-idle strategy and uncovering card synergies

Not for: players who want direct control of combat or hate grinding for upgrades
