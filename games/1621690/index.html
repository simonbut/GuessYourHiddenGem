What players say

A top-down mining sandbox for 1-8 players: dig through an underground world, build a base, farm, craft gear and fight bosses. Players compare it to a calmer mix of Terraria and Stardew Valley.

Watch out for

  • slow travel through mined tunnels

Best for: players who want a laid-back Terraria-style game to share with friends or a partner

Not for: players after varied bosses or Terraria-level depth
