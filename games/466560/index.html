What players say

A Norse-themed strategy game that's part city builder, part RTS: you claim territory tile by tile, manage food and wood, prepare for harsh winters and fight rival clans. Many players find the pace relaxed and comfy.

Players love

  • clans with very different playstyles
  • harsh winters that force planning
  • relaxed pace for city-builder fans
  • enjoyable solo campaign and conquest modes
  • easy to start, deep to master

What stands out

  • Great with friends

How it feels

  • cozy
  • strategic
  • harsh

Watch out for

  • AI that cheats with resources yet plays poorly
  • many clans sold as paid DLC
  • bugs, lag and network issues; game feels abandoned
  • online play blocked in Russia

Best for: city-builder fans who want a gentler, economy-focused take on RTS

Not for: serious PvP players and those wanting all content in the base game
