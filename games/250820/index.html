What players say

Valve's free software for playing PC VR games, supporting many headsets including Quest through Steam Link or a cable. It isn't a game; it's the layer most PC VR titles run through.

Players love

  • required for most PC VR games
  • free
  • works with Quest headsets via Steam Link
  • wired connection gives the smoothest play

How it feels

  • practical
  • finicky

Watch out for

  • updates often break things that worked
  • connection failures and error 450
  • stutters and lag over wireless
  • poor Linux support

Best for: anyone who wants to play PC VR games, since most require it

Not for: Linux users or anyone hoping for flawless wireless play
