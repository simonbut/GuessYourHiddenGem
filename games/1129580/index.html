What players say

A first-person medieval life sim mixing survival, crafting, farming, hunting and village management. You start with almost nothing and slowly grow a settlement, a family and a dynasty. Players describe it as relaxed, cozy and often funny.

Players love

  • chill pace with calm music
  • satisfying growth from a hut to a full village
  • custom settings let you tune difficulty
  • beautiful, lived-in valley
  • more fun in co-op

What stands out

How it feels

  • cozy
  • relaxing

  • humorous

Watch out for

  • clunky combat and odd animal AI
  • bugs left unfixed while paid DLC comes out
  • building limit and thin late game
  • slow, grindy pace, especially solo

Best for: players who like slow, cozy survival-building and managing a village by hand

Not for: people wanting polished combat or fast action
