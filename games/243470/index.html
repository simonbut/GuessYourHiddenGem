What players say

An open-world action game set in a surveillance-heavy Chicago, where vigilante hacker Aiden Pearce mixes cover shooting, driving and hacking the city around him.

Players love

  • creative city-wide hacking
  • Atmospheric Chicago setting
  • strong revenge story for many
  • punchy combat and takedowns
  • holds up and runs well today

How it feels

  • gritty
  • dark
  • tense

Watch out for

  • forced aim assist and poor mouse aiming
  • Ubisoft launcher breaks online play
  • dull story and flat dialogue for some
  • clunky controls, stutters and crashes for some

Best for: players who want a cheap, story-driven open-world game with a hacking twist

Not for: mouse-and-keyboard purists or completionists who need online content
