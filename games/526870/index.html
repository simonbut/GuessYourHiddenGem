What players say

A first-person factory builder on an alien planet. You start with a few drills and conveyor belts and grow into huge, multi-story production lines, then rebuild them better. Players describe it as absorbing and easy to lose track of time in.

Players love

  • hours vanish optimizing factories
  • satisfying loop of improving and rebuilding
  • beautiful hand-crafted world to explore
  • adjustable settings to skip parts you dislike
  • can be played as a chill, easy-going game

What stands out

  • Addictive, one-more-run

How it feels

  • absorbing
  • relaxing
  • methodical

Watch out for

  • epic account requirement and data-collection changes
  • performance problems and crashes, especially on weaker PCs
  • fiddly power and fluid systems
  • producing large quantities can feel tedious

Best for: players who love planning, tinkering and perfecting production lines

Not for: players who want a strong story push or dislike constant rebuilding
