What players say

A third-person Star Wars adventure following a Padawan in hiding after Order 66. It mixes souls-lite lightsaber combat built on parrying with Tomb Raider-style climbing, puzzles and backtracking across several planets.

Players love

  • strong Star Wars atmosphere and story
  • satisfying lightsaber and parry combat
  • memorable villains and companions like BD-1
  • beautiful planets to explore

How it feels

  • cinematic
  • adventurous
  • epic

Watch out for

  • confusing 3D map, easy to get lost
  • long runbacks and no fast travel
  • combat can feel clunky and frustrating

Best for: Star Wars fans who want a cinematic, lightly souls-like action adventure

Not for: players who hate backtracking or want deep, precise combat
