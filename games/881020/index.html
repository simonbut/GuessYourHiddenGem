What players say

An anime action RPG built around flashy boss fights, played solo with AI allies or in 4-player co-op. The story is short and the real game is the endgame grind for better builds.

Players love

  • spectacular boss fights
  • fast, flashy action combat
  • deep build-crafting with characters that each play differently
  • full voice acting and a rousing soundtrack

How it feels

  • flashy
  • energetic
  • upbeat

Watch out for

  • main story is short and childish
  • heavy, repetitive endgame grind for sigils
  • online matchmaking is quiet and players drop mid-fight
  • higher difficulties reuse the same bosses

Best for: fans of Monster Hunter-style boss rushes who enjoy grinding out builds

Not for: players who want a long, mature story
