What players say

A free-to-play online cops-vs-criminals shooter set in an open city, with very deep character and car customization. Today it is a small, veteran-heavy community that mostly runs on nostalgia.

Players love

  • extremely deep character and car customization
  • unique open-world cops-vs-criminals concept
  • nostalgic for long-time players
  • Free, GTA-style alternative

How it feels

  • chaotic
  • nostalgic
  • competitive
  • frustrating

Watch out for

  • cheaters and no effective anti-cheat
  • pay-to-win weapon progression
  • new players matched against geared veterans
  • tiny, often toxic player base

Best for: returning veterans and players who mainly enjoy designing characters and cars

Not for: new or casual players looking for fair PvP
