What players say

A brutal 2D survival platformer where you play a small slugcat in a ruined, living ecosystem, scavenging food and fleeing predators before deadly rain arrives. It explains almost nothing and you will die constantly.

Players love

  • gorgeous pixel art and environments
  • living, unpredictable creature ecosystem
  • outstanding music and sound design
  • exploration feels hard-earned and rewarding
  • mysterious, open-ended lore

What stands out

  • Punishing, rage-inducing
  • Pixel art
  • Challenging

How it feels

  • atmospheric
  • brutal
  • mysterious
  • frustrating

Watch out for

  • no guidance, easy to feel lost
  • deaths can feel random or unfair
  • slippery controls take getting used to
  • karma progression can feel like lost time

Best for: patient players who enjoy punishing, mysterious survival and figuring things out alone

Not for: anyone who wants clear goals, steady progress or a relaxing game
