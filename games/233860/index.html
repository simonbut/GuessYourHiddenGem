What players say

A free-roaming squad-based sandbox RPG with no linear story. You start weak in a harsh post-apocalyptic world and can become a trader, thief, warlord or anything else. Players describe brutal, often darkly funny emergent stories of getting beaten, enslaved and slowly growing stronger.

Players love

  • total freedom to make your own story
  • rewarding climb from weakling to powerhouse
  • memorable, brutal emergent moments
  • strong modding community
  • intriguing world, lore and music

How it feels

  • brutal
  • absurd
  • freeing
  • bleak

Watch out for

  • steep learning curve with no hand-holding
  • janky, buggy and dated-looking
  • slow, heavy grind
  • little direction or goal

Best for: patient sandbox fans who enjoy writing their own story through failure

Not for: players who want a guided story, polish or quick progress
