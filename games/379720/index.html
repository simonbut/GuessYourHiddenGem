What players say

The 2016 reboot of DOOM: a fast, brutal first-person shooter where you tear through demons on Mars and in Hell. Standing still gets you killed, so you keep pushing forward with glory kills and the chainsaw.

Players love

  • hard-hitting metal soundtrack

How it feels

  • brutal
  • fast
  • adrenaline-fueled

Watch out for

  • arena-to-arena structure gets repetitive
  • easy to get lost finding the way

Best for: anyone craving pure, adrenaline-fueled shooter action

Not for: players who want slow, tactical or story-heavy shooters
