What players say

A Telltale choice-driven adventure where you play Bigby Wolf, sheriff of a hidden community of fairy-tale characters, investigating a brutal murder. A moody noir mystery told mostly through dialogue choices and quick-time events.

Players love

  • moody noir take on fairy tales
  • Bigby and a memorable cast
  • bold comic-book art and lighting
  • mystery that keeps you guessing
  • choices shape how characters see you

What stands out

  • Multiple endings & choices

How it feels

  • noir
  • moody
  • gritty
  • suspenseful

Watch out for

  • choices rarely change the main story
  • light gameplay, mostly watching
  • Owners asked to pay again for remaster

Best for: Fans of Telltale storytelling and hard-boiled detective stories

Not for: Players who want real gameplay challenge or wildly branching endings
