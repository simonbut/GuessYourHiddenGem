What players say

An open-world shooter set in a colorful, overgrown Hope County 17 years after Far Cry 5's nuclear ending, fighting the Highwaymen with crafted weapons. A short spin-off that many players say feels like a big expansion.

Players love

  • vibrant, overgrown post-apocalyptic world
  • solid gunplay
  • crafting and upgrading weapons and base
  • Closure for Far Cry 5's story

How it feels

  • colorful
  • action-packed
  • post-apocalyptic

Watch out for

  • forgettable twin villains
  • feels like a full-priced DLC
  • bullet-sponge enemy tiers
  • weak, rushed story

Best for: Far Cry 5 fans wanting closure, ideally bought on sale

Not for: Players who want a strong villain or dislike RPG-style enemy levels
