What players say

A free-to-play military shooter in the old-school Counter-Strike/CoD mold with no abilities or heroes, just guns, maps and modes. It runs on almost any PC, but most players say its best days are behind it.

  • launch issues like terms screen bug

Best for: players with weak PCs who miss simple, classic free shooters

Not for: anyone who wants full lobbies and fair, cheater-free matches
