What players say

A roguelike kingdom builder where you draft one card a turn onto a grid, then watch your tiny pixel army auto-battle rival kings. Runs snowball into absurd chain-reaction combos.

Players love

  • chaining combos into broken builds
  • each king plays very differently
  • charming pixel-art armies
  • steady perk and king unlocks
  • simple rules with real depth

What stands out

  • Addictive, one-more-run

How it feels

  • strategic
  • addictive
  • satisfying

Watch out for

  • shaky balance with a few dominant cards
  • runs hinge on drawing the right cards
  • quest mode feels brutally hard
  • multiplayer and ranked feel tacked on

Best for: roguelike deckbuilder fans who love breaking a game with synergies

Not for: players who want hands-on combat or tight balance
