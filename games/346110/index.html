What players say

An open-world survival game where you start with nothing on a dinosaur island, then tame creatures and build huge bases. Players love the dinos, but there are a lot of complaints about performance and stability.

Watch out for

  • recent update broke mods and servers
  • losing hours of progress to wild dinos

Best for: dinosaur lovers with patience and friends to tame and build with

Not for: players with weaker PCs or little tolerance for bugs
