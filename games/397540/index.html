What players say

A looter shooter where you pick one of four Vault Hunters and blast across planets for piles of randomized guns. Players praise the shooting and loot as the series' best but widely dislike the story.

Players love

  • series-best gunplay and movement
  • rewarding loot farming with frequent legendaries
  • strong DLC campaigns
  • great soundtrack

What stands out

  • Genuinely funny
  • Great with friends

How it feels

  • chaotic
  • loud
  • goofy
  • action-packed

Watch out for

  • weak story and grating dialogue
  • clunky inventory and menu UI
  • poor PC optimization, stutter and crashes

Best for: loot grinders who care about shooting more than story

Not for: Borderlands 2 fans who want great writing and villains
