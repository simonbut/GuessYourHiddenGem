What players say

A single-player survivors-style auto-shooter set in the Deep Rock Galactic universe: you walk a lone dwarf through caves, mine minerals and shred alien swarms while picking upgrades. Many play it as a relaxed background game, but it is harder and grindier than most of the genre.

Players love

  • Faithful DRG dwarves, bugs and mining
  • Mining through rock adds a fresh twist to the genre
  • relaxing to play alongside a podcast or video
  • satisfying long-term unlocks and upgrades
  • lots of classes, weapons and biomes

What stands out

  • Addictive, one-more-run

How it feels

  • chill
  • grindy
  • chaotic

Watch out for

  • tiny 1-2% meta upgrades make progress a slow grind
  • runs rarely make you feel powerful
  • little weapon and build variety between runs
  • Single-player only, no co-op

Best for: DRG fans and bullet-heaven players who enjoy a long, steady grind

Not for: players who want to feel overpowered quickly or want co-op
