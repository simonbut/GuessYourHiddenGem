What players say

A co-op-only cinematic prison break story where two convicts escape and go on the run, played in split screen. Short and linear, it feels like starring in a crime movie with a friend, with an ending players keep talking about.

  • short, little replay value

Best for: two players wanting a story-heavy evening co-op adventure

Not for: players looking for challenging gameplay or solo play
