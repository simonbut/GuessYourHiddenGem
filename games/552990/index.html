What players say

A free-to-play online naval shooter where you command early 20th-century destroyers, cruisers, battleships and carriers in team battles. It is slower and more tactical than it looks, with angling, armor and spotting mattering a lot.

Players love

  • huge variety of historical ships
  • satisfying salvos and naval combat
  • enjoyable PvE and co-op modes
  • runs on modest hardware
  • playable without paying

What stands out

  • Competitive / ranked play

How it feels

  • tactical
  • tense
  • grindy

Watch out for

  • aggressive, pricey monetization
  • long grind with many currencies
  • carriers and submarines unbalance battles
  • punishing for beginners, toxic players

Best for: naval history buffs and patient players who enjoy tactical team battles

Not for: newcomers who hate grinding or heavy upsell pressure
