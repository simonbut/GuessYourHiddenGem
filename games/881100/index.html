What players say

A brutally hard roguelite where every pixel is physically simulated: fire spreads, liquids flow and everything can blow up. You build absurd spells in wands and keep discovering new things over hundreds of hours.

Players love

  • every pixel simulated, wild interactions
  • deep, programmable wand crafting
  • still new things to find after hundreds of hours
  • hilarious self-inflicted deaths

What stands out

How it feels

  • chaotic
  • punishing

  • funny

Watch out for

  • punishingly hard, frequent instant deaths
  • no meta progression between runs
  • obscure mechanics you need guides for

Best for: experimenters who love hard roguelikes and figuring things out by dying a lot

Not for: players who want steady progress, gentler difficulty or clear explanations
