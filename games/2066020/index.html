What players say

A survivors-style action roguelite where you fight huge hordes with stacking spells, then grow talent trees and unlock heroes between runs. Runs escalate into a screen-filling light show of combos.

Players love

  • huge build variety and talent trees
  • lots of characters to unlock
  • constant unlocks and rewarding progression
  • banging soundtrack
  • screen-filling spell effects

What stands out

  • Addictive, one-more-run

How it feels

  • flashy
  • chaotic
  • addictive

Watch out for

  • effects so dense you can't see
  • heroes and skills feel samey
  • combat boils down to dodging red circles
  • repetitive maps on the achievement grind

Best for: survivors-like fans who love theorycrafting builds and long unlock grinds

Not for: players wanting real challenge, punchy combat or a readable screen
