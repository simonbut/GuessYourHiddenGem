What players say

A side-scrolling bullet-time shooter where a talking banana sends you on violent missions. Flips, dodges and slow motion make each gunfight feel like a goofy action-movie stunt.

Players love

  • stylish slow-motion acrobatic gunfights
  • soundtrack that drives the action
  • absurd humor and a scheming banana
  • Max Payne and John Wick action-movie feel

How it feels

  • stylish
  • frantic
  • funny

Watch out for

  • gets repetitive after a few hours
  • frustrating late levels and final bosses
  • strict, confusing scoring for S ranks
  • floaty movement and awkward aiming

Best for: bullet-time action fans who enjoy chaining stylish combos

Not for: players wanting a long campaign or tight, precise controls
