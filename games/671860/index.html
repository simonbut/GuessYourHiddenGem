What players say

A low-poly, Battlefield-style shooter with 254-player servers, vehicles and mostly destructible maps. Matches are loud, chaotic and full of proximity voice chat, but the player count has shrunk a lot and updates stalled for years.

Players love

  • massive, chaotic large-scale battles
  • destructible buildings and C4 antics
  • runs on low-end PCs
  • proximity voice chat banter
  • feels like older Battlefield games

How it feels

  • chaotic
  • goofy
  • nostalgic

Watch out for

  • very few active servers left
  • years without meaningful updates
  • remaining lobbies full of sweaty veterans

Best for: players who want cheap, chaotic Battlefield-style mayhem and don't mind a small community

Not for: anyone who needs full servers in every region or a game that's still actively updated
