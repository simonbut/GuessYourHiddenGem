What players say

A remake of the classic RPG set in a walled-off mining prison colony, where you start as a nobody and work your way up among rival camps. It feels harsh and old-school, with no quest markers and a slow climb from weakling to hero.

Players love

  • strong sound and music

How it feels

Watch out for

  • performance drops in busy areas
  • tedious lockpicking minigame
  • clunky combat controls

Best for: fans of the original and players who love harsh, old-school RPGs

Not for: those who want polished combat, modern conveniences or a bug-free experience
