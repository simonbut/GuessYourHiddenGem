What players say

A third-person sci-fi survival horror game about escaping a prison on Jupiter's dead moon Callisto. Gorgeous, grim and gory, built more around close-up melee and dodging than shooting.

Players love

  • jaw-dropping graphics
  • thick, creepy atmosphere
  • excellent sound design
  • brutal melee and dismemberment
  • solid story, boosted by DLC

What stands out

  • Genuinely scary

How it feels

  • tense
  • gruesome
  • atmospheric

Watch out for

  • dodge-and-hit combat gets repetitive
  • few enemy types, recycled bosses

Best for: horror fans after a gorgeous, gory melee-focused sci-fi survival game

Not for: players expecting a Dead Space-style shooter with varied combat
