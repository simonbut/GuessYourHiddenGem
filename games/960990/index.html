What players say

A cinematic, choice-driven drama following Jodie across years of her life, linked to an invisible spirit, Aiden, that you can control. It plays like an interactive movie built on quick-time events and short exploration scenes.

Players love

  • emotional, character-focused story
  • strong performances from the lead cast
  • controlling the spirit Aiden
  • Jodie's well-developed character
  • striking cinematic presentation

How it feels

  • emotional
  • cinematic
  • melancholic
  • tense

Watch out for

  • clunky controls and unclear QTE prompts
  • choices often feel like they change little
  • feels more like a movie than a game
  • jumpy, non-linear story order

Best for: fans of Heavy Rain and Detroit who care most about story and acting

Not for: players who want real gameplay or tight controls
