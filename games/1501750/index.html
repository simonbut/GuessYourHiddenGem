What players say

A dark fantasy Souls-like where a lantern lets you cross into Umbral, a nightmare version of the world, to find paths and fight. After years of patches many players call it a strong Souls-like, though performance complaints remain.

Players love

  • gorgeous dark fantasy atmosphere
  • interconnected level design and shortcuts
  • Umbral dual-world mechanic
  • good weapon and build variety
  • much improved after patches

What stands out

  • Great with friends

How it feels

  • dark
  • atmospheric
  • challenging

Watch out for

  • stuttering and frame drops
  • laggy, unreliable co-op
  • repetitive enemies and environments
  • inconsistent hitboxes and combat feedback

Best for: Souls fans looking for another atmospheric world to explore

Not for: players sensitive to performance issues or who mainly want smooth co-op
