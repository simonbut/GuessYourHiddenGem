What players say

An open-world action game where you play an undercover cop infiltrating gangs in Hong Kong, with all DLC included. Players often call it an underrated GTA-like with its own identity, built around satisfying martial-arts combat and a gritty crime story.

Watch out for

Best for: fans of open-world crime games who love hand-to-hand fighting

Not for: players wanting a modern, deep open world or shooting-focused action
