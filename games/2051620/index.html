What players say

A free-to-play WWII shooter where you command a small AI squad in large battles with infantry, tanks and planes. The pace is slower and more tactical than Battlefield or Call of Duty.

Players love

How it feels

Watch out for

  • pay-to-win premium squads
  • long weapon research grind
  • lopsided matches and unfair matchmaking

Best for: WWII shooter fans who want big free battles and do not mind a grind

Not for: players who dislike monetized progression
