What players say

A fantasy strategy game mixing turn-based empire building with large real-time battles, with very different Warhammer races like Skaven, Lizardmen and Elves. Many fans consider it the high point of the trilogy.

Players love

  • very distinct, asymmetric factions
  • spectacular battles with monsters and magic
  • huge campaign replayability
  • often preferred over Warhammer III and runs better on weaker PCs
  • great for Warhammer Fantasy fans

How it feels

  • epic
  • strategic
  • bombastic

Watch out for

  • many races and lords locked behind costly DLC
  • Mortal Empires needs the first game
  • odd difficulty balance and unit pathing
  • distrust of Creative Assembly's business practices

Best for: strategy fans who want deep fantasy campaigns and big monster battles

Not for: players unwilling to buy DLC to get the full roster
