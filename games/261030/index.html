What players say

An episodic, choice-driven adventure continuing Clementine's story as she survives the zombie apocalypse with new groups of survivors. It plays like an interactive drama, tense and often heartbreaking.

  • some characters rushed or underdeveloped

Best for: players who finished Season One and want more of Clementine's story

Not for: those who want choices that reshape the plot or more hands-on gameplay
