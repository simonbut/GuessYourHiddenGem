What players say

A realistic first-person medieval RPG where you start as an illiterate blacksmith's son who can barely swing a sword and slowly become someone. Players find its story and historical world absorbing, though rough around the edges.

Players love

  • engaging story and voice acting
  • satisfying zero-to-hero progression
  • realistic, historically grounded world
  • beautiful, detailed environments
  • funny, memorable moments

How it feels

  • immersive
  • grounded
  • challenging
  • funny

Watch out for

  • clunky combat, especially when outnumbered
  • bugs, crashes and performance issues
  • steep, punishing early hours
  • awkward movement and controls

Best for: players who want a slow, realistic medieval RPG and don't mind jank

Not for: players who want smooth, forgiving action combat
