What players say

An open-world post-apocalyptic RPG set in a ruined, retro-futuristic Boston, full of quests, shooting, looting and settlement building. Easy to sink hundreds of hours into, especially with mods.

Best for: players who love roaming a big wasteland and tinkering with mods

Not for: players who need a gripping main story or can't tolerate technical issues
