What players say

A huge first-person medieval RPG set in 15th-century Bohemia that picks up Henry's story right where the first game ended. Players describe it as slow, grounded and very immersive, a world to live in more than rush through.

Players love

  • deeply immersive, detailed medieval world
  • engaging story and memorable characters
  • beautiful graphics and landscapes
  • lots to explore and get sidetracked by
  • plenty of humor and funny moments

How it feels

  • immersive
  • grounded
  • epic
  • funny

Watch out for

  • clunky combat, especially against groups
  • slow, frustrating opening hours
  • bugs and jank

Best for: patient players who want a realistic, story-heavy medieval RPG to sink many hours into

Not for: anyone who wants fast action or quick progress
