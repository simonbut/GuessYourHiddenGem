What players say

A 1940s Los Angeles detective game where you work cases as cop Cole Phelps, combing crime scenes for clues and interrogating suspects by reading their faces. Slow, methodical and soaked in noir atmosphere.

Players love

  • Investigating scenes and grilling suspects
  • impressive facial-capture animation
  • rich 1940s Los Angeles atmosphere
  • engaging crime story

How it feels

  • moody
  • methodical
  • cinematic

Watch out for

  • Forced Rockstar launcher and account
  • 30 FPS cap and dated PC port
  • Interrogation lie-reading feels arbitrary
  • cases turn formulaic later on

Best for: Detective fans who enjoy slow, methodical crime-solving

Not for: Players wanting fast action or who refuse mandatory launchers
