What players say

An anime-style souls-like where you play a vampire-like Revenant with swappable class 'codes' and an AI partner. Players call it a lower-pressure take on the genre, carried by its character creator and build freedom.

Players love

  • deep, hours-long character creator
  • flexible build and Blood Code swapping
  • Helpful AI partner that revives you
  • easier entry point to souls-likes
  • likable cast and anime style

How it feels

  • stylish
  • dark
  • challenging

Watch out for

  • maze-like levels, especially Cathedral of Sacred Blood
  • floaty combat with weak hit feedback
  • awkward controls, worst on keyboard and mouse
  • launch errors and crashes on some PCs, including Linux

Best for: anime fans and souls-like newcomers who love customizing characters and builds

Not for: veterans seeking tight, weighty combat or clear level design
