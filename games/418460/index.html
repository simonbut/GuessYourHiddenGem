What players say

A 64-player Vietnam War shooter from the Red Orchestra makers, with US, Australian, NVA and Viet Cong factions and jungle-heavy maps. Firefights are sudden, tense and brutal, and you die fast.

Players love

  • authentic Vietnam atmosphere and maps
  • balance between arcade shooter and milsim
  • lethal, satisfying gunplay
  • often called better than Hell Let Loose: Vietnam

How it feels

  • tense
  • brutal
  • chaotic
  • gritty

Watch out for

  • only a few populated servers left
  • toxic, sometimes racist community
  • veterans and snipers stomp newcomers
  • little teamwork or voice comms in public games

Best for: players who want a gritty, lethal Vietnam shooter that's easier to pick up than Arma

Not for: players in regions with few servers, or anyone put off by a hostile community
