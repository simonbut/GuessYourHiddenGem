What players say

A cheap tactical close-quarters shooter set in Bangladesh, often compared to Ready or Not, with team PvP and a campaign you can play solo with AI squadmates. Players like it for the price, but the online community has largely dried up and the rough edges show.

  • little improvement since early access

Best for: budget tactical shooter fans happy to play solo against bots

Not for: anyone wanting active PvP matches or reliable co-op
