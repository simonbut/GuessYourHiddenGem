What players say

A first-person survival game where you wash up in the Pacific after a plane crash. You gather, craft, build rafts and hop between islands. Players find it challenging and atmospheric but rough around the edges.

  • repetitive, samey islands

Best for: survival fans who want a castaway island experience and can tolerate jank

Not for: groups who want smooth co-op, or players who hate bugs
