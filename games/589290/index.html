What players say

A huge online musket-and-bayonet shooter set in the Napoleonic era and WW1. Battles are slow, loud and chaotic, and the strangers yelling and roleplaying over proximity chat are a big part of the show.

Players love

  • huge 100+ player battles
  • slow, laid-back musket combat

  • arcadey and easy to pick up

  • lag and unreliable hit registration
  • toxic players in voice chat
  • cheaters and admin abuse

Best for: Napoleonic history buffs who love big, chaotic battles with lots of voice chat

Not for: players who want precise gunplay or a friendly, clean chat
