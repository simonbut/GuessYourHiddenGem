What players say

A physics sandbox where you bolt together medieval machines (carts, catapults, even flying contraptions) to smash armies and castles. Expect a lot of trial and error and plenty of builds falling apart before one works.

Players love

  • huge creative building freedom
  • satisfying when a build finally works
  • hilarious physics destruction
  • workshop and mods add endless content
  • rewards engineering problem-solving

How it feels

  • creative
  • chaotic
  • funny

Watch out for

  • no real tutorial, steep learning curve
  • awkward controls and camera
  • crashes and bugs, especially with the DLC
  • building tools lack some features

Best for: tinkerers who love engineering and learning through failure

Not for: players who want a guided tutorial or get frustrated by trial and error
