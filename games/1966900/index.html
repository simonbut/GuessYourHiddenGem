What players say

A top-down survival roguelite where you shoot through 20-minute nights of Lovecraftian hordes, stacking upgrades into overpowered builds. Simple, cheap and addictive, but the PC version no longer gets updates.

Players love

  • wild build synergies and broken combos
  • dark pixel-art aesthetic
  • great value for around $5
  • characters with distinct abilities
  • good for short, casual sessions

How it feels

  • dark
  • frantic
  • addictive

Watch out for

  • PC version abandoned for mobile
  • runs grow repetitive quickly
  • fairly small amount of content

Best for: players who want a cheap, bite-sized survivor-like with lots of build crafting

Not for: players who want a game that is still updated or offers lots of long-term content
