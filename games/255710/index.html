What players say

A city builder focused on zoning, services and especially traffic. You design anything from grid towns to realistic metropolises, then spend hours untangling jams, with a huge Workshop of mods and assets.

Players love

  • endless traffic and road puzzles
  • huge creative freedom
  • massive Workshop mods and assets
  • satisfying problem solving
  • hours disappear

How it feels

  • relaxing
  • creative
  • absorbing

Watch out for

  • huge number of costly DLCs
  • Paradox launcher hassles
  • lag and RAM strain in big or modded cities
  • poor pathfinding causes jams

Best for: players who love designing cities and solving traffic

Not for: players wanting deep economic management or who resent DLC
