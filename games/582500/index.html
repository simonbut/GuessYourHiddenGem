What players say

A free, short co-op escape-room puzzler in a frozen castle: two split-up players solve puzzles by describing clues over walkie-talkies. Takes an hour or two of talking, shouting and laughing it out together.

Players love

  • completely free to play
  • puzzles built on talking each other through
  • laughs and bickering with your partner
  • creative escape-room puzzles
  • great taster for the series

What stands out

  • Great with friends

How it feels

  • eerie
  • funny
  • cozy

Watch out for

  • clunky in-game walkie-talkie voice chat
  • connection and invite troubles
  • no checkpoints, progress easily lost
  • very short with no replay value

Best for: pairs of friends or partners wanting a free one-evening co-op puzzle

Not for: solo players or anyone without a patient partner to talk with
