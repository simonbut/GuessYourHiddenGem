What players say

The Call of Duty hub for Modern Warfare 4, Black Ops 7 and Warzone. Players describe classic fast COD action, with a strong MW4 campaign, the returning DMZ extraction mode and multiplayer that can be a blast or a grind.

Players love

Watch out for

  • PC players can't turn off crossplay against strong controller aim assist
  • strict skill-based matchmaking makes casual play exhausting
  • cheaters and rough spawns in multiplayer
  • campaign failing to launch or crashing for some players

Best for: players who want a solid COD campaign plus familiar fast-paced multiplayer and DMZ

Not for: mouse-and-keyboard players who hate facing aim assist, casual players put off by SBMM, or anyone tired of the same yearly COD formula
