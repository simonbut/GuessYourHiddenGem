About this site and its data

Steam data snapshot: 15 November 2026.

What's here

  • Game facts from Steam's public store: names, release dates, developers and publishers, platforms, tags, and review scores, as they were on the date above.
  • Short excerpts of store descriptions, each linked to its Steam page. They belong to the game's developer or publisher.
  • Cover images, loaded straight from Steam's servers. They are not copied here.
  • "What players say": summaries written by an AI in its own words after reading many public Steam reviews of a game, plus statistics computed from reviews (how often players mention boss fights, bugs and so on).

The site contains no review text, no Steam IDs and no player names.

Provided as is

All Steam data on this site is provided "as is", without any warranty. It may be incomplete, out of date or wrong. Check the game's Steam page before you buy anything.

Your privacy

  • No accounts, cookies, analytics or tracking.
  • Your quiz answers, favourites and filters are saved only in your own browser, so you can come back to them. "Start over" clears your quiz; clearing this site's data in your browser removes everything. The recommender runs in your browser too: nothing you answer is sent to us.
  • Cover images load from Steam's servers and the in-browser engine from the jsDelivr CDN, so those servers see a request from your browser, as with any image or script on the web.
  • "Ask Google AI Mode" sends your wish to Google, only when you click it.
  • The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which keeps its own logs (GitHub's privacy statement).

Are you a developer, a publisher or a reviewer and want something here removed or corrected? Open an issue and it will be taken down in the next update.
